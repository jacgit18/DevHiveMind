Got it! Here are **top 3 exercises** for each category based on your goals of functional strength, martial arts support, and muscle growth:

---

### **Isometric Calisthenics (No Weights)**

1. **Long Lever Plank** – Builds core endurance and shoulder stability essential for grappling and striking.
2. **Side Plank** – Enhances lateral core stability, crucial for rotational movements in striking and escapes in BJJ.
3. **Tuck Jumps to Plank** – Combines explosive power with core activation for improved transitions and agility.

---

### **Rope Training**

1. **Battle Rope Slams** – Builds explosive power and conditions shoulders, core, and grip. Perfect for martial arts endurance.
2. **Alternating Waves** – Enhances upper body endurance and coordination while simulating punching rhythms.
3. **Side-to-Side Waves** – Improves rotational power and agility, key for transitioning in BJJ or striking combos.

---

### **Medicine Ball**

1. **Rotational Slam** – Develops rotational core power for striking and grappling movements.
2. **Explosive Overhead Throw** – Builds total-body explosive power, mimicking takedown strength.
3. **Russian Twists** – Strengthens rotational core muscles, critical for maintaining control in grappling and improving flexibility.

---

### **Full Body**

1. **Turkish Get-Up** – Excellent for building strength, stability, and flexibility in transitions and control.
2. **Clean & Press** – Enhances explosive power and functional strength across the entire body.
3. **Farmer’s Walk** – Builds grip strength, shoulder stability, and core endurance for holding positions or controlling opponents.

---

### **Free Weight**

1. **Kettlebell Swing** – Develops explosive hip drive for takedowns, kicks, and overall posterior chain strength.
2. **Renegade Row (Dumbbell or Kettlebell)** – Combines core stability with upper body pulling strength, great for grappling.
3. **Bulgarian Split Squat** – Strengthens single-leg stability and balance, improving kicks and base control.

---

### **Upper Body**

1. **Pull-Ups** – Essential for upper body pulling strength, mimicking grips and chokes in BJJ.
2. **Single-Arm Press (Kettlebell)** – Builds unilateral shoulder strength and control for striking and guard work.
3. **Bench Press (Barbell)** – Develops chest and arm strength for pushes and transitions.

---

### **Lower Body**

1. **Romanian Deadlift (Barbell)** – Targets hamstrings and glutes for hip extension, critical for bridging and escapes.
2. **Cossack Squat** – Enhances lateral flexibility and mobility in hips, ideal for guard retention and kicking.
3. **Kettlebell Step-Up** – Builds single-leg strength, balance, and explosiveness for takedowns and kicks.

---

### **Plyometric Calisthenics (Jumps)**

1. **Box Jumps** – Improves explosive lower-body power and agility for quick takedowns and evasions.
2. **Skater Jumps** – Builds lateral power and balance for agility in grappling and striking footwork.
3. **Split Squat Jumps** – Strengthens explosive leg drive for kicks and transitioning to takedowns.
