# **Plyometric Exercises for Functional Strength and Martial Arts**

### **Key Principles**

- Focus on explosive, dynamic movements to build power and speed.
- Incorporate exercises that mimic the motions used in martial arts like BJJ, Muay Thai, kickboxing, and daily functional movements.

## **Upper Body**

1. **Clap Push-Ups**
    - Improves explosive pushing power for strikes and framing in grappling.
2. **Plyometric Chest Pass (Medicine Ball)**
    - Simulates punching explosiveness for strikes.
3. **Explosive Overhead Throw (Medicine Ball)**
    - Builds shoulder and tricep power for punches.
4. **Kneeling Power Push-Up**
    - Develops explosive upper-body strength for takedown drives.
5. **Plyometric Landmine Press**
    - Mimics explosive pushing motions in grappling or striking.

---

## **Lower Body**

1. **Depth Jumps**
    - Builds explosive leg power for takedowns and evasion.
2. **Box Jumps**
    - Improves vertical explosiveness for kicks and guard passing.
3. **Broad Jumps**
    - Develops horizontal power for tackling or shooting in.
4. **Split Squat Jumps**
    - Enhances single-leg power for kicks and takedowns.
5. **Lateral Bounds**
    - Builds lateral agility and power for evasion and sweeps.
6. **Plyometric Step-Ups**
    - Simulates explosive stepping movements for takedowns.
7. **Skater Jumps**
    - Strengthens lateral explosiveness for dynamic movement in striking.
8. **Squat to Jump Kick**
    - Combines explosive leg power with striking mechanics.

---

## **Full Body**

1. **Burpees with Tuck Jump**
    - Develops full-body explosiveness and conditioning.
2. **Medicine Ball Overhead Slam to Squat Jump**
    - Combines explosive upper and lower-body strength.
3. **Jumping Lunges with Twist (Medicine Ball)**
    - Builds rotational power and dynamic leg strength.
4. **Bounding (Explosive Running Leaps)**
    - Improves speed and power for explosive movements.
5. **Rotational Box Jumps**
    - Enhances rotational power and dynamic balance for kicks and sweeps.

# **Calisthenics Exercises for Functional Strength and Martial Arts**

### **Key Benefits**

- Improves bodyweight control, strength, and endurance.
- Builds functional movement patterns for martial arts like BJJ, Muay Thai, kickboxing, and daily life.
- No equipment required, making it accessible and versatile.

---

## **Back**

1. **Pull-Ups**
    
    - Variations: Wide grip, neutral grip, chin-up grip.
    - Builds pulling strength for grappling and climbing.
2. **Australian Rows (Bodyweight Rows)**
    
    - Develops mid-back strength for grappling control.
3. **Arch Hold (Superman Hold)**
    
    - Strengthens the lower back and improves posture for groundwork.
4. **Climbing Holds (on Pull-Up Bar)**
    
    - Enhances grip and pulling strength for takedowns.

---

## **Core**

1. **Plank Variations**
    
    - Forearm, side plank, and dynamic planks.
    - Builds core stability for balance and transitions.
2. **Hollow Body Hold**
    
    - Develops core tension and endurance for guard retention.
3. **Leg Raises**
    
    - Variations: Hanging, lying, or with a twist.
    - Strengthens lower abs for kicks and core control.
4. **Mountain Climbers**
    
    - Improves core strength and agility for scrambles.
5. **Bicycle Crunches**
    
    - Builds rotational strength for striking and sweeps.
6. **Flutter Kicks**
    
    - Targets lower abs and hip flexors for mobility.
7. **Dragon Flag**
    
    - Advanced core exercise for explosive strength and control.

---

## **Upper Body**

1. **Push-Ups**
    
    - Variations: Standard, diamond, wide-arm, or archer push-ups.
    - Builds chest, shoulder, and tricep strength for striking.
2. **Dips (on Parallel Bars or Chairs)**
    
    - Targets chest, triceps, and shoulders for pushing strength.
3. **Pike Push-Ups**
    
    - Strengthens shoulders and triceps for striking and grappling.
4. **Handstand Push-Ups**
    
    - Advanced exercise for shoulder and tricep power.
5. **Wall Walks**
    
    - Improves shoulder stability and core strength.
6. **Planche Progressions**
    
    - Builds upper-body strength and full-body tension.

---

## **Lower Body**

1. **Squats**
    
    - Variations: Bodyweight, narrow stance, or wide stance.
    - Builds leg strength and endurance for groundwork and footwork.
2. **Lunges**
    
    - Variations: Forward, reverse, or lateral lunges.
    - Develops single-leg strength and balance for kicks.
3. **Step-Ups (on Stairs or Bench)**
    
    - Enhances explosive leg power for takedowns.
4. **Cossack Squats**
    
    - Improves hip mobility and lateral strength for sweeps.
5. **Single-Leg Deadlift (Bodyweight)**
    
    - Builds balance and hamstring strength for groundwork.
6. **Jump Squats**
    
    - Develops explosive leg power for takedowns and kicks.
7. **Skater Lunges**
    
    - Strengthens lateral explosiveness for evasive movements.

---

## **Full Body**

1. **Burpees**
    
    - Combines strength and cardio for conditioning.
2. **Animal Crawls**
    
    - Variations: Bear, crab, or lizard crawls.
    - Improves mobility and full-body coordination.
3. **Climbing Movements (Spider Walks)**
    
    - Builds strength, flexibility, and dynamic control for groundwork.
4. **Bodyweight Clean and Press (Explosive Stand-Up)**
    
    - Simulates getting up explosively from the ground.
5. **Plyometric Push-Ups to Squat Jump**
    
    - Combines upper and lower body explosiveness.
