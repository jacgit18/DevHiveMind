### **1. Butterfly Stretch → Dynamic Butterfly Hip Rocks**

- **Muscles Targeted:** Inner thighs (adductors), hips.
- **Why:** Improves hip flexibility for guard work in BJJ, kicking range in Muay Thai, and overall hip mobility for squatting or sitting cross-legged in daily life.
- **Dynamic Steps:** Sit in a butterfly position and gently bounce or rock your knees up and down. Alternatively, move your knees toward the floor in small pulses.

---

### **2. Seated Forward Fold → Dynamic Hamstring Scoops**

- **Muscles Targeted:** Hamstrings, calves, lower back.
- **Why:** Aids flexibility for triangle chokes in BJJ, head kicks in Muay Thai, and better posture during everyday tasks.
- **Dynamic Steps:** Stand with one foot extended forward, heel on the ground, and scoop your arms toward the ground while hinging at the hips. Alternate legs.

---

### **3. Lizard Pose → Dynamic Hip Flexor Swings**

- **Muscles Targeted:** Hip flexors, hamstrings, groin.
- **Why:** Opens up hips for guard retention and sweeps in BJJ, supports dynamic kicking in Muay Thai, and counters tightness from sitting or walking.
- **Dynamic Steps:** From a low lunge (lizard pose), rock your hips forward and backward or drop your elbows to the ground and return to your hands.

---

### **4. Spinal Twist → Supine Windshield Wipers**

- **Muscles Targeted:** Spine, obliques, lower back.
- **Why:** Improves rotational flexibility for shrimping in BJJ, torque during Muay Thai strikes, and relieving back tension from daily activities.
- **Dynamic Steps:** Lie on your back with your knees bent and feet flat. Slowly rotate your knees side to side while keeping your shoulders on the ground.
---

### **5. Pigeon Pose → Dynamic Pigeon Transitions**

- **Muscles Targeted:** Hips, glutes, piriformis.
- **Why:** Enhances hip mobility for guard passing in BJJ, rotational kicking in Muay Thai, and counters tightness from sitting long hours.
- **Dynamic Steps:**  Move between pigeon pose and a high plank, or gently pulse your hips forward and backward in the pigeon position.
---

### **6. Wall or Doorway Chest Stretch → Dynamic Arm Circles or Shoulder Openers**

- **Muscles Targeted:** Chest, shoulders.
- **Why:** Opens the chest for postural improvement, key for clinch work in Muay Thai, and everyday activities like lifting or pushing.
- **Dynamic Steps:** Perform large arm circles or dynamic chest expansions by interlocking your fingers behind your back and gently pulsing your arms upward.
---

### **7. Frog Stretch → Dynamic Frog Rockbacks**

- **Muscles Targeted:** Groin, inner thighs.
- **Why:** Improves flexibility for butterfly guard in BJJ, wide stances in Muay Thai, and increases range of motion for everyday lower body movements.
- **Dynamic Steps:** Start in a frog stretch position and slowly rock your hips backward and forward while keeping your knees wide.
---

### **8. Standing Quad Stretch → High Knee Pulls**

- **Muscles Targeted:** Quadriceps, hip flexors.
- **Why:** Stretches the quads for explosive knee strikes in Muay Thai, guard work in BJJ, and walking or running in daily life.
- **Dynamic Steps:**  Pull one knee toward your chest while standing, then alternate legs. You can also add a slight hop for dynamic movement.
---

### **9. Shoulder Stretch → Dynamic Scapular Push-Ups**

- **Muscles Targeted:** Deltoids, traps.
- **Why:** Improves shoulder mobility for underhooks in BJJ, clinch control in Muay Thai, and upper body range for lifting or reaching movements.
- **Dynamic Steps:**  In a push-up position, perform scapular push-ups by squeezing your shoulder blades together and then separating them without bending your elbows.
---

### **10. Cat-Cow Stretch → Dynamic Cat-Cow Transitions**

- **Muscles Targeted:** Spine, core, lower back.
- **Why:** Increases spinal flexibility for bridging in BJJ, rotation during strikes in Muay Thai, and prevents stiffness from sitting or bending.
- **Dynamic Steps:**  Flow between the "cat" and "cow" positions, emphasizing a smooth spinal flexion and extension motion.
---

### 11. Couch Stretch → Dynamic Lunging Hip Flexor Stretch**

- **Muscles Targeted:** Quads, hip flexors.
- **Why:** Essential for improving posture, dynamic kicking in Muay Thai, and countering tightness from daily sitting habits.
- **Dynamic Steps:** Perform a low lunge and gently pulse your hips forward, then alternate legs.
