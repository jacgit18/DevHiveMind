# Boost Your ROUNDHOUSE KICKS


![[Peek 2024-12-13 18-01.gif]]Here’s a list of **battle rope exercises** for a full-body workout, focusing on power, endurance, and coordination:

---

### **Upper Body Dominant**

1. **Double Arm Waves**
    
    - Both arms move up and down simultaneously to create waves.
    - Targets: Shoulders, biceps, triceps, upper back.
2. **Alternating Waves**
    
    - Arms alternate in an up-and-down motion.
    - Targets: Shoulders, arms, core stability.
3. **Side-to-Side Waves**
    
    - Swing ropes outward and inward like a horizontal wave.
    - Targets: Obliques, shoulders, arms.
4. **Seated Waves**
    
    - Perform alternating waves while seated on the ground.
    - Targets: Core, arms, shoulders.

---

### **Lower Body Dominant**

5. **Battle Rope Slams with Squat**
    
    - Slam the ropes down while performing a squat.
    - Targets: Quads, glutes, shoulders, core.
6. **Lunge with Rope Waves**
    
    - Perform alternating waves while holding a lunge position.
    - Targets: Quads, hamstrings, glutes, core.
7. **Jump Squat Slams**
    
    - Combine rope slams with a jump squat.
    - Targets: Explosive power in legs, core, and arms.

---

### **Core Focused**

8. **Rope Circles**
    
    - Move the ropes in small, controlled circular motions.
    - Targets: Core, shoulders, obliques.
9. **Side Plank Waves**
    
    - Hold a side plank while performing small waves with one arm.
    - Targets: Core, shoulders, and stabilizers.
10. **Russian Twist Rope Slams**
    
    - Slam the ropes to each side while twisting your torso.
    - Targets: Obliques, shoulders, arms.

---

### **Full-Body Power**

11. **Rope Slams**
    
    - Slam the ropes as hard as possible onto the ground.
    - Targets: Shoulders, arms, core, legs.
12. **Rope Burpees**
    
    - Combine a burpee with a rope slam after jumping back up.
    - Targets: Full body (cardio and power).
13. **Rope Snakes**
    
    - Move ropes side-to-side along the ground in a snake-like motion.
    - Targets: Core, arms, shoulders.
14. **Battle Rope Pulls**
    
    - Anchor the rope to a sled or weight and pull it toward you.
    - Targets: Back, arms, legs, and grip strength.

---

### **Cardio & Agility**

15. **Lateral Shuffles with Waves**
    
    - Perform alternating waves while shuffling side-to-side.
    - Targets: Legs, core, shoulders, and agility.
16. **Battle Rope Jacks**
    
    - Perform jumping jacks while alternating rope waves.
    - Targets: Full body, cardiovascular endurance.

---

Incorporate **high-intensity intervals** (e.g., 30 seconds work, 15 seconds rest) for maximum results in strength, power, and conditioning.
