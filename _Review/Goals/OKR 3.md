### **AWS Certifications:**

#### **Key Results:**

1. **Complete AWS Training:**
    
    - Enroll in at least **2 AWS certification courses** (e.g., AWS Certified Solutions Architect, AWS Certified Developer) on platforms like **Udemy**, **A Cloud Guru**, or **Coursera**.
    - Dedicate **10 hours per week** to studying AWS services, concepts, and best practices.
2. **Complete Hands-on Labs and Projects:**
    
    - Complete **10 hands-on labs** covering services like EC2, S3, Lambda, RDS, and IAM.
    - Showcase your AWS projects on your **resume** or **LinkedIn** profile.
3. **Engage in AWS Community:**
    
    - Attend at least **2 AWS community events** (webinars, meetups).
    - Actively participate in forums like **Stack Overflow** and **AWS Developer Forums**.
4. **Apply AWS Skills to Real Projects:**
    
    - Implement AWS services in at least one **personal or professional project** for scalability and cost optimization.
    - Document the implementation process for your portfolio.

#### **AWS Certification Goals:**

- **AWS Certified Solutions Architect – Associate**: Focuses on designing distributed systems that are cost-efficient, scalable, and fault-tolerant.
- **AWS Certified Developer – Associate**: Geared towards application development and deployment, covering security best practices.
