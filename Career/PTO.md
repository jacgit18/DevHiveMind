Strategically planning your paid time off (PTO) around public holidays can significantly extend your vacation time. Here's how to maximize your days off in 2025 and 2026:  
  
## **2025:**  
  
1. **New Year's Day (Wednesday, January 1):**  
- **Take Off:** Thursday, January 2, and Friday, January 3  
- **Total Days Off:** 5 days (Wednesday, January 1 – Sunday, January 5)  
  
2. **Martin Luther King Jr. Day (Monday, January 20):**  
- **Take Off:** Friday, January 17  
- **Total Days Off:** 4 days (Friday, January 17 – Monday, January 20)  
  
3. **Presidents' Day (Monday, February 17):**  
- **Take Off:** Friday, February 14  
- **Total Days Off:** 4 days (Friday, February 14 – Monday, February 17)  
  
4. **Memorial Day (Monday, May 26):**  
- **Take Off:** Friday, May 23  
- **Total Days Off:** 4 days (Friday, May 23 – Monday, May 26)  
  
5. **Juneteenth (Thursday, June 19):**  
- **Take Off:** Friday, June 20  
- **Total Days Off:** 4 days (Thursday, June 19 – Sunday, June 22)  
  
6. **Independence Day (Friday, July 4):**  
- **Take Off:** Monday, July 7  
- **Total Days Off:** 4 days (Friday, July 4 – Monday, July 7)  
  
7. **Labor Day (Monday, September 1):**  
- **Take Off:** Friday, August 29  
- **Total Days Off:** 4 days (Friday, August 29 – Monday, September 1)  
  
8. **Indigenous Peoples' Day (Monday, October 13):**  
- **Take Off:** Friday, October 10  
- **Total Days Off:** 4 days (Friday, October 10 – Monday, October 13)  
  
9. **Veterans Day (Tuesday, November 11):**  
- **Take Off:** Monday, November 10  
- **Total Days Off:** 4 days (Saturday, November 8 – Tuesday, November 11)  
  
10. **Thanksgiving (Thursday, November 27):**  
- **Take Off:** Friday, November 28  
- **Total Days Off:** 4 days (Thursday, November 27 – Sunday, November 30)  
  
11. **Christmas (Thursday, December 25):**  
- **Take Off:** Friday, December 26  
- **Total Days Off:** 4 days (Thursday, December 25 – Sunday, December 28)
