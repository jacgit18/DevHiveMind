When making a business decision or entering a partnership, it's important to ask strategic questions to evaluate risks, alignment, and potential outcomes. Here’s a structured list of questions covering various aspects, including other parties involved:


> [!attention] Stay focused on your own rhythm.
> Not everyone is on the same page, and even if they seem aligned, their actions might not be entirely transparent.

^68639f


### **General Business Fit & Goals**

1. What are our key objectives in this partnership/decision?
2. How does this align with our long-term strategy?
3. What does success look like for both parties?
4. Are there any conflicting interests between us and the other party?
5. What unique value does each party bring to the table?

### **Financial & Legal Considerations**

6. What are the financial commitments and revenue-sharing terms?
7. Are there any hidden costs or long-term financial risks?
8. How will profits, losses, and liabilities be distributed?
9. Are there any legal constraints or regulatory requirements?
10. Do we need a formal contract, and what key terms should be included?

### **Operational & Execution Details**

11. Who will be responsible for what tasks, and how will performance be measured?
12. What are the expected timelines and key milestones?
13. How will we handle potential operational challenges or delays?
14. Do we have the necessary infrastructure and resources to execute this properly?
15. How will we integrate workflows between teams?

### **Risk Management & Contingencies**

16. What are the biggest risks associated with this decision?
17. What happens if the partnership fails or underperforms?
18. How will disputes or disagreements be resolved?
19. Are there exit strategies or termination clauses in place?
20. What happens if one party undergoes significant changes (e.g., acquisition, leadership change, bankruptcy)?

### **Third-Party Involvement & Stakeholder Considerations**

21. Are there any external stakeholders (e.g., investors, customers, regulators) who need to be consulted?
22. How does this impact our existing relationships with suppliers, clients, or partners?
23. Are there any potential conflicts of interest with third parties?
24. What level of transparency and communication will we maintain with third parties?
25. How does this decision affect our brand reputation and public perception?

### **Scalability & Future Growth**

26. Can this partnership/decision scale as we grow?
27. What happens if one party wants to expand beyond the initial scope?
28. Are there exclusivity clauses or non-compete agreements?
29. Will this limit our ability to work with other partners in the future?
30. How will we adapt if market conditions or business needs change?
