### **Core Question:**
- **"Can you tell me about your journey and how you got to where you are today? Are you currently working on anything particularly interesting?"**
  - **Supporting Questions:**
    - "What were some of the biggest challenges you faced along the way, and how did you overcome them?"
    - "Were there any pivotal moments or decisions that significantly influenced your path?"
    - "How has your role evolved since you first started?"

### **Process and Habits:**
- **"What personal habits or processes have you developed that you think are crucial to your success?"**
  - **Supporting Questions:**
    - "How do you typically approach your work on a day-to-day basis?"
    - "Do you have any specific routines that help you stay productive or creative?"
    - "Is there a particular strategy or tool you rely on to keep everything organized?"

### **Programs and Organizations:**
- **"Are you involved in any programs or organizations that have been instrumental in your journey? Would you recommend any for someone trying to get into tech?"**
  - **Supporting Questions:**
    - "How did these programs help shape your career?"
    - "What would you say are the key benefits of being part of these organizations?"
    - "Are there any specific events or networking opportunities that you found particularly valuable?"

### **Pivotal Changes:**
- **"Was there a pivotal step or change in your process when trying to get into tech that turned out to be a game-changer for you?"**
  - **Supporting Questions:**
    - "What led you to make that change, and how did it impact your career?"
    - "Would you recommend others follow a similar path?"
    - "Looking back, is there anything you wish you had done differently?"

### **Personal Interests:**
- **"What do you enjoy doing outside of work? Do you have any hobbies or personal interests that help you recharge?"**
  - **Supporting Questions:**
    - "How do these hobbies influence or balance your professional life?"
    - "Are there any skills or lessons from your hobbies that you apply to your work?"

---

### **End of Meeting Wrap-Up:**
At the end of the meeting, say:
- **"Thank you for your time. I'll definitely take all those things into consideration. I’d love to continue meeting with you, depending on your schedule—I’m sure you’re very busy, so I’ll keep my calendar open and flexible for whenever it works for you."**

### **Follow-Up Email:**
After the meeting, send an email:
- **"Thank you again for taking the time to meet with me. The conversation was incredibly informative, and I’m excited to apply some of the insights you shared. I’ll keep you updated on my progress and would love to continue learning from you. Thanks again for your guidance."**
