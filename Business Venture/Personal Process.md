### **Prepare**

1. **Prioritize Tasks**
    - Identify 1–3 high-priority tasks to focus on.
    - Categories things into sub Categories of 4.
2. **Break Down Tasks**  
    For each task, determine:
    - **What**: Clearly define the task.
    - **When**: Set a specific time to work on it.
    - **How**: Outline the steps or process.
    - Optional: Include **Who** (if collaborating or outsourcing) and **Where** (if location matters).

3. **Eliminate Confusion**
    - Clarify uncertainties to avoid procrastination.
    - Simplify the task or break it into smaller, actionable steps.
    - Consider collaboration to fill knowledge or skill gaps.

### **Engage**

1. **Use the FOCUS Checklist**
    
    - **Familiar Ritual**: Do something energizing or grounding, like shadowboxing or a quick warm-up.
    - **Objective**: Set a clear, specific goal for the session (e.g., "Finish draft of section A").
    - **Capture Ideas**: Keep a notebook or app handy to jot down spontaneous ideas.
    - **Urgency**: Define a clear time block for the task (e.g., 30 minutes, 1 hour).
    - **Surroundings**: Eliminate distractions (e.g., turn off notifications, close irrelevant tabs).
2. **Boost Motivation**
    
    - Increase arousal by engaging in an activity that sparks momentum (e.g., asking ChatGPT questions, quick research, or brainstorming).
    - Start with an easy task or small action to create a sense of progress.
3. **Maintain Focus**
    
    - If stuck, return to your ritual or take a short break to reset.
    - Remind yourself of the importance of the task and its end goal.
