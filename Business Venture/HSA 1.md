Health Savings Accounts (HSAs) are designed to cover qualified medical expenses for the account holder, their spouse, and their dependents. You generally cannot use the HSA to cover medical expenses for individuals who are not your dependents or spouse. The IRS provides specific guidelines on eligible individuals and qualified medical expenses.  
  
Here are some key points to consider:  
  
1. **Eligible Individuals:**  
- You (the HSA account holder).  
- Your spouse.  
- Your dependents.  

2. **Qualified Medical Expenses:**  
- Medical expenses must be incurred by you, your spouse, or your dependents to be considered qualified.  
- Qualified medical expenses include a wide range of healthcare costs, such as doctor visits, prescription medications, certain medical procedures, and other eligible expenses.  
 
3. **Non-Qualified Individuals:**  
- Generally, you cannot use your HSA funds to cover medical expenses for individuals who are not your spouse or dependents.  
 
4. **Reimbursement for Eligible Individuals:**  
- You can use HSA funds to pay for eligible medical expenses for yourself, your spouse, or your dependents, even if they are not covered by your high-deductible health plan (HDHP).  
 
It's important to keep detailed records of your medical expenses and ensure that the expenses meet the IRS criteria for qualified medical expenses. Using HSA funds for non-qualified expenses may result in taxes and penalties.  
  
If you have specific questions about your HSA and its usage, it's recommended to consult with a tax professional or financial advisor who can provide guidance based on your individual circumstances and the most up-to-date tax regulations.
