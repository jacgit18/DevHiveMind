When describing how the trademark will be used and providing a specimen, follow these guidelines:

### **Description of Use**
1. **Identify the Goods/Services**: Clearly describe the goods or services with which the trademark will be associated. For example, if you’re offering software development services, specify the type of software and the services (e.g., custom software solutions, consulting, etc.).
2. **Specify the Usage**: Explain how the trademark will be used in connection with these goods or services. For example, "The trademark will be used to brand our software development services, including custom application development and IT consulting."

### **Specimen Examples**
1. **For Services**: Provide examples of how the trademark is used in a service context, such as:
   - A screenshot of your website featuring the trademark prominently, showing it in connection with your services.
   - A brochure or marketing materials displaying the trademark alongside descriptions of the services offered.
   - A business card or letterhead with the trademark used in conjunction with your services.

2. **For Goods**: Provide examples showing the trademark directly on the products or packaging, such as:
   - A product label or packaging that includes the trademark.
   - A screenshot of a software application interface displaying the trademark.

Ensure that your specimen clearly shows the trademark as used in commerce and directly connects it to the goods or services you're offering.


## Software Engineer Context 
For a contracting software engineer, you might describe the trademark use as follows:

### **Description of Use**

“The trademark will be used in connection with providing software engineering services on a contract basis. These services include designing, developing, testing, and maintaining software applications and systems for clients. The services may also encompass technical consulting, system integration, and software architecture design.”

### **Specimen Examples**

1. **Website**: A screenshot of your professional website or portfolio, showing the trademark prominently alongside descriptions of your software engineering services.
2. **Marketing Materials**: A brochure or flyer detailing your contracting services with the trademark included.
3. **Business Card**: A business card displaying the trademark and your role as a contracting software engineer.
4. **Proposal Documents**: A sample of a client proposal or contract with the trademark used in the header or footer, demonstrating its use in a professional context.

These examples should clearly show how the trademark is used in relation to the software engineering services you provide.
