### Monthly

1. **Inspect Lights**
    - Test headlights, brake lights, turn signals, and hazard lights. Replace any burnt-out bulbs.
### **Every 6 Months:**

1. **Change Engine Oil and Filter**
    
    - Replace oil and filter based on the recommended interval.
2. **Inspect Belts and Hoses**
    
    - Look for wear or cracks on the serpentine belt, radiator hoses, and other rubber components.
3. **Battery Maintenance**
    
    - Clean corroded terminals, ensure connections are tight, and test the voltage.
4. **Brake Inspection**
    
    - Check pads, rotors, and fluid levels. Replace components if necessary.

### **Annually:**

1. **Comprehensive Vehicle Inspection**
    
    - Inspect the suspension, steering, exhaust system, and undercarriage for leaks, corrosion, or damage.
2. **Replace Engine Air Filter**
    
    - A clean air filter ensures optimal engine performance and fuel economy.
3. **Flush Coolant System**
    
    - Replace coolant to prevent overheating and corrosion in the cooling system.
4. **Inspect Wiper Blades**
    
    - Replace worn wiper blades for clear visibility in rain or snow.
5. **Check Exhaust System**
    
    - Look for leaks, rust, or damage and address issues promptly.

## **Mileage-Based Maintenance Schedule**


### **Every 3,000 to 5,000 Miles:**

1. **Oil Change**
    
    - Replace engine oil and filter to maintain lubrication and prolong engine life. Follow the manufacturer's recommended interval (time or mileage, whichever comes first).
2. **Check Fluid Levels**
    
    - Inspect and top up **engine oil, coolant, brake fluid, power steering fluid**, and **windshield washer fluid**.
3. **Inspect Tires**
    
    - **Check tire pressure** and adjust to manufacturer-recommended levels.
    - Inspect tread wear for safety.
    - Plan tire rotation every **6,000 to 8,000 miles** to ensure even wear.

### **Every 15,000 to 30,000 Miles:**

1. **Air Filter Replacement**
    
    - Replace the engine air filter to maintain optimal airflow, engine performance, and fuel efficiency.
2. **Brake Inspection**
    
    - Inspect brake pads, rotors, and fluid. Replace worn components to maintain braking performance.
3. **Fluid Flushes**
    
    - Replace fluids such as **coolant**, **transmission fluid**, and **differential fluid** per manufacturer guidelines.
4. **Suspension and Steering Inspection**
    
    - Check suspension and steering components (bushings, ball joints, tie rods) for wear and replace as necessary.
### **Every 60,000 to 100,000 Miles:**

1. **Timing Belt Replacement**
    
    - Replace the timing belt according to the manufacturer's recommendation. A broken belt can cause severe engine damage.
2. **Spark Plug Replacement**
    
    - Replace spark plugs and ignition components to prevent misfires and maintain fuel efficiency.
3. **Cooling System Maintenance**
    
    - Inspect hoses, the radiator, and the water pump. Replace any damaged components and flush the coolant.
4. **Transmission Service**
    
    - Replace transmission fluid and filters to ensure smooth shifting and prolong transmission life.

## **Tire Maintenance Schedule**

### **Monthly:**

1. **Check Tire Pressure**
    
    - Use a tire pressure gauge to measure pressure (when cold) and adjust to the recommended PSI.
2. **Inspect Tread Wear**
    
    - Use a tread depth gauge or the **penny test**: Insert a penny with Lincoln's head upside down into the tread. If the top of his head is visible, the tire needs replacing.
3. **Look for Damage**
    
    - Inspect for cuts, bulges, cracks, or punctures. Address any visible damage promptly.

### **Bi-Annually (Every 6 Months):**

1. **Rotate Tires**
    
    - Rotate tires to ensure even wear. Refer to the vehicle manual for the correct rotation pattern.
2. **Wheel Alignment**
    
    - Check alignment if you notice uneven tread wear, steering pull, or vibrations.

### **Annually:**

1. **Check Tire Age**
    
    - Inspect the sidewalls for the **DOT code** (manufacture date). Replace tires older than **6–10 years** regardless of tread condition.
2. **Inspect Valve Stems**
    
    - Check for cracks or leaks. Replace faulty stems to maintain air pressure.
3. **Tire Balancing**
    
    - Balance tires annually or if you experience vibrations at certain speeds.
4. **Evaluate Seasonal Tires**
    
    - Consider switching to **winter tires** or **summer tires** based on climate for better traction.
5. **Inspect Spare Tire**
    
    - Ensure your spare tire is inflated and in good condition.
