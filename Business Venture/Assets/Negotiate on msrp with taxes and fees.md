Selling Price: The selling price refers to the initial price set by the seller for a product or service. It is the price at which the seller is willing to sell the item or service before any additional charges or fees are added. The selling price is often negotiable, and buyers can try to negotiate a lower price with the seller.  
  
Out the Door Price: The out the door price, also known as the total purchase price or the all-inclusive price, is the final amount that a buyer will pay to purchase the product or service. It includes not only the selling price but also all applicable taxes, fees, and additional charges associated with the purchase. These additional charges may include sales tax, documentation fees, registration fees, title fees, destination charges, and any other applicable fees specific to the transaction.
