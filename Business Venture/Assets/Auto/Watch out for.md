**1. Negotiating Monthly Payments:**

   - **Bank Pre-Approval:** Begin by getting pre-approved for a car loan from a bank, establishing a baseline for negotiations.
  
   - **Dealership Negotiation:** Engage with the dealership separately to negotiate both the car's price and financing terms.
  
   - **Beware of Loan Extensions:** Be cautious when presented with extended loan durations instead of reduced car prices, as this may not be financially favorable.

**2. Trade-In Tactics:**

   - **Independent Negotiations:** Negotiate the selling price of the new car independently of the trade-in to secure better deals.
  
   - **Reveal Trade-In Later:** Declare the absence of a trade-in during initial negotiations and reveal it later to negotiate each aspect separately.

**3. Maximizing Value in the Deal:**

   - **Evaluate Additional Products:** Assess the necessity and cost-effectiveness of supplementary products, services, or warranties offered by the dealership.
  
   - **Awareness of Profit Margins:** Understand that the dealership may find higher profit margins in add-ons rather than the main purchase.

**4. Practical Advice:**

   - **Income-Based Purchase:** Avoid buying a car that exceeds half of your annual income to maintain financial stability.
  
   - **Consider Used Cars:** Opt for used cars with 3-5 years of depreciation for better value.
  
   - **Cash Payments:** Whenever feasible, consider paying in cash to eliminate interest costs and long-term financial commitments.
