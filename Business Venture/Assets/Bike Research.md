### Bike Specs to Aim For
- **Frame Size:** Small to Medium (15 to 17 inches)
- **Top Tube Effective:** 21.65 inches / 550 mm to 22.95 inches / 583 mm
- **Head Tube Angle:** 71°
- **Seat Tube Angle:** 74.5°
- **Chainstay Length:** 17.13 inches / 435 mm to 17.32 inches / 440 mm
- **Wheelbase:** 41.22 inches / 1047 mm to 42.76 inches / 1086 mm
- **Fork Rake:** 2.09 inches / 53 mm
- **Bottom Bracket Drop:** 2.76 inches / 70 mm
- **Headtube Length:** 3.54 inches / 90 mm to 4.72 inches / 120 mm
- **Standover Height:** 27.80 inches / 706 mm to 29.13 inches / 740 mm
- **Stack:** 20.91 inches / 531 mm to 22.01 inches / 559 mm
- **Reach:** 15.87 inches / 403 mm to 16.85 inches / 428 mm

### Best Practice Tips

- **Left Front Gear:** Use for major pedal resistance changes depending on terrain.
- **Right Rear Gear:** Adjust for more resistance to increase speed or more torque (slightly adjusted compared to left gear).
- **Disc Brakes:** Always get a bike with disc brakes.
- **Right Rear Brake:** Use on turns instead of front brake. Use both brakes for general stopping.
- **Bike Lane:** Stick to the left side of the road when there’s a bike lane or none at all.
- **Distance from Cars:** Maintain a distance of 4 ft to 8 ft from cars.
