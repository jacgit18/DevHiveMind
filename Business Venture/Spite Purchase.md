## Spite Purchase:

- **Home Purchase and Rental:**
  - **Scenario:** Buying a property near someone you want to spite.
  - **Financial Strategy:** Live in the house for a few years, then either:
    - **Option 1:** Rent it out to the person.

## Mortgage:

- **Debt and Inflation:**
  - **Strategy:** Leveraging inflation to pay off debts, especially applicable to **mortgages**.
  - **Benefit:** As inflation reduces the real value of money, debt payments become relatively smaller over time.

## Rent:

- **Renewal and Costs:**
  - **Rent Renewal:** Renewing a lease may come with increased rent.
  - **Costs:** Additional costs include security deposits.
  - **Short-Term Rentals:** Renting out the property for short durations can generate tax-free income.

- **Tax Implications:**
  - **Tax Avoidance:** Moving every 90 days or residing on a yacht can potentially be used to avoid certain taxes.
