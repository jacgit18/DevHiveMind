A good indicator to buy is a **bullish RSI divergence**. This occurs when the price is trending downward and reaches a support level, but the RSI starts forming higher lows as the price continues to make lower lows. This divergence suggests that downward momentum is weakening, signaling a potential reversal to the upside.

Another useful indicator is when the **RSI repeatedly tests the 50 line** during trending markets. When the RSI crosses and holds above 50, it signals bullish momentum, and when it holds below 50, it suggests bearish momentum. Watching for these tests can help confirm the direction of the trend and guide your entry or exit points.
