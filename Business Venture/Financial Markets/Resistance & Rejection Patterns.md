## Rejection
If there are more rejections, it could be a positive sign in terms of price resistance. However, if you're already invested, this may signal caution, as the price hasn't moved higher yet and could face further downward pressure.

In this example, the price initially finds support, then reaches a new support level, but is subsequently rejected by that level. This suggests that while there was an attempt to push higher, the price failed to break through the new support, indicating a potential consolidation or reversal.

## Double price resistance
A Tweezer Top is a strong signal to exit a position, as it typically indicates a reversal at a resistance level. If you see this pattern forming, it may be wise to take profits or sell. On the other hand, a Tweezer Bottom is generally a good trigger to buy, as it suggests a reversal at a support level, indicating that the price is likely to rise.
