The more rejections at a particular price level, the better, especially if you're already invested in shares. Multiple rejections at the same level indicate that support is being formed, meaning traders are consistently taking action at this price during different points in time. This could signify strong demand at that price, increasing the likelihood of a reversal or continuation of the trend. For an investor, this level of consistency suggests that the support is solid, and the price may not fall below it easily, potentially providing a good entry or holding opportunity.


## Trend line Support
An effective **entry trigger** is when a **bullish engulfing pattern** forms. This pattern occurs when a small bearish candlestick is completely engulfed by a larger bullish candlestick, signaling a potential reversal from a downtrend to an uptrend.

The bullish engulfing pattern suggests that buyers have taken control, overpowering the previous selling pressure, and may indicate the start of upward momentum. It's a strong entry signal, especially when it occurs at key support levels or after a period of consolidation. Always confirm with additional indicators, such as RSI or trendlines, to increase the reliability of the trade.
