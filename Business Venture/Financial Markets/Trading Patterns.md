### TAE Framework

Read more https://www.tastyfx.com/news/16-candlestick-patterns-every-trader-should-know-180615/#:~:text=A%20candlestick%20is%20a%20way,just%20a%20few%20price%20bars

#### Green Candle vs. Red Candle:

- **Green Candle**: Opens at the bottom and closes at the top, indicating strong buying pressure.
- **Red Candle**: Opens at the top and closes at the bottom, indicating strong selling pressure.

#### Trend:

- **Above 200MA**: When the price is above the 200-day moving average (200MA), it's a signal to start looking for buying opportunities, but this is not a requirement. The range can adjust as the market moves, and the moving average (MA) can also shift.
- **EMA Resistance**: If the stock isn’t being rejected by the Exponential Moving Average (EMA), it’s likely to continue trending up. However, if it is rejected by the EMA, the stock may downtrend.

#### Area of Value:

- **Support/Resistance**: Critical price levels where the stock tends to reverse.
- **MA (Moving Average)**: Helps determine the overall trend direction and possible entry points.
- **EMA (Exponential Moving Average)**: Focuses on more recent price data and reacts faster than the MA.
- **Trendline**: Watch for the rate of increase or decrease in price.
- **Channel**: A defined range of price movement between support and resistance.

#### Entry Triggers - Candlestick Patterns:

- **Bullish Engulfing**: This pattern consists of two candlesticks. The first is a small red candle, and the second is a larger green candle that fully engulfs the red candle. It signifies a strong shift from bearish to bullish sentiment.
- **Hammer**: A hammer has a small body and a long lower wick, appearing at the bottom of a downtrend. It signals potential reversal, as strong buying pressure overcomes selling during the session. Green hammers are stronger indicators of a bullish market.
- **Morning Star**: A three-candle pattern signaling the end of a downtrend and the start of an uptrend. The pattern consists of a long red candle, a short-bodied candle (the 'star'), and a long green candle. It indicates that selling pressure is subsiding and buyers are gaining control.
- **Doji**: A doji has nearly identical opening and closing prices, indicating indecision between buyers and sellers. On its own, it is neutral, but when found in reversal patterns like the morning star or evening star, it signals potential trend changes.
- **Spinning Top**: A candlestick with a small body and long wicks on both sides. It signifies indecision in the market and is typically a consolidation pattern after a strong trend. This suggests a shift in market control, often indicating a potential reversal.
- **Tweezer Top**: A bearish reversal pattern that appears at the top of an uptrend, with two candles having nearly identical highs. This indicates that the buying pressure is weakening and that a potential downtrend is starting.
- **Tweezer Bottom**: A bullish reversal pattern at the bottom of a downtrend, with two candles having nearly identical lows. It signals that the selling pressure is subsiding and a potential uptrend may follow.
