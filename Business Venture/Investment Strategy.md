## 🧨 Key Components

### 1. 📈 **Options Swing Trading (Target: 10–25% monthly)**

- Focus: Buying **calls/puts** on high-momentum stocks (e.g. NVDA, TSLA, SPY)
    
- Timeframes: 3–10 days swings based on technicals (breakouts, reversals)
    
- Position size: ~$1K–2K per trade
    
- Exit early on 20–50% profit or strict stop-loss (20–30% loss)
    
- Weekly 2–4 trades with strong risk/reward setups
    

**Tools Needed:** TradingView, unusual options flow tools (like Cheddar Flow or Tradytics), earnings calendars

---

### 2. 💸 **Day Trading / Momentum Plays (Target: $500–1,000/month)**

- Focus on low-float runners, news catalysts, and premarket gaps
    
- Hold time: minutes to hours
    
- Use **tight stop-losses** (2–5%)
    
- Focus on 1–2 trades per day max
    

**Platforms:** Thinkorswim, Interactive Brokers, or WeBull for fast execution

---

### 3. 🧠 **LEAPS + Call Spreads (Target: 5–10%/month)**

- Buy **LEAPS** on quality growth stocks (e.g. AAPL, AMD, MSFT)
    
- Sell **monthly calls** against them (covered call strategy)
    
- Also use **bull call spreads** on SPY or QQQ for directional bias
    

This helps provide **lower risk**, predictable income when market is stable or trending.

---

### 4. 🧨 **Occasional Short Selling or Put Buying**

- Don’t make this your core — use **puts or inverse ETFs** to hedge positions
    
- If a sector looks extremely overbought (e.g., meme stocks), take advantage using:
    
    - Buying puts
        
    - Bearish credit spreads (like call credit spreads)
        
    - Short selling with **tight risk controls**
        

---

## 💼 Risk Management Rules

- Never risk more than **2–3% of total capital** on a single trade
    
- Use **hard stop-losses**
    
- Don’t overleverage even if using margin — stick to cash until consistent
    
- If drawdown exceeds 25%, **pause all trading** and reassess
    
- Always have a **weekly review** of wins/losses and reasons
