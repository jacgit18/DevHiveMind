Great question — when organizing workouts across **sessions or days**, the **order of operations** depends on your **goal** (power, strength, endurance, etc.), but there _is_ a general hierarchy that prioritizes **neurological demand and coordination first**, then **strength**, then **stability or burn-out work**.

---

### **General Order of Operations (Within a Single Session):**

1. **Explosive & Coordinated Movements (High Skill, Low Fatigue)**
    
    - **Medicine ball throws** (e.g., slams, rotational throws)
        
    - **Battle ropes** (short bursts)
        
    - **Sled pushes/pulls** (for speed/power)
        
    - **Tire flips**  
        _These activate the CNS and improve athletic power_
        
2. **Free Weights & Landmine Work (Strength & Power Core)**
    
    - **Squats, Deadlifts, Presses, Pulls**
        
    - **Landmine presses, rows, twists**  
        _These build the raw strength and movement control_
        
3. **Cable Machine & TRX (Controlled Tension & Stability)**
    
    - **Single-arm/balance work**
        
    - **TRX push-ups, rows, knee tucks**  
        _These focus on stability, mid-range strength, and joint control_
        
4. **Resistance Bands (Accessory & Burnout)**
    
    - **Band pull-aparts, bicep/tricep work, monster walks**  
        _Used for activation, volume, or prehab_
        
5. **Fixed Machines (Isolation, Prehab, Low Coordination)**
    
    - **Leg curl, pec deck, tricep pushdowns, etc.**  
        _Safe to do when fatigued; less CNS demand_
        

---

### **When Organizing by Days:**

You can **rotate tools across days** based on your training goal:

#### **Day 1 (Power + Full-body Strength)**

- Start with: **Medicine ball, sled, landmine**
    
- Then: **Free weights (compound lifts)**
    
- End with: **Battle ropes or TRX core finisher**
    

#### **Day 2 (Hypertrophy or Conditioning Focus)**

- Start with: **Cable or Landmine for volume**
    
- Then: **Resistance bands and TRX**
    
- Finish with: **Fixed machines burnouts or sled drag repeats**
    

#### **Day 3 (Mobility & Recovery / Stability)**

- **TRX, resistance bands, and cables**
    
- Light sled pulls or tire drags
    
- No free weights or high CNS lifts
    

---

### **Quick Ranking by Neural Demand (High to Low):**

1. **Medicine Ball / Battle Ropes (explosive coordination)**
    
2. **Sled / Tire (power and metabolic fatigue)**
    
3. **Free Weights / Landmine (compound strength)**
    
4. **Cable Machine / TRX (mid-level strength + stability)**
    
5. **Resistance Bands (activation or volume)**
    
6. **Fixed Machines (isolation or rehab)**



For someone focused on **building strength and size (hypertrophy + power)** using all the tools you mentioned — bands, TRX, sled, tire, medicine ball, ropes, landmine, free weights, cable machine, and machines — here’s a **4-day split** structured for **maximal results**:

---

## **Weekly Structure: (4-Day Push-Pull-Legs-Core Split)**

### **Day 1 – Upper Body Push (Chest, Shoulders, Triceps)**

**Goal**: Heavy compound lifts + hypertrophy + finishers

1. **Warm-Up Activation**
    
    - Band pull-aparts
        
    - TRX shoulder I-Y-T raises
        
2. **Primary Strength (Free Weights / Landmine)**
    
    - Bench Press or Landmine Press – 4×6
        
    - Dumbbell Shoulder Press – 3×8
        
    - Landmine Incline Press – 3×10
        
3. **Accessory Hypertrophy (Cable / Bands / Machines)**
    
    - Cable chest fly or banded fly – 3×12
        
    - Cable lateral raises or TRX lateral lean raises – 3×12
        
    - Tricep pushdowns or banded kickbacks – 3×15
        
4. **Finisher**
    
    - Sled push or tire push – 4×20 yards
        
    - Optional: Battle ropes – 3×30s for conditioning
        

---

### **Day 2 – Lower Body Strength & Size (Glutes, Quads, Hamstrings)**

**Goal**: Posterior chain + explosive legs

1. **Warm-Up Activation**
    
    - Band monster walks
        
    - Glute bridges
        
2. **Primary Strength (Free Weights / Sled)**
    
    - Trap bar deadlift or landmine squat – 4×6
        
    - Bulgarian split squat – 3×8 each leg
        
    - Sled push/pull heavy – 4×20 yards
        
3. **Accessory Work (Cable / Bands / Machines)**
    
    - Cable kickbacks or donkey kicks – 3×12
        
    - Leg curl machine or banded hamstring curls – 3×15
        
    - Calf raises (bodyweight or on machine) – 3×20
        
4. **Finisher**
    
    - Tire flips – 4×5
        
    - Optional: Jump rope or med ball slams – 3×30s
        

---

### **Day 3 – Rest or Active Recovery (Mobility / Core Stability / Swim)**

- TRX planks, stability ball rollouts, side planks
    
- Light sled drag, walking lunges
    
- Easy swimming or band mobility work
    

---

### **Day 4 – Upper Body Pull (Back, Biceps, Rear Delts)**

**Goal**: Heavy pulls + hypertrophy + grip

1. **Warm-Up Activation**
    
    - Band rows
        
    - Scapula pull-ups
        
2. **Primary Strength (Free Weights / Landmine)**
    
    - Barbell or Landmine row – 4×6
        
    - Weighted pull-ups or assisted TRX rows – 3×8
        
    - Landmine rear delt rows – 3×10
        
3. **Accessory Hypertrophy (Cable / Bands / TRX)**
    
    - Face pulls (cable or band) – 3×12
        
    - Bicep curls (cable or band) – 3×15
        
    - TRX reverse flys – 3×12
        
4. **Finisher**
    
    - Battle ropes (with squat variation) – 3×30s
        
    - Optional: Sled rope pull for grip and arms
        

---

### **Day 5 – Core + Full-Body Athletic Day**

**Goal**: Rotational strength + stability + athletic carryover

1. **Medicine Ball Work**
    
    - Rotational med ball throws – 3×6/side
        
    - Slam variations – 3×10
        
2. **Landmine Core / Anti-Rotation**
    
    - Landmine twists – 3×12
        
    - Landmine rainbow arc press – 3×10
        
3. **TRX & Cable Core Stability**
    
    - TRX knee tucks or rollouts – 3×10
        
    - Cable woodchopper or Pallof press – 3×12
        
4. **Finisher**
    
    - Sled sprint x 4
        
    - Optional: Tire slams or rope waves
        

---

### Notes:

- **Progressive overload** each week (either reps, sets, tempo, or resistance).
    
- **Nutrition** should support hypertrophy: eat in a slight caloric surplus.
    
- **Focus on form** and **tempo** — don’t rush eccentric phases.
