Throwing over **300 punches in under a minute**—an average of **5 punches per second**—is a serious conditioning feat. This requires explosive speed, muscular endurance, coordination, and efficient mechanics. Below is a **4-phase training regimen** to build up to this goal in about 8–10 weeks, assuming you already have basic boxing experience.

---

## **PHASE 1: FOUNDATIONS (Weeks 1–2)**

Focus: Technique, baseline conditioning, and form  
**Goal:** Build good punching mechanics to avoid injury and wasted energy.

**Daily Practice (30–45 mins/day)**

- **Shadowboxing:** 3 rounds x 2 min (focus on form and fluidity)
    
- **Straight Punch Volume Drill:** 5 sets x 30 seconds non-stop 1-2s (jab-cross), moderate pace
    
- **Push-ups + Plank Combo:**
    
    - 3 sets of 15 push-ups
        
    - 3 x 30 sec planks (focus on bracing the core while punching)
        
- **Jump rope:** 3 rounds x 2 min
    

---

## **PHASE 2: SPEED DEVELOPMENT (Weeks 3–4)**

Focus: Explosiveness, speed drills, and neuromuscular efficiency  
**Goal:** Build fast-twitch muscle activation and hand speed.

**Key Workouts (4–5x/week):**

- **Speed shadowboxing rounds:**
    
    - 3 rounds x 1 min “punch as fast as possible” in 10-second bursts
        
    - Rest 1 min between rounds
        
- **Resistance Band Punches:**
    
    - 3 sets x 20 punches per hand with resistance band
        
- **Plyometric Push-ups:** 3 sets of 10
    
- **High-speed double jab-cross drill (on bag):** 6 sets x 15 sec (max output) / 45 sec rest
    

---

## **PHASE 3: ENDURANCE + DENSITY (Weeks 5–7)**

Focus: Sustain high punch rate under fatigue  
**Goal:** Condition muscles and lungs to tolerate 60 seconds of output

**Key Workouts:**

- **Tabata bag rounds (20 sec on, 10 sec off x 8):** All-out punching
    
- **1-minute max punches (weekly test):** Count punches and record
    
- **Combo ladders (bag or shadow):**
    
    - 1-2 x 5 punches, rest 15 sec
        
    - Increase to 10, 15, 20, etc. (go as high as possible)
        
- **Hill sprints or Assault Bike sprints:** 6 x 15 sec all-out
    

---

## **PHASE 4: PEAKING + TESTING (Weeks 8–10)**

Focus: Refine breathing, rhythm, and max performance

**Goal:** Surpass 300 punches in 60 seconds.

**Key Practice:**

- **1-minute all-out punch test (2–3x/week)**
    
    - Focus on rhythm (e.g., 5 punches/second = 25 punches every 5 sec)
        
- **Mental rehearsal + breath control:**
    
    - Practice staying calm under fatigue—inhale every few punches
        
- **High-rep burnout sets (post-training):**
    
    - 100 straight punches as fast as possible, 3 rounds
        

---

## **Additional Notes:**

- **Track punch counts** each week and aim for +10% improvement
    
- Use apps or punch trackers (like FightCamp or punch counters on wearable)
    
- Focus on **light, fast punches**, not power—minimize wasted motion
    
- Recovery matters: sleep, protein intake, and hydration are key
