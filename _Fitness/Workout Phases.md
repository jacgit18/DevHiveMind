## Training Phases

**Phase shifts every 4–6 weeks** to avoid adaptation plateaus.  

### **Month 1: Hypertrophy Phase**

**Goal**: Build volume and muscle mass with moderate loads.

- **Techniques**:
    - Weekly rep undulation (e.g., 4x8 → 3x12)
    - Drop sets and cluster sets for isolation work

- **Progression**: Increase total reps/sets weekly
- **Tempo**: Slow eccentrics and shorter rest (30–60s)


### **Month 2: Strength & Power Phase**

**Goal**: Peak force output and speed.

- **Focus**:
    - Low-rep, fast-tempo compound lifts (3–6 reps)
    - Contrast training (e.g., squat + jump)

- **Key Rules**:
    - Prioritize power lifts early in session
    - Deload every 4th week (reduce volume by ~50%)

### **Month 3: Explosive Power Phase**

**Goal**: Convert strength into speed and dynamic athleticism.

- **Style**: Power-focused full-body (2–3x/week) + accessory work
    
- **Core Lifts**:
    - Olympic lift variations (hang cleans, push press)
    - Plyometrics (box jumps, broad jumps, med balthrows)
    - Speed squats, banded deadlifts

- **Methods**:
    - Contrast and complex training (e.g., trap bar deadlift → vertical jump)
    - Velocity-based work (bar speed monitored or light weight moved fast)

- **Rest**: Full recovery (2–5 min) between explosive sets
- **Optional**: Sled pushes, bounding drills, single-leg hops for coordination

### **2. Set & Rep Recommendations**  
| **Exercise Type** | **Sets** | **Reps** | **Tempo** | **Notes** |  
|-------------------------|----------|----------|-----------------|----------------------------|  
| **Lower-Body Explosive** (e.g., Banded Squat Jumps) | 3–5 | 3–6 | Fast concentric, controlled eccentric | Stop when speed declines (~10% drop in height/speed). |  
| **Upper-Body Explosive** (e.g., Banded Plyo Push-Ups) | 3–4 | 4–8 | Explode up, slow down | Focus on punching/clinch power. |  
| **Rotational Power** (e.g., Banded Medicine Ball Throws) | 3–4 | 5–8/side | Max effort throws | Anchor band behind for resistance. |  
| **Sport-Specific** (e.g., Banded Knee Strikes) | 3–5 | 5–8/leg | Fast recoil | Mimic Muay Thai clinch knees. |
