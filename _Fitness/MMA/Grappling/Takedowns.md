Here's a breakdown of **BJJ** and **judo takedowns**, along with the guards and submissions you can flow into from a standing position:

---

### **Judo Takedowns (Throws & Sweeps):**

#### 1. **Ouchi Gari (Inner Reap)**

- **Guard Transition**: Closed guard, single-leg X-guard, butterfly guard.
- **Submission Follow-Up**: Triangle choke, armbar, omoplata.

#### 2. **Osoto Gari (Outer Reap)**

- **Guard Transition**: Side control, closed guard if countered.
- **Submission Follow-Up**: Americana, kimura, guillotine (if opponent turtles).

#### 3. **Seoi Nage (Shoulder Throw)**

- **Guard Transition**: Side control, knee-on-belly.
- **Submission Follow-Up**: Armbar, Ezekiel choke, spinning arm lock.

#### 4. **Harai Goshi (Sweeping Hip Throw)**

- **Guard Transition**: Side control, mount.
- **Submission Follow-Up**: Mounted armbar, lapel choke, arm triangle.

#### 5. **Kouchi Gari (Small Inner Reap)**

- **Guard Transition**: Half guard, De La Riva guard.
- **Submission Follow-Up**: Guillotine, heel hook, kneebar.

#### 6. **Uchi Mata (Inner Thigh Throw)**

- **Guard Transition**: Side control, open guard if opponent scrambles.
- **Submission Follow-Up**: Triangle choke, north-south choke, leg lock.

#### 7. **Tomoe Nage (Sacrifice Throw)**

- **Guard Transition**: Closed guard, spider guard.
- **Submission Follow-Up**: Armbar, triangle, wrist lock.

---

### **BJJ-Specific Takedowns:**

#### 1. **Double-Leg Takedown**

- **Guard Transition**: Half guard, butterfly guard, or straight into side control.
- **Submission Follow-Up**: Guillotine (defense opportunity), D'arce choke, kimura.

#### 2. **Single-Leg Takedown**

- **Guard Transition**: Half guard, X-guard, or straight to back control.
- **Submission Follow-Up**: Guillotine, anaconda choke, calf slicer.

#### 3. **Body Lock Takedown**

- **Guard Transition**: Closed guard (if opponent recovers), side control.
- **Submission Follow-Up**: Americana, Ezekiel choke, rear-naked choke.

#### 4. **Snap-Down to Front Headlock**

- **Guard Transition**: Turtle position, sprawl guard.
- **Submission Follow-Up**: Guillotine, anaconda choke, Peruvian necktie.

#### 5. **Flying Armbar**

- **Guard Transition**: None (submission attempt from standing).
- **Submission Follow-Up**: Immediate armbar or triangle choke if countered.

#### 6. **Guard Pull to Sweep**

- **Guard Transition**: Butterfly guard, closed guard, De La Riva.
- **Submission Follow-Up**: Omoplata, ankle lock, triangle.

---

### **Flow Guards and Submission Pairings:**

#### **From Side Control:**

- Americana, kimura, arm triangle.
- Transition to knee-on-belly or mount for better control.

#### **From Mount:**

- Armbar, triangle, Ezekiel choke.

#### **From Half Guard:**

- Transition to deep half guard for sweeps.
- Use underhooks to go for kneebars or guillotines.

#### **From Butterfly Guard:**

- Use sweeps (e.g., butterfly sweep) to get on top.
- Triangle choke, armbar, or guillotine if the opponent pressures forward.

#### **From Open Guard (e.g., De La Riva):**

- Sweep to mount or back take.
- Go for leg locks, especially ankle locks or heel hooks.

---

### **Flow Drill Example:**

1. Start with **Ouchi Gari** takedown → land in **closed guard** → opponent defends → transition into a **triangle choke**.
2. Perform a **single-leg takedown** → land in **side control** → attack with **kimura** → transition to mount if defended.
