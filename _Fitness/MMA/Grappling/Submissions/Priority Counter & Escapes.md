### **Kimura (Shoulder Lock)**

#### **Escapes**:
 **Grip Prevention**:
- Lock your hands together (rear-naked choke grip) or hug your own thigh for stability.

**Roll with the Pressure**:
- Bridge and roll toward their attacking side (from top half guard).

**Straighten Your Arm**:
- Extend your arm to break their grip and reframe against their chest.

 **Posture Up (From Guard)**: 
- Drive your arm forward and posture away to escape.

**Step Over Their Head (From Bottom)**:
    - Use your free leg to step over their head, forcing them to release.

#### **Counters**:
1. **Attack Their Neck**:
    
    - Use a guillotine or other neck submission while they focus on the lock.
2. **Reverse the Kimura**:
    
    - Rotate your trapped arm inward and take control of their grip.

### **Americana (Keylock)**

#### **Escapes**:

1. **Grip Prevention**:
    
    - Straighten your arm or grab your own thigh/belt to block the lock.
2. **Bridge and Turn**:
    
    - Bridge and turn toward the side being attacked to relieve pressure.
3. **Trap and Re-Guard**:
    
    - Use your legs to trap their attacking arm and escape to guard.
4. **Shrimp Away (From Side Control)**:
    
    - Create space by shrimping your hips away to free your elbow.

#### **Counters**:

1. **Sweep Them**:
    
    - Explode with a bridge to roll them into bottom position.
2. **Overhook Their Arm**:
    
    - Overhook their elbow to reverse or set up your own submission.

### **Omoplata (Shoulder Lock from Guard)**

#### **Escapes**:

1. **Posture Up**:
    
    - Immediately straighten your back to prevent the lock.
2. **Roll Forward**:
    
    - Forward roll to escape and end in side control or turtle.
3. **Step Over Their Body**:
    
    - Step over their torso with your free leg to break their control.
4. **Circle Your Arm Free**:
    
    - Rotate your trapped arm backward in a circular motion to escape.
5. **Backstep (From Top)**:
    
    - Backstep with your free leg to create space and pull your arm free.

#### **Counters**:

1. **Stack Defense**:
    
    - Drive forward, stacking their body, and transition into a guard pass.
2. **Leg Lock Counter**:
    
    - Attack their free leg with a knee bar or ankle lock.
3. **Spin Around**:
    
    - Spin toward their legs to scramble or gain a dominant position.

---

### **Key Tips**:

- **Prevent First**: Strong posture and limb awareness stop setups before they happen.
- **Stay Calm**: Use angles and leverage, not brute strength.
- **Prioritize Escaping**: Focus on freeing yourself before thinking of counters.
