Here’s a breakdown of escapes and counters for the Kimura, Americana, and Omoplata:  
  
  
---  
  
Kimura (Shoulder Lock)  
  
Escapes:  
  
1. Grip Prevention:  
  
Clamp your hands together (e.g., rear-naked choke grip or figure-four grip) to prevent the Kimura grip from breaking your posture.  
  
Hug your own thigh to create stability.  
  
  
  
2. Roll with the Pressure:  
  
If they lock it from top half guard, bridge and roll toward the side they’re attacking to reduce pressure.  
  
  
  
3. Straighten the Arm:  
  
Straighten your arm to break the figure-four grip and reframe into your opponent’s chest to regain posture.  
  
  
  
4. Posture Up (From Guard):  
  
Drive your trapped arm forward and upward while posturing your body away.  
  
  
  
5. Step Over the Head (If on Bottom):  
  
Use your free leg to step over their head, forcing them to release pressure or lose position.  
  
  
  
  
  
---  
  
Counters:  
  
1. Counter Submission:  
  
While they focus on locking the Kimura, use your free hand to attack their neck (e.g., guillotine).  
  
  
  
2. Reverse the Lock:  
  
If they overcommit, rotate your trapped arm inward and attempt to reverse the Kimura grip onto them.  
  
  
  
  
  
---  
  
Americana (Keylock)  
  
Escapes:  
  
1. Grip Prevention:  
  
Straighten your arm before they lock the figure-four position.  
  
Grab your own thigh or belt to delay the submission.  
  
  
  
2. Bridge and Turn:  
  
Turn your body toward the side being attacked to alleviate shoulder pressure.  
  
  
  
3. Re-Guard:  
  
From bottom mount, use your legs to trap their attacking arm, creating space to escape to half or full guard.  
  
  
  
4. Shrimp Away (From Side Control):  
  
Shrimp your hips away from the opponent to slide your elbow free.  
  
  
  
  
  
---  
  
Counters:  
  
1. Sweep Attempt:  
  
Use their focus on the Americana to bridge explosively and roll into top position.  
  
  
  
2. Arm Isolation:  
  
As they isolate your arm, overhook their elbow with your free arm to set up a counter submission or reversal.  
  
  
  
  
  
---  
  
Omoplata (Shoulder Lock from Guard)  
  
Escapes:  
  
1. Posture Up:  
  
Straighten your back and posture immediately before they break your posture.  
  
  
  
2. Roll Forward:  
  
Forward roll to alleviate pressure, ending in side control or turtle position.  
  
  
  
3. Step Over Their Body:  
  
Use your free leg to step over their torso, forcing them to release the lock.  
  
  
  
4. Circle Arm Free:  
  
Rotate your trapped arm backward in a circular motion to slip out.  
  
  
  
5. Backstep:  
  
If in top half guard or mount, backstep with your free leg to create space and pull your arm free.  
  
  
  
  
  
---  
  
Counters:  
  
1. Stack Defense:  
  
Stack their body by driving forward into them and pinning their shoulders, then transition into a pass.  
  
  
  
2. Counter Submission:  
  
If they overcommit their legs, attack their free leg with a knee bar or ankle lock.  
  
  
  
3. Spin Around:  
  
Spin toward their legs to turn the position into a scramble or transition to a dominant position.  
  
  
  
  
  
---  
  
Key Tip: In all three cases, prevention is the first step—focus on maintaining strong posture and avoiding isolation of your limbs to stop the setups early. If you're caught, prioritize escaping the position over brute-forcing your way out.
