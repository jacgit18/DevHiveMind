### **1. Armbar (Straight Armbar)**

#### **From Top:**

- **Mount:** Isolate the arm by trapping it across your chest, swing your leg over the opponent’s head, and extend the arm.
- **S-Mount:** High mount allows tighter control of the arm before transitioning to the armbar.
- **Side Control:** Isolate the arm by controlling their wrist and elbow, then swing your leg over their head.
- **Knee-on-Belly:** Use pressure to force an arm extension and transition to the armbar.

#### **From Bottom:**

- **Closed Guard:** Break posture, isolate the arm, and pivot your hips to swing the leg over their head.
- **Spider Guard:** Use grip control to stretch the opponent’s arm and transition into an armbar.
- **Butterfly Guard:** Sweep or off-balance the opponent, isolating the arm during the transition.

#### **Counters:**

- **From a Guillotine Defense:** If the opponent defends a guillotine by extending an arm, transition into an armbar.
- **From a Failed Triangle:** If they stack to escape a triangle, isolate the arm and apply the armbar.

### **2. Guillotine Choke**

#### **From Top:**

- **Standing:** Snap down their head during a sprawl and wrap the neck for the choke.
- **Front Headlock:** Control their head after a takedown attempt and secure the guillotine.
- **Mount:** If they push into you, drop your chest and wrap their neck.

#### **From Bottom:**

- **Closed Guard:** Wrap their neck when they posture forward.
- **Butterfly Guard:** Use an arm drag to expose their neck.
- **Half Guard:** Trap their head and arm during pressure passing.

#### **Counters:**

- **From an Arm Triangle Escape:** If they roll out of an arm triangle, wrap their neck for a guillotine.
- **From Failed Armbar Defense:** When they posture out of an armbar, use the momentum to wrap their neck.

---

### **3. Rear Naked Choke (RNC)**

#### **From Top:**

- **Back Mount:** Use seatbelt control, secure one arm under the chin, and lock the choke.
- **Turtle Position:** Attack from behind when they’re defending from turtle.

#### **From Bottom:**

- **After a Sweep:** Sweep them and take their back as they recover.
- **From Transition:** If you escape side control, transition to their back for the RNC.


#### **Counters:**

- **From a Guillotine Defense:** If they defend by pulling their neck out, take their back for the RNC.
- **From Triangle Escape:** If they posture out of a triangle, transition to their back.

---

### **4. Triangle Choke**

#### **From Top:**

- **From Mount:** Trap one arm and isolate their head to drop into a mounted triangle.
- **Side Control:** Step over their shoulder to set up the triangle.

#### **From Bottom:**

- **Closed Guard:** Trap one arm and pivot your hips to lock your legs around their head.
- **Spider Guard:** Use grip control to manipulate their arms and isolate the neck.

#### **Counters:**

- **From a Guillotine Defense:** If they pull their neck out, trap one arm for a triangle.
- **From an Armbar Escape:** When they pull their arm free, switch to a triangle.

### **Flow Summary**

1. **Armbar → Triangle → Omoplata**
2. **Guillotine → Arm-In Guillotine → Arm Triangle**
3. **Rear Naked Choke → Armbar → Bow and Arrow Choke**
4. **Triangle → Armbar → Guillotine**
5. **Arm Triangle → Kimura → Guillotine**
6. **Ezekiel → Arm Triangle → Rear Naked Choke**

By mastering these sequences, you can create a dynamic submission strategy while recognizing counters and transitions.
