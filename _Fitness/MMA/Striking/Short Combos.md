## Short Combos

Here are **short MMA-optimized striking combinations**, designed for **quick execution** and **fluid movement**, while minimizing the risk of takedowns. These combos focus on **fast entries, angle changes, and exits** to avoid prolonged exchanges.

---

### **Mirror Opposite Stance (Orthodox vs. Southpaw or Vice Versa)**

_(Emphasizing open angles and defensive exits after striking.)_

-  **1 → 2 → 7.2**  
    _(Jab → Cross → Rear Round Kick)_
    
    
- **9 → 2 → 7.2**  
    _(Lead Teep → Cross → Rear Round Kick)_
    

- **1 → 2 → 15** 
    _(Jab → Cross → Spinning Back Kick)_
    

- **1 → 7.2 → 2**  
    _(Jab → Rear Round Kick → Cross)_

### **MMA Adaptations**

- **Shorter combos (2-4 strikes max)** to reduce vulnerability to takedowns.
- **Leg kicks & teeps** to off-balance opponents and create openings.
- **Elbows in close** to capitalize on clinch-range striking.
- **Side kicks to manage range** and prevent level changes for takedowns.
