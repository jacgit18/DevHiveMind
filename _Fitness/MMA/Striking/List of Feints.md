Feint with arm your trying to land with or to get to a certain position to land something


1. **Arm Pump Feint**: A quick upward motion with one or both arms, mimicking the preparation for a punch without committing. This can bait your opponent into reacting defensively.

2. **Elbow Lift Feint**: Slightly raise your elbow as though setting up a hook or uppercut, then pull back. This draws their attention to your head or guard, leaving other targets open.
    
3. **Arm Extension Feint**: Extend an arm as if to throw a jab or long punch, then pause and retract before making contact. This can mislead your opponent into overcommitting to a defense.
    
4. **Lead Hand Swish**: A wide, quick motion with the lead hand without punching. This disrupts your opponent’s rhythm and creates confusion about your intentions.
    
5. **Body Pullback**: Pull one arm back while keeping a forward stance, faking retreat or preparation for a low counter. This can provoke your opponent into overextending.
    
6. **Arm Flare**: Rapidly flare your arms outward, mimicking preparation for a clinch or unorthodox strike. This breaks their focus and anticipation.
    
7. **Fake Guard Adjustment**: Slightly shift or raise your hands as if adjusting your guard, making your opponent think you're preparing to block, which might lure them into attacking prematurely.
    
8. **Arm Circle Feint**: Draw small circles with your arm, creating a rhythm that distracts or misleads your opponent into preparing for a bigger strike.
    
9. **Arm Wave Feint**: Sweep your arm in a wide motion, resembling the start of a hook or looping punch. This can trigger your opponent’s defense unnecessarily.
    
10. **Shoulder Roll Feint**: Roll your shoulder slightly, as though setting up an uppercut or punch, but stop short. This can manipulate their timing and distance management.
    
11. **Elbow Dip Feint**: Lower your elbow subtly, imitating a setup for a low punch or body shot, then retract or redirect. This fakes a vulnerability or threat to the body.
    
12. **Torso Shift Feint**: Exaggerate an upper-body shift as if moving in aggressively, without closing the distance. This can prompt defensive reactions or retreat.
    
13. **Fake Body Turn**: Rotate your upper body quickly as though preparing a spinning strike, then stop midway. This deceives your opponent into guarding against an unlikely attack.
    
14. **Hands Behind Head Feint**: Briefly raise your arms near your head or neck, mimicking a clinch setup or counter-preparation. This can draw your opponent into overextending offensively.
    
15. **Arm Flick Feint**: Flick your hand or fingers like you’re snapping a jab or push-off. This subtle motion can disrupt your opponent's concentration and bait a reaction.
    
16. **Cross-Body Sweep Feint**: Sweep your arm across your body as if launching a hook or counterpunch, but stop mid-motion. This misleads them into guarding a wrong angle.
    
17. **Defensive Guard Feint**: Overemphasize raising your arms defensively, making it seem like you're blocking. This can make the opponent misjudge your readiness to counterattack.
    
18. **Fake Break or Reset**: Move your arms and posture as though stepping back to reset, then strike or engage unexpectedly. This creates a false sense of security for your opponent.
