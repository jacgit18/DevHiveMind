### **Leading with Your Left Leg (Orthodox Stance)**

Primary Stance

#### **Stepping Outside Their Left Leg (Same Stance as Opponent)**

- **Opportunities:**
    - **Angle Advantage**: By stepping outside your opponent’s lead leg, you gain a superior angle that forces them to adjust defensively. This positioning limits their ability to land effective counterattacks and opens up opportunities for you to land clean shots.
    - **Power Strikes**: The outside angle allows you to throw high-impact strikes, particularly with your rear hand and leg. Hooks and overhand punches become more effective from this position.
    - **Kicking Opportunities**: You can utilize your rear leg to land hard low kicks or body kicks to your opponent’s open side.
    - **Takedowns**: In grappling exchanges, this angle provides access to single-leg takedowns, sweeps, or trips by capitalizing on their off-balance position.
    
- **Weapons Open:**
    - Rear cross or overhand right
    - Lead hook to the head or body
    - Rear low kick or body kick
    - Clinch or grappling entries, such as a single-leg takedown



### **Leading with Your Right Leg (Southpaw Stance)**

#### **Stepping Outside Their Left Leg (Opposite Stance as Opponent)**

- **Opportunities:**
    - **Surprise Factor**: Many fighters are less experienced in dealing with southpaw opponents, making your movements and attacks more difficult to anticipate and defend against.
    - **Open Side**: By stepping outside their lead leg, you expose their open side, creating opportunities for strong rear-hand strikes and kicks.
    - **Counter Strikes**: This movement places you in an ideal position to evade their lead hand and foot while setting up precise counters with your rear hand and leg.

- **Weapons Open:**
    - Rear straight or overhand left
    - Lead hook to the head or body
    - Rear body kick or head kick
    - Clinch or grappling entries, such as a double-leg takedown



### **General Benefits of Moving Outside Your Opponent’s Lead Leg**

Regardless of which stance you use, stepping outside your opponent’s lead leg offers several key advantages:

- **Improved Defense**: By angling away from their lead hand and leg, you reduce their ability to land effective strikes, improving your defensive positioning.
- **Better Angles for Attack**: Moving to the outside creates openings for strikes that are harder for your opponent to see or block, especially powerful rear-hand punches and kicks.
- **Control of the Fight**: With better positioning, you can dictate the pace, direction, and rhythm of the fight, making it easier to execute your strategies.
- **Setting Traps**: Moving to the outside can bait your opponent into overextending, allowing you to counter effectively.
