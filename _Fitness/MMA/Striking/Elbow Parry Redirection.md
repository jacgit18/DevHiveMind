When using an elbow parry at close range, you can extend its effectiveness beyond just deflecting strikes. By slightly scooping or hooking under your opponent’s armpit, you can disrupt their balance and create openings for offense or disengagement.

### 🔹 **How It Works:**

1. **Scoop & Redirect** – Instead of a simple redirection, angle your elbow inward to wedge under their armpit. This creates a lifting effect, momentarily unweighting them.
    
2. **Off-Balancing** – By hoisting or guiding their upper body to the opposite side, you shift their center of gravity, making it harder for them to stay grounded.
    
3. **Follow-Up Opportunities:**
    
    - **Angle Out & Counter** – As they stumble or reset, pivot off and fire a strike.
        
    - **Clinch Entry** – If they react by stiffening up, transition into an underhook or collar tie.
        
    - **Breakaway & Reset** – If you need space, use their reaction to disengage safely.
        

This variation of the elbow parry is particularly useful against aggressive forward pressure, allowing you to turn their momentum against them while staying in control of the exchange.
