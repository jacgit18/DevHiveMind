### **Top Position Submissions**

#### **Passing Full Guard**

- **Arm Triangle**: Trap head and arm post-pass for a submission.
- **Americana**: Target a pinned arm once you've passed to a dominant position.
- **Kimura**: Transition to side control from guard pass and apply shoulder lock.

#### **Side Control**

- **Americana**: Pin the wrist and apply pressure to the shoulder joint.
    
    - **Safety Tip**: Perform from dominant positions to minimize risk of strikes.
- **Kimura**: Leverage wrist control to isolate the shoulder for a submission.
    
- **Arm Triangle**: Trap the opponent’s head and arm during guard pass transition.
    
- **Baseball Bat Choke**: Utilize lapel grips to choke the opponent.
    

#### **Mount**

- **Armbar**: Isolate the arm from mount, swinging into the lock.
- **Mounted Triangle**: Transition by trapping an arm and locking your legs.
- **Ezekiel Choke**: Apply a forearm choke using sleeve grips.
- **Cross Collar Choke**: Use deep lapel grips to apply pressure.

#### **North-South**

- **Kimura**: Leverage isolated arm control.
- **North-South Choke**: Apply choke pressure with head-and-arm control.

#### **Back Mount**

- **Rear Naked Choke (RNC)**: Use seatbelt grips and apply the choke.
- **Bow and Arrow Choke**: Use lapel grips while controlling the leg for the choke.

#### **Leg Entanglements (Top/Guard Passing)**

- **Straight Ankle Locks**: Attack exposed legs after passing guard.
- **Heel Hooks**: Apply rotational pressure on the knee during transitions.
- **Kneebars**: Isolate and extend the opponent's leg to secure the submission.

### **Key Safety Tips in Self-Defense Submissions:**

- **Avoid Exposing Yourself to Strikes**:
    
    - **Armbar** or **Triangle** from closed guard can expose you to punches if your opponent has better posture control.
- **Minimize Prolonged Leg Entanglements**:
    
    - Leg locks like **heel hooks** can leave you vulnerable to strikes, especially if the opponent remains mobile.
- **Don’t Sacrifice Position for Submissions**:
    
    - Avoid pulling guard or attempting submissions that may leave you open to slams or strikes.
- **Prioritize Control First**:
    
    - Always establish control of the opponent's posture, arms, and head before going for a submission.

### **Summary**

- **Bottom Position**: Focus on transitioning with sweeps into submissions like armbars, triangles, and guillotines. Open guards allow for defensive setups and offensive attacks.
- **Top Position**: After passing guard, focus on controlling the opponent and applying submissions like arm triangles, chokes, and leg locks.
