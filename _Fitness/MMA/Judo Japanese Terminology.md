### Throws (Nage-Waza)  
1. **Ippon Seoi Nage** - One Arm Shoulder Throw  
- **Description**: The thrower (Tori) uses their grip on the opponent’s (Uke) arm, rotating to bring Uke onto their back, and then throws Uke over the shoulder. This requires a strong pivot and use of the hips and legs for leverage.  
2. **Osoto Gari** - Major Outer Reap  
- **Description**: Tori steps to the outside of Uke and uses a sweeping motion with the leg to reap Uke’s leg from the outside, causing Uke to fall backward. This technique emphasizes timing and balance disruption.  
3. **Ouchi Gari** - Major Inner Reap  
- **Description**: Tori steps inside Uke’s stance and reaps Uke’s inner leg with their own leg while pulling Uke forward and down. The aim is to unbalance Uke and sweep their leg out from under them.  
4. **Kouchi Gari** - Minor Inner Reap  
- **Description**: Similar to Ouchi Gari but focuses on reaping Uke’s leg closer to the foot. Tori uses a quick and sharp movement to destabilize Uke and cause them to fall backward.  
5. **Harai Goshi** - Sweeping Hip Throw  
- **Description**: Tori turns into Uke, bringing their hip in close, and uses a sweeping motion with their leg to throw Uke over the hip. This requires strong hip movement and coordination.  
6. **Uchi Mata** - Inner Thigh Throw  
- **Description**: Tori positions themselves close to Uke and uses their leg to sweep Uke’s inner thigh while pulling down on Uke’s upper body. This throw is often used when Uke has a strong forward stance.  
7. **Tai Otoshi** - Body Drop  
- **Description**: Tori steps across in front of Uke, blocking Uke’s foot with their own foot and dropping their body to the side, pulling Uke down and over the blocked leg. This is a powerful and direct throw.  
8. **Koshi Guruma** - Hip Wheel  
- **Description**: Tori grips around Uke’s head and arm, bringing Uke onto their hip, and then rotates to throw Uke over the hip. This throw leverages the hips and upper body control.  
9. **Tani Otoshi** - Valley Drop  
- **Description**: Tori steps backward and to the side of Uke, using their leg to block Uke’s leg, then pulls Uke down into the "valley" created by Tori’s body. This is a counter-throw that capitalizes on Uke’s forward momentum.  
10. **Tomoe Nage** - Circle Throw  
- **Description**: Tori drops onto their back while placing a foot on Uke’s stomach, then uses the momentum and a pushing motion with the leg to flip Uke over in a circular motion. This throw requires precise timing and control.  
### Pins (Osae-Komi-Waza)  
1. **Kesa Gatame** - Scarf Hold  
- **Description**: Tori holds Uke by wrapping one arm around Uke’s head and the other under their arm, controlling Uke’s body with their legs spread wide for stability. This pin immobilizes Uke effectively.  
2. **Yoko Shiho Gatame** - Side Four Quarter Hold  
- **Description**: Tori lies perpendicular to Uke, controlling Uke’s upper body with a strong grip and spreading their legs for stability. This hold emphasizes chest pressure and control over Uke’s arms and head.  
3. **Kami Shiho Gatame** - Upper Four Quarter Hold  
- **Description**: Tori positions themselves above Uke’s head, using their arms to control Uke’s shoulders and their weight to pin Uke’s body to the mat. This hold is powerful and limits Uke’s movement.  
4. **Tate Shiho Gatame** - Mounted Position  
- **Description**: Tori sits astride Uke’s chest, using their weight and grip to control Uke’s arms and upper body. This dominant position allows for strong control and transition to other techniques.  
### Joint Locks (Kansetsu-Waza)  
1. **Ude Garami** - Bent Arm Lock  
- **Description**: Tori controls Uke’s arm, bending it at the elbow and applying pressure to the shoulder joint, either by twisting the arm outward or inward. This lock can cause significant discomfort and submission.  
2. **Juji Gatame** - Cross Arm Lock  
- **Description**: Tori isolates Uke’s arm, placing it between their legs and using the hips to hyperextend the elbow joint. This is a classic armbar that is highly effective for submission.  
3. **Ude Hishigi Hiza Gatame** - Knee Arm Lock  
- **Description**: Tori uses their knee to press down on Uke’s elbow, creating a lever effect that locks and hyperextends the arm. This lock is powerful and often used in transitions.  
### Strangles (Shime-Waza)  
1. **Hadaka Jime** - Naked Strangle  
- **Description**: Tori uses their arms to apply pressure to Uke’s carotid arteries from behind, without the use of the gi (uniform), to cut off blood flow and induce a choke. This can lead to quick submission.  
2. **Okuri Eri Jime** - Sliding Lapel Strangle  
- **Description**: Tori uses Uke’s lapel to apply pressure to the neck, sliding one lapel across while pulling the other side tight. This choke is effective and utilizes the gi for leverage.  
3. **Kataha Jime** - Single Wing Strangle  
- **Description**: Tori uses one of Uke’s arms to assist in the strangle, applying pressure with the lapel and the arm simultaneously. This creates a strong and inescapable choke.  
4. **Gyaku Juji Jime** - Reverse Cross Strangle  
- **Description**: Tori crosses their arms and uses the lapels to apply pressure to Uke’s neck, creating a cross-choke effect. This technique can be executed quickly and effectively.
