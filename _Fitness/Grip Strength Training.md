## 💪 Grip Strength Regimen: 50 lb to 200 lb (50 lb Increments)

### 🎯 Goal: Progress through 50 lb, 100 lb, 150 lb, and 200 lb grippers

**Timeframe per level:** ~4–6 weeks (varies by individual)

---

### 📍 **Phase 1: 50 lb Gripper – Foundation**

**Weeks 1–2**

- 2 sets of 20–25 reps, 2x/day
    
- Focus on smooth, full-range motion
    

**Weeks 3–6**

- Increase to 30 reps
    
- Add 1–2 second holds at the peak of each rep
    
- Start doing 1–2 sets of **negatives** (close with both hands, release slowly with one)
    

---

### 📍 **Phase 2: 100 lb Gripper – Strength Building**

**Weeks 1–2**

- 2–3 sets of 10–15 reps, 1–2x/day
    
- Add 5–10 second **static holds** on last rep
    
- Continue **negatives** for 1–2 sets
    

**Weeks 3–6**

- Push to 15–20 reps per set
    
- Focus on **explosive closes** and **slower releases**
    

---

### 📍 **Phase 3: 150 lb Gripper – Power Phase**

**Weeks 1–2**

- 3–5 sets of 5–8 reps
    
- Emphasize **quality over quantity**
    
- Continue using **static holds** and **negatives**
    

**Weeks 3–6**

- Drop to **lower rep ranges (3–5)** with **longer static holds (10–15 sec)**
    
- Use **thicker handles/towels** for variety
    
- Add **tempo reps** (3 sec up, 3 sec down)
    

---

### 📍 **Phase 4: 200 lb Gripper – Max Strength**

**Weeks 1–6 (varies)**

- Work up to **max effort singles and doubles**
    
- Hold each close for 3–5 seconds if possible
    
- Use **assisted closes**, **band-resisted work**, or **isometric crush holds** against an immovable object if needed
    
- Don’t rush — may take longer depending on recovery and hand size
    

---

### 🛠️ Tips Across All Phases

- **Rest 1–2 days/week** — grip takes time to recover
    
- **Stretch and massage forearms/hands** post-workout
    
- **Train extensors** (rubber band finger opens) to prevent imbalance
    
- Use **chalk or grip pads** if slipping
    
- Track your progress every 2 weeks
