### **Step 1: Reduce Inflammation & Pain**  

- **RICE Method** (Post-Training):  

- **Rest**: Avoid aggressive toe-loading (e.g., jump kicks) for 1–2 weeks.  

- **Ice**: 10 mins on/off every 2 hours (reduce swelling).  

- **Compression**: Wear a **toe sleeve** or buddy-tape to the 2nd toe for stability.  

- **Elevation**: Prop foot up when sitting.  

- **Topical Relief**: Arnica gel or **voltaren (diclofenac)** for acute pain.  

  

---

  

### **Step 2: Restore Mobility**  

#### **A. Toe CARs (Controlled Articular Rotations)**  

- *How*: Sit barefoot, lift big toe, and slowly:  

  1. **Circle it clockwise/counterclockwise** (10 reps each direction).  

  2. **Dorsiflex/Press into floor** (hold 5 sec, 10 reps).  

- *Goal*: Lubricate the joint and break up adhesions.  

  

#### **B. Passive Toe Stretches**  

1. **Towel Scrunches**: Sit, place towel under toes, and pull it toward you using only toe flexion (3x15 reps).  

2. **Manual Extension Stretch**:  

   - Grab your big toe, gently pull it upward (dorsiflex) until you feel a stretch.  

   - Hold 30 sec, 3x/day.  

  

---

  

### **Step 3: Strengthen the Toe**  

#### **A. Intrinsic Foot Work**  

- **Marble Pickups**: Use toes to grab marbles/pebbles and drop into a cup (3x10 reps).  

- **Toe Spreading**: Sit barefoot, spread toes apart maximally, hold 5 sec (3x10 reps).  

  

#### **B. Loaded Toe Flexion**  

- **Band-Resisted Toe Press**:  

  - Loop a resistance band around your big toe and press downward (plantarflex) against tension (3x12 reps).  

  

---

  

### **Step 4: Rebuild Fight-Specific Function**  

#### **A. Pivot Drills (Progressive Loading)**  

1. **Slow Pivots**: Barefoot, practice turning on the ball of your foot *without toe crunching* (3x10 reps each side).  

2. **Kick Rehab**: Light round kicks on pads, focusing on **landing on the ball of the foot** (not toes).  

  

#### **B. Balance Work**  

- **Single-Leg Stance on Toe Board**: Stand on a slant board (toes elevated), hold 30 sec/side.  

  

---

  

### **Step 5: Prevent Re-Injury**  

- **Tape for Support**: Use **rigid athletic tape** to limit excessive toe flexion during training:  

  - Wrap from under the big toe, around the heel, and back (creates a "sling").  

- **Footwear Check**:  

  - Avoid shoes with tight toe boxes (squishes toes).  

  - Opt for minimalist shoes (like **Vivobarefoot**) for toe splay.  

  

---

  

### **When to See a Doc**  

If pain persists >2 weeks or you notice:  

- **Clicking/locking** (may indicate **hallux rigidus** or bone spurs).  

- **Bruising/swelling** (possible sesamoid injury).  

  

---

1. **AM**: Toe CARs + manual stretch (5 mins).  

2. **Pre-Training**: Band-resisted toe presses (2x12).  

3. **Post-Training**: Ice + towel scrunches.  





## Wrist Curl


Here’s a **balanced weekly forearm and grip regimen** using the **3 wrist curl variations** — **neutral**, **palm-up**, and **palm-down** — with minimal equipment (dumbbells, resistance bands, or even water bottles/backpack). Designed for **2–3x per week**, 20 minutes max.

---

## 🏠 **Wrist Curl & Grip Strength Regimen (At-Home, 2–3x/Week)**

### 🧠 **Goal**:

- Improve **forearm strength**, **grip endurance**, and **wrist control**
    
- Prevent muscle imbalance (by training both flexors and extensors)
    

---

### 🗓️ **Weekly Plan Overview**

|Day|Focus|Notes|
|---|---|---|
|Mon|Strength + Control (Full)|All 3 grip variations|
|Wed|Endurance & Burnout Focus|High-rep + time-under-tension|
|Fri|Grip + Stability Focus|Isometrics & finger rolls|

---

## 🔄 **Core Regimen (Day 1 – Strength & Control)**

**Warm-Up (3–4 min)**

- Wrist Circles – 10 each way
    
- Finger Extensions – 20 reps (open/close fast)
    
- Towel Squeeze – 20 sec hold x 2
    

**Main Sets (Use Moderate Weight)**

|Exercise|Sets|Reps|Rest|
|---|---|---|---|
|Neutral Wrist Curls (Hammer Grip)|3|8–10 reps|60 sec|
|Palm-Up Wrist Curls (Supinated Grip)|3|10–12 reps|45–60 sec|
|Palm-Down Wrist Curls (Pronated Grip)|3|10–12 reps|45–60 sec|
|Standing Finger Roll Wrist Curls|2|8–10 reps|60 sec|

**Optional Finisher**:

- **Farmer’s Carry Hold** (with dumbbells or heavy bag) – 2 x 30 sec hold
    

---

## 🔥 **Day 2 – Endurance & Burnout (Light to Moderate Weight)**

|Exercise|Sets|Reps / Time|Notes|
|---|---|---|---|
|Palm-Up Wrist Curls|2|15–20 reps|Short rest (~30 sec)|
|Palm-Down Wrist Curls|2|15 reps|Slow eccentric (~3 sec)|
|Standing Finger Roll Wrist Curls|2|10–12 reps + 20 sec hold|Hold at fingertips on last rep|
|Towel Squeeze (using rolled towel)|2|30 sec squeeze hold|Max effort|
|Isometric Wrist Flexion Hold (Palms Up)|2|20 sec|Squeeze at mid-curl|

---

## 🧱 **Day 3 – Grip & Stability**

**Tools**: Band, towel, weight

| Exercise                             | Sets | Time / Reps          | Notes                     |
| ------------------------------------ | ---- | -------------------- | ------------------------- |
| Plate or Book Pinch Hold (1–2 books) | 3    | 20–30 sec            | Fingers only              |
| Band-Resisted Wrist Extension        | 2    | 12–15 reps           | Controlled pace           |
| Finger Extensions (with rubber band) | 3    | 20 reps              | Burnout at the end        |
| Neutral Wrist Curl + Pause at Top    | 2    | 8 reps + 2 sec pause | Add control challenge     |

---

### ✅ **Tips for Progression**

- **Add weight** or increase **range of motion** slowly
    
- Increase **reps**, then **sets**, then weight over weeks
    
- Focus on **tempo**: slow down the lowering phase (eccentric)
