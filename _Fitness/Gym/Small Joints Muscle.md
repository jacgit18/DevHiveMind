## Wrist Curl

Here’s a **balanced weekly forearm and grip regimen** using the **3 wrist curl variations** — **neutral**, **palm-up**, and **palm-down** — with minimal equipment (dumbbells, resistance bands, or even water bottles/backpack). Designed for **2–3x per week**, 20 minutes max.

---

## 🏠 **Wrist Curl & Grip Strength Regimen (At-Home, 2–3x/Week)**

### 🧠 **Goal**:

- Improve **forearm strength**, **grip endurance**, and **wrist control**
    
- Prevent muscle imbalance (by training both flexors and extensors)
    

---

### 🗓️ **Weekly Plan Overview**

|Day|Focus|Notes|
|---|---|---|
|Mon|Strength + Control (Full)|All 3 grip variations|
|Wed|Endurance & Burnout Focus|High-rep + time-under-tension|
|Fri|Grip + Stability Focus|Isometrics & finger rolls|

---

## 🔄 **Core Regimen (Day 1 – Strength & Control)**

**Warm-Up (3–4 min)**

- Wrist Circles – 10 each way
    
- Finger Extensions – 20 reps (open/close fast)
    
- Towel Squeeze – 20 sec hold x 2

- Band roll-ups & unrolls - 20lb/20lb/20lb
    

**Main Sets (Use Moderate Weight)**

|Exercise|Sets|Reps|Rest|
|---|---|---|---|
|Neutral Wrist Curls (Hammer Grip)|3|8–10 reps|60 sec|
|Palm-Up Wrist Curls (Supinated Grip)|3|10–12 reps|45–60 sec|
|Palm-Down Wrist Curls (Pronated Grip)|3|10–12 reps|45–60 sec|
|Standing Finger Roll Wrist Curls|2|8–10 reps|60 sec|

**Optional Finisher**:

- **Farmer’s Carry Hold** (with dumbbells or heavy bag) – 2 x 30 sec hold
    

---

## 🔥 **Day 2 – Endurance & Burnout (Light to Moderate Weight)**

|Exercise|Sets|Reps / Time|Notes|
|---|---|---|---|
|Palm-Up Wrist Curls|2|15–20 reps|Short rest (~30 sec)|
|Palm-Down Wrist Curls|2|15 reps|Slow eccentric (~3 sec)|
|Standing Finger Roll Wrist Curls|2|10–12 reps + 20 sec hold|Hold at fingertips on last rep|
|Towel Squeeze (using rolled towel)|2|30 sec squeeze hold|Max effort|
|Isometric Wrist Flexion Hold (Palms Up)|2|20 sec|Squeeze at mid-curl|

---

## 🧱 **Day 3 – Grip & Stability**

**Tools**: Band, towel, weight

| Exercise                             | Sets | Time / Reps          | Notes                     |
| ------------------------------------ | ---- | -------------------- | ------------------------- |
| Plate or Book Pinch Hold (1–2 books) | 3    | 20–30 sec            | Fingers only              |
| Band-Resisted Wrist Extension        | 2    | 12–15 reps           | Controlled pace           |
| Finger Extensions (with rubber band) | 3    | 20 reps              | Burnout at the end        |
| Neutral Wrist Curl + Pause at Top    | 2    | 8 reps + 2 sec pause | Add control challenge     |

---

### ✅ **Tips for Progression**

- **Add weight** or increase **range of motion** slowly
    
- Increase **reps**, then **sets**, then weight over weeks
    
- Focus on **tempo**: slow down the lowering phase (eccentric)
