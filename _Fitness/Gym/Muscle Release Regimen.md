## *RELEASE: Full-Body Self-Myofascial Protocol (10–20 min)**

**Tools Needed**:

- Foam roller (standard or textured)
- Lacrosse ball or massage ball
- Optional: peanut ball, massage stick, yoga block



---

### 🦶 **FEET & LOWER LEG & Toes**

|Area|Tool|Duration|Notes|
|---|---|---|---|
|Plantar Fascia|Lacrosse Ball|1 min/foot|Roll from heel to toe ball slowly|
|Calves|Foam Roller|1–2 min/leg|Prop up other leg for deeper pressure, dorsiflex foot while rolling|
|Peroneals (outer shin)|Lacrosse Ball|1 min/side|Sit and dig along outer shin/calf|

---

### 🦵 **THIGHS (FRONT, SIDE, INNER)**

|Area|Tool|Duration|Notes|
|---|---|---|---|
|Quads|Foam Roller|2 min|Go top to bottom; lean to target vastus lateralis|
|IT Band / TFL|Foam Roller|1 min|Roll gently, don’t stay long — aim to release tension, not torture|
|Adductors|Lacrosse Ball or Foam Roller|1 min/side|Lie face-down, place ball inside thigh, bend and straighten knee slowly|

---

### 🍑 **HIPS & GLUTES**

|Area|Tool|Duration|Notes|
|---|---|---|---|
|Glute Max/Med|Lacrosse Ball|1–2 min/side|Cross ankle over opposite knee to open up|
|Piriformis|Lacrosse Ball|1 min|Slightly deeper — target top outer butt|
|Hip Flexors|Lacrosse Ball (floor or wall)|1 min/side|Lie face-down, find point near ASIS (hip bone), press gently|

---

### 🧍 **LOW BACK & SPINE (Light Pressure)**

|Area|Tool|Duration|Notes|
|---|---|---|---|
|Thoracolumbar Fascia|Foam Roller|1–2 min|Avoid direct lumbar pressure — roll gently above sacrum|
|QL (Side of lower back)|Lacrosse Ball|1 min/side|Lean into wall/ball, breathe deeply into tension|

---

### 🦴 **MID & UPPER BACK**

|Area|Tool|Duration|Notes|
|---|---|---|---|
|Thoracic Spine|Foam Roller|2 min|Hug yourself, roll from mid-back to top of shoulders|
|Rhomboids / Traps|Peanut Ball / Lacrosse Ball|1–2 min|Lie on ball between spine and shoulder blade|
|Lats|Foam Roller / Ball|1 min/side|Lay on side, reach arm overhead, roll slowly from armpit to mid-ribcage|

---

### 💪 **SHOULDERS & CHEST**

|Area|Tool|Duration|Notes|
|---|---|---|---|
|Rear Delt|Lacrosse Ball|1 min|Lie on ball or use wall, move arm across body while rolling|
|Pec Minor|Lacrosse Ball|1 min/side|Place ball just below collarbone, press against wall or floor and move arm in circles|
|Front Delt|Lacrosse Ball|1 min|Use wall or floor pressure, sweep arm gently for active release|

---

### ✋ **ARMS & FOREARMS**

|Area|Tool|Duration|Notes|
|---|---|---|---|
|Biceps|Lacrosse Ball|1 min/side|Roll on bench or floor, rotate hand palm-up/down|
|Triceps|Lacrosse Ball|1 min/side|Prop on edge of bench or wall, work from elbow up|
|Forearms|Lacrosse Ball|1–2 min each|Roll both flexor (palm side) and extensor sides|

---

### 🧠 **BONUS: SCALP, NECK & JAW (Gentle Work)**

|Area|Tool|Duration|Notes|
|---|---|---|---|
|Upper Traps|Lacrosse Ball|1 min/side|Lean against wall, move head side-to-side slowly|
|Suboccipitals|Peanut Ball (floor)|1–2 min|Lie on back, place ball under skull base, gently nod head|
|Jaw (Masseter)|Fingers or soft ball|30s–1 min|Gentle pressure near cheekbone while opening jaw slowly|

---

### ☑️ Optional Flow Order

1. **Start from feet → move up the chain.**
    
2. Hit “problem” zones twice/week with longer sessions.
    
3. Daily mini-targeting (5–10 min) for problem areas works wonders.
