### When to Breathe: Inhale vs. Exhale

Proper breathing enhances efficiency, safety, and stability during exercises. Here's a clear guide:

---

### **General Rule**

- **Exhale**: During the hardest part of the movement (concentric/ascending phase).
- **Inhale**: During the easier part (eccentric/descending phase).

---

### **Breathing Guide by Movement**

1. **Eccentric (Descending Phase)**:
    - **Inhale**: Prepares your body for exertion and maintains control.

2. **Concentric (Ascending Phase)**:
    - **Exhale**: Generates power and stabilizes the core.

3. **Isometric Hold**:
    - **Exhale slightly** or **briefly hold your breath**: Avoid excessive straining, maintaining stability.

4. **Push (Pressing a Weight)**:
    - **Exhale**: During the pushing motion (e.g., bench press).

5. **Pull (Drawing a Weight)**:
    - **Exhale**: While pulling toward your body (e.g., pull-up or row).

6. **Brace (Core Engagement)**:
    - **Inhale and hold briefly**: Increases intra-abdominal pressure for stability (e.g., deadlifts).

7. **Joint Extension**:
    - **Exhale**: As the joint straightens against resistance (e.g., leg extension).

8. **Joint Flexion**:
    - **Exhale**: As the joint bends under resistance (e.g., bicep curls).

9. **Twist/Rotate**:
    - **Exhale**: During twisting for core engagement.

10. **Explosive Movement (Drive)**:
    - **Exhale forcefully**: Maximizes power during explosive phases.

11. **Stabilization (Balance or Holds)**:
    - **Controlled, rhythmic breathing**: Maintains balance and focus.

---

### **Practical Applications**

#### **Squat**
- **Inhale**: Lowering into the squat (eccentric).
- **Exhale**: Standing up (concentric).

#### **Bench Press**
- **Inhale**: Lowering the bar (eccentric).
- **Exhale**: Pressing the bar up (concentric).

#### **Deadlift**
- **Inhale and brace**: Before lifting the weight.
- **Exhale**: As you lock out at the top.

#### **Plank**
- Maintain **slow, steady breathing** throughout the hold.

---

**Key Benefits**:

- Reduces fatigue.
- Improves stability.
- Enhances safety and performance.
