### **How the Brock String Helps You (Age 31, Nearsighted, Worsening Vision)**  

> Alternate with and without glasses

1. **Reduces Eye Strain**  

   - If you spend long hours on screens (computer/phone), your eye muscles can "lock" in near-focus mode, worsening myopia. The Brock String trains your eyes to **flexibly switch focus** between distances, reducing strain.  

  

2. **Strengthens Eye Teamwork**  

   - Nearsightedness often leads to **lazy eye teaming** (eyes not coordinating well). The string forces both eyes to align properly, preventing **subtle misalignment** that can blur vision.  

  

3. **Slows Myopia Progression**  

   - While it won’t reverse myopia, studies show **active focus training** (like Brock String drills) may slow worsening by improving how your eyes manage close-up stress.  

  

4. **Improves Clarity & Depth Perception**  

   - If your distance vision feels "fuzzier" over time, this tool sharpens **convergence** (how eyes turn inward for near tasks) and **accommodation** (focusing power).

### **Your Customized Brock String Plan**  

![📍How To Do The FAMOUS Vision Therapy Exercise: Brock String Exercise - YouTube](https://youtu.be/4rDygaF3Dog?si=0isBlPCEC9mv-kaQ)

**Goal:** Combat worsening myopia + reduce digital eye strain.  

**Frequency:** 3–5 days/week, 5–10 mins/day.  

  

#### **Phase 1 (Weeks 1–2): Reset Your Focus**  

- **Exercise:** Static Bead Focus (as in the earlier regimen).  

- **Key Focus:** Ensure the "X" shape appears correctly at each bead. If not, your eyes are misaligned—keep practicing!  

  

#### **Phase 2 (Weeks 3–4): Dynamic Drills**  

- **Exercise 1:** Rapid Near-Far Shifts  

  - Alternate between the closest bead (15cm) and farthest (60cm) every 2 seconds.  

  - *Why?* This mimics "focus flexibility" needed to combat screen-induced myopia.  

  

- **Exercise 2:** "Blur-to-Clear" Training  

  - Focus on a mid-distance bead until it’s crystal clear, then shift to a farther object (e.g., a wall clock).  

  

#### **Phase 3 (Ongoing): Prevent Regression**  

- Use the string **2–3x/week** as maintenance.  

- Pair with the **20-20-20 Rule**: Every 20 mins on screens, look at a bead 20cm away for 20 sec.

### **What to Expect**  

- **1–2 Weeks:** Reduced eye fatigue, easier switching between screens/distance.  

- **1 Month:** Sharper focus at intermediate distances (e.g., dashboard, whiteboard).  

- **Long-Term:** Potentially slower myopia progression if combined with:  

  - Outdoor time (natural light helps myopia control).  

  - Regular optometrist checkups (rule out other issues).  

  

---

  

### **Critical Notes**  

- **Not a Cure:** It won’t replace glasses/contacts but can improve how your eyes function.  

- **For Best Results:** Combine with:  

  - Proper screen ergonomics (arm’s length distance).  

  - Blue-light filters at night.  

  

If your myopia is worsening rapidly, see an **optometrist specializing in myopia management** (they may suggest ortho-k lenses or low-dose atropine).
