It sounds like you're dealing with **stiffness, pain, and reduced mobility in your big toe (hallux)**—common issues for fighters who frequently land on their toes during kicks, pivots, or footwork. Here’s a targeted rehab plan to restore flexibility, strength, and pain-free movement:

  

---

  

### **Step 1: Reduce Inflammation & Pain**  

- **RICE Method** (Post-Training):  

  - **Rest**: Avoid aggressive toe-loading (e.g., jump kicks) for 1–2 weeks.  

  - **Ice**: 10 mins on/off every 2 hours (reduce swelling).  

  - **Compression**: Wear a **toe sleeve** or buddy-tape to the 2nd toe for stability.  

  - **Elevation**: Prop foot up when sitting.  

  

- **Topical Relief**: Arnica gel or **voltaren (diclofenac)** for acute pain.  

  

---

  

### **Step 2: Restore Mobility**  

#### **A. Toe CARs (Controlled Articular Rotations)**  

- *How*: Sit barefoot, lift big toe, and slowly:  

  1. **Circle it clockwise/counterclockwise** (10 reps each direction).  

  2. **Dorsiflex/Press into floor** (hold 5 sec, 10 reps).  

- *Goal*: Lubricate the joint and break up adhesions.  

  

#### **B. Passive Toe Stretches**  

1. **Towel Scrunches**: Sit, place towel under toes, and pull it toward you using only toe flexion (3x15 reps).  

2. **Manual Extension Stretch**:  

   - Grab your big toe, gently pull it upward (dorsiflex) until you feel a stretch.  

   - Hold 30 sec, 3x/day.  

  

---

  

### **Step 3: Strengthen the Toe**  

#### **A. Intrinsic Foot Work**  

- **Marble Pickups**: Use toes to grab marbles/pebbles and drop into a cup (3x10 reps).  

- **Toe Spreading**: Sit barefoot, spread toes apart maximally, hold 5 sec (3x10 reps).  

  

#### **B. Loaded Toe Flexion**  

- **Band-Resisted Toe Press**:  

  - Loop a resistance band around your big toe and press downward (plantarflex) against tension (3x12 reps).  

  

---

  

### **Step 4: Rebuild Fight-Specific Function**  

#### **A. Pivot Drills (Progressive Loading)**  

1. **Slow Pivots**: Barefoot, practice turning on the ball of your foot *without toe crunching* (3x10 reps each side).  

2. **Kick Rehab**: Light round kicks on pads, focusing on **landing on the ball of the foot** (not toes).  

  

#### **B. Balance Work**  

- **Single-Leg Stance on Toe Board**: Stand on a slant board (toes elevated), hold 30 sec/side.  

  

---

  

### **Step 5: Prevent Re-Injury**  

- **Tape for Support**: Use **rigid athletic tape** to limit excessive toe flexion during training:  

  - Wrap from under the big toe, around the heel, and back (creates a "sling").  

- **Footwear Check**:  

  - Avoid shoes with tight toe boxes (squishes toes).  

  - Opt for minimalist shoes (like **Vivobarefoot**) for toe splay.  

  

---

  

### **When to See a Doc**  

If pain persists >2 weeks or you notice:  

- **Clicking/locking** (may indicate **hallux rigidus** or bone spurs).  

- **Bruising/swelling** (possible sesamoid injury).  

  

---

  

### **Sample Daily Routine**  

1. **AM**: Toe CARs + manual stretch (5 mins).  

2. **Pre-Training**: Band-resisted toe presses (2x12).  

3. **Post-Training**: Ice + towel scrunches.  

  

**Key Insight**: Most toe stiffness comes from **poor toe push-off mechanics**. Retrain your foot to **land/load through the ball of the foot**, not the toe tip.
