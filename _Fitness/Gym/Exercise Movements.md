## Types of Exercise Movements  
  
1. Descending (Eccentric Phase): Lowering the weight (e.g., lowering during a squat).  
  
  
2. Ascending (Concentric Phase): Lifting the weight (e.g., pushing up in a bench press).  
  
  
3. Squeeze/Contraction: Isometric hold (e.g., pausing at the top of a bicep curl).  
  
  
4. Push: Pressing the weight away (e.g., during a chest press).  
  
  
5. Pull: Drawing the weight toward the body (e.g., in a row or lat pulldown).  
  
  
6. Brace: Tightening the core (e.g., during a deadlift setup).  
  
  
7. Extend: Straightening a joint (e.g., extending the legs during a leg extension).  
  
  
8. Flex: Bending a joint (e.g., curling the arms in a bicep curl).  
  
  
9. Isometric Hold: Holding a position without movement (e.g., plank or wall sit).  
  
  
10. Twist/Rotate: Rotational movement (e.g., Russian twists).  
  
  
11. Drive: Explosive movement (e.g., jumping or sprinting).  
  
  
12. Stabilize: Balancing or maintaining position (e.g., during single-leg exercises).
