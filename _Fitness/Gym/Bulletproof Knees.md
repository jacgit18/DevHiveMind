Here’s a **Bulletproof Knees Regimen** — a structured weekly plan designed to strengthen the muscles, tendons, and ligaments around the knees, improve mobility, and reduce injury risk. This is inspired by protocols like **ATG (Knees Over Toes)** and physical therapy best practices.

---

## 🔥 **Bulletproof Knees Weekly Regimen** (Beginner to Intermediate)

**Frequency**: 3–4x/week  
**Goal**: Strengthen VMO, hamstrings, glutes, calves, and improve ankle/hip/knee mobility.

---

### 🧠 **Key Principles**

- **Full Range of Motion** beats partial.
    
- **Slow, controlled eccentric** builds resilience.
    
- **Assisted > Weighted** until form is flawless.
    
- Prioritize **knee travel and mobility** early on.
    

---

## 🔹 **Day Structure (30–45 min)**

### 1. **Warm-Up (5–10 min)**

- **Sled Push/Drag** – 2x20m (if unavailable, do backward walking)
    
- **Backward Treadmill Walk** – 3 min
    
- **Ankle Bounces** – 2x20
    
- **Hip Flexor Stretch + Glute Activation** – 1 min per side
    

---

### 2. **Mobility + Stability Work (10–15 min)**

|Exercise|Sets x Reps|Notes|
|---|---|---|
|**ATG Split Squat**|3x5/side|Knee over toe, heel flat, go slow|
|**Patrick Step**|2x10/side|Step down, control eccentric|
|**Tibialis Raise (Wall/Slant)**|3x15|Tibialis anterior is key to knee control|
|**Hamstring Isometric Bridge**|3x30s|Heel digs or sliders|
|**Slant Board Calf Stretch**|2x30s|Keep knees bent during stretch|

---

### 3. **Strength & Resilience (15–20 min)**

|Exercise|Sets x Reps|Notes|
|---|---|---|
|**Poliquin Step-Up**|3x6/side|Emphasize controlled descent|
|**Sissy Squats (assisted)**|3x8|Go deep but don’t collapse at bottom|
|**Nordic Curl (eccentric focus)**|3x3-5|Use assistance if needed|
|**Reverse Sled Drag**|3x20m|Constant tension, burn the quads|
|**Box Jumps / Broad Jumps (Low Impact)**|2x5|Optional – focus on soft landings|

---

### 4. **Cool Down & Flexibility (5–10 min)**

- **90/90 Hip Switches** – 2x10
    
- **Couch Stretch** – 1 min per side
    
- **Seated Hamstring Reach** – 1 min
    
- **Wall Calf Stretch** – 1 min per side
    

---

## 🗓 Suggested Weekly Plan

|Day|Focus|
|---|---|
|Monday|Full Session|
|Tuesday|Active Recovery (Walk, Cycle, Stretch)|
|Wednesday|Full Session|
|Thursday|Rest / Light Mobility|
|Friday|Full Session|
|Saturday|Optional Sled Work + Stretch|
|Sunday|Rest|

---

### ✅ Progression Tips

- Don’t rush weight – **form first**.
    
- Log ROM and **track pain-free depth**.
    
- Progress from assisted → bodyweight → loaded.
    
- If knees hurt: regress range, load, or frequency.
