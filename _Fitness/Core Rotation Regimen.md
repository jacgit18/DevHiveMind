### 5. 🧎‍♂️ **Good Mornings**

- **Frequency:** 1–2x/week (replace Romanian Deadlifts)
    
- **Phase 1:** Bodyweight or light bar (PVC or empty barbell)
    
- **Phase 2:** Add barbell gradually (start with 45–65 lbs)
    
- **Transition When:** 3x10 with flat back and hip hinge control
    

✅ _Brace core like you’re doing a plank during reps_

---

### 6. 🧘 **Jefferson Curl**

- **Frequency:** 1–2x/week (mobilization + spinal strength)
    
- **Phase 1:** Use 5–10 lb dumbbell, slow descent (3x5)
    
- **Phase 2:** Add more weight or increase reps (up to 20–25 lbs over time)
    
- **Transition When:** Full range with control and no lower back discomfort
    

✅ _Use slow 3–5 sec descent, pause at bottom_

---

### 7. ↩️ **Side Lateral QL Extension (Side Bends on Hyper Bench)**

- **Frequency:** 1–2x/week
    
- **Phase 1:** Bodyweight only (3x8–10 each side)
    
- **Phase 2:** Hold a dumbbell on top-side
    
- **Transition When:** You can control 3x12 with light load
    

✅ _Focus on contracting QL without hip hiking or shoulder dipping_

---

### 8. 🌀 **Core Rotation on Back Extension Machine**

- **Frequency:** 2–3x/week
    
- **Phase 1:** Bodyweight torso rotations (2x10–15 per side)
    
- **Phase 2:** Light plate/dumbbell in hand
    
- **Phase 3:** Add resistance bands or weighted reps
    
- **Transition When:** 3x15 controlled reps with rotation through T-spine
    

✅ _Exhale with rotation to activate obliques better_

|Week|Focus|
|---|---|
|1–3|Master bodyweight form & mobility|

|6–10|Progress to barbell & weighted reps|
|10+|Layer in tempo, rotation, and power|

## 🏋️ Add-On Free Weight Tools

- Dumbbells (5–30 lbs to start)
    
- Weighted plates (5–25 lbs)
    
- Barbell with light plates (35–65 lbs)
    
- Resistance bands (for controlled rotation)
    

---

## 💡 Tips

- Warm up hips, hamstrings, and core before every session
    
- Focus on tempo and stability over load early on
    
- Combine back/QL work with rotational core for spinal control
    
- Film reps to monitor posture and symmetry
