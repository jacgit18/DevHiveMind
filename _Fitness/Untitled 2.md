### **Ultimate Guide to Push vs. Pull Exercises (Categorized by Body Part & Movement Patterns)**  
  
This guide breaks down **every major exercise** into **push** or **pull** based on the **primary muscles worked** and **joint movement**. Use this to design balanced workouts and prevent muscle imbalances.  
  
---  
  
## **1. PUSH EXERCISES**  
*(Movements where you **push** resistance away from your body or your body away from a surface.)*  
  
### **A. Horizontal Push** *(Forward/Backward Motion)*  
**Primary Muscles:** Chest (Pecs), Front Delts, Triceps  
- **Barbell Bench Press**  
- **Dumbbell Bench Press**  
- **Push-Ups**  
- **Incline Bench Press** (Upper Chest Focus)  
- **Decline Bench Press** (Lower Chest Focus)  
- **Chest Flys** *(Technically not a push but often grouped here)*  
  
### **B. Vertical Push** *(Up/Down Motion Overhead)*  
**Primary Muscles:** Shoulders (Delts), Triceps  
- **Overhead Press (OHP) / Military Press**  
- **Dumbbell Shoulder Press**  
- **Arnold Press**  
- **Push Press** (Explosive)  
- **Handstand Push-Ups**  
  
### **C. Elbow Extension (Triceps-Dominant Push)**  
**Primary Muscles:** Triceps  
- **Tricep Dips** (Parallel Bars or Bench)  
- **Skull Crushers (Lying Tricep Extensions)**  
- **Tricep Pushdowns (Cable/Rope)**  
- **Close-Grip Bench Press**  
- **Overhead Tricep Extension**  
  
---  
  
## **2. PULL EXERCISES**  
*(Movements where you **pull** resistance toward your body or your body toward a surface.)*  
  
### **A. Horizontal Pull** *(Rowing Motion)*  
**Primary Muscles:** Upper/Mid Back (Lats, Traps, Rhomboids), Rear Delts, Biceps  
- **Bent-Over Barbell Rows**  
- **Seated Cable Rows**  
- **T-Bar Rows**  
- **Dumbbell Rows (Single-Arm or Chest-Supported)**  
- **Inverted Rows (Bodyweight)**  
- **Face Pulls** *(Rear Delt Focus)*  
  
### **B. Vertical Pull** *(Up/Down Motion Toward Body)*  
**Primary Muscles:** Lats, Biceps, Traps  
- **Pull-Ups / Chin-Ups**  
- **Lat Pulldowns (Wide/Narrow Grip)**  
- **Assisted Pull-Up Machine**  
- **Straight-Arm Pulldowns**  
  
### **C. Elbow Flexion (Biceps-Dominant Pull)**  
**Primary Muscles:** Biceps, Forearms  
- **Barbell Bicep Curls**  
- **Dumbbell Curls (Hammer, Concentration, etc.)**  
- **Preacher Curls**  
- **Chin-Ups (Biceps Emphasis)**  
- **Cable Curls**  
  
---  
  
## **3. SPECIAL CASES (Push/Pull Hybrid or Unique)**  
Some exercises don’t fit neatly into **strict push/pull** categories but are worth noting:  
  
### **A. Compound Lifts (Mixed Push/Pull)**  
- **Deadlifts** *(Hip hinge, but involves pulling the bar up)*  
- **Kettlebell Swings** *(Hip-dominant but not pure pull)*  
- **Power Cleans** *(Explosive pull + push at the top)*  
  
### **B. Leg Exercises (Mostly Push, Some Pull)**  
- **Squats** *(Quad-dominant = Push)*  
- **Lunges** *(Push)*  
- **Romanian Deadlifts (RDLs)** *(Hamstring-focused = Pull)*  
- **Leg Curls** *(Pure hamstring pull)*  
- **Calf Raises** *(Push, but often isolated)*  
  
---  
  
## **4. HOW TO USE THIS GUIDE FOR WORKOUT SPLITS**  
### **Option 1: Classic Push/Pull/Legs (PPL)**  
- **Push Day:** Bench Press, OHP, Tricep Dips  
- **Pull Day:** Pull-Ups, Rows, Bicep Curls  
- **Legs Day:** Squats, RDLs, Calf Raises  
  
### **Option 2: Upper (Push/Pull) + Lower Split**  
- **Upper Push:** Bench, OHP, Triceps  
- **Upper Pull:** Rows, Pull-Ups, Biceps  
- **Lower Body:** Squats, Deadlifts, Lunges  
  
### **Option 3: Movement-Based Split**  
- **Horizontal Push/Pull:** Bench + Rows  
- **Vertical Push/Pull:** OHP + Pull-Ups  
- **Legs Push/Pull:** Squats + RDLs  
  
---  
  
## **5. KEY TAKEAWAYS**  
✅ **Push Exercises** = Chest, Shoulders, Triceps  
✅ **Pull Exercises** = Back, Rear Delts, Biceps  
✅ **Legs** = Mostly push (squats), some pull (deadlifts)  
✅ **Balance Push/Pull** to avoid posture issues (e.g., rounded shoulders)

| **Tuesday** | Upper Pull | Pull-Ups, Rows, Biceps |

---  
  
### **Final Notes**  
- **If you’re into sports (BJJ, Muay Thai),** prioritize **pull exercises** (grip, back strength) and **explosive pushes** (overhead, dips).  
- **For bodybuilding,** ensure **equal push/pull volume** to avoid imbalances.
