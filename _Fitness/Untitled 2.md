**Core Principles Throughout:**
1.  **Stand Tall:** Chest open, shoulders down/back straight, head neutral (eyes forward/up), core  engaged, bring thighs high without bringing knees up to high.
2.  **Expressive & Fluid:** Move with purpose and rhythm, not rigidity.
3.  **Ankle Stiffness (Skipping/Running):** Actively dorsiflex foot (pull toes up towards shin) *just before* and *during* ground contact. Imagine landing on a "ball" under the forefoot/midfoot.
4.  **Controlled Landings:** Land softly, absorbing impact through muscles.

**The Warm-Up Sequence (10-15 mins):**

1.  **Skipping Foundation (2-3 mins):**
    *   **Focus:** Tall posture, head up, expressive arms, **active ankle dorsiflexion on landing.**
    *   **Cue:** "Land like you're stepping on a hot plate - quick, light, front of foot angled up (dorsiflexed)."
    *   **Execution:** Skip continuously, focusing on rhythm and form. Keep core engaged, back straight.

2.  **Dynamic Movement Prep (4-5 mins):** *Alternate 20-30m walks/jogs between exercises.*
    *   **Walking Lunges with Reach:** Step into lunge, drive *up* powerfully through the front heel, reaching both arms overhead tall. Keep torso upright. (Focuses on extension, hip flexor stretch).
    *   **Side Shuffles:** Stay low, quick steps. Add expressive "waving" arm drive across the body and up. Keep chest facing forward.
    *   **Backward Variations:**
        *   *Backward Walking:* Exaggerate knee lift and heel-to-butt action. Stay tall.
        *   *Backward Skipping:* Light, quick skips backward, focusing on forefoot contact and ankle stiffness.
    *   **Carioca (Grapevine):** Smooth cross-steps front and back. Keep hips/shoulders relatively square, use expressive arm swing for rhythm.

3.  **Mobility & Coordination (2-3 mins):**
    *   **Linear Leg Swings (forward/back):** Stand tall on one leg (hold wall if needed), swing other leg straight forward and back (like a pendulum). Stay on supporting toe. Emphasize controlled swing, not momentum. (Hip flexor/hamstring mobility).
    *   **Lateral Leg Swings (side-to-side):** Stand tall, swing one leg across the body and out to the side. Stay on supporting toe. (Hip abductor/adductor mobility).
    *   **High Knee Circles:** Standing tall, lift knee high and make controlled internal (towards midline) and external (away from midline) circles. (Hip mobility/control).
    *   **Internal/External Ankle Circles:** Draw circles with toes, keeping leg still (ankle mobility).

4.  **Plyometric Prep & Stability (2 mins):**
    *   **Single-Leg RDL w/ Knee Drive Swing:**
        1.  Stand tall on one leg, slight bend in standing knee.
        2.  Hinge at hips into a shallow Single-Leg RDL position, letting the non-standing leg extend straight back (optional slight bend).
        3.  *Explosively* swing the non-standing leg *forward* into a high knee drive position. **Hold the high knee position tall for 3 seconds** (balance challenge).
        4.  *Explosively* plant that foot forward into the next step/stance.
        5.  Alternate legs with each rep. Focus on controlled hinge, explosive drive, stable hold, and controlled landing.
        *   **Cue:** "Hinge - Swing & Hold Tall (3 sec) - Explode Step."

5.  **Ground Mobility (1 min - Hold each 15-20 sec per side):**
    *   **Scorpion Stretch:** Lie prone (face down), reach one arm out to side. Bend opposite knee, rotate hips/torso to try and touch bent foot to the floor near the outstretched hand. Keep shoulders down.
    *   **Eagle Arms (Seated/Standing):** Cross one arm over the other at the elbows, wrap forearms and press palms together (or backs of hands). Lift elbows slightly. (Shoulder/upper back mobility).

6.  **Explosive Upper Body (1 min):**
    *   **Elbow Push-ups (Pike Push-up Focus):** Start in a strong plank. Walk feet towards hands, lifting hips high into a deep pike position. Bend elbows *back* along your ribs (not flared out), lowering the top of your head towards the floor between hands. Push back up powerfully through the "pike" hinge. Keep core tight. (Focuses on shoulder strength/stability and explosive drive through upper back/shoulders).

at the 7:30 mark hinging up almost like pike


7.  **Rotational Core (1 min):**
    *   **Rotational Side Plank:** Start in a strong side plank on forearm, feet stacked or staggered. Extend the top arm straight up. Rotate the torso *under* the body, reaching the top arm down and "through" the space beneath you (like giving the floor a hug). Rotate back open. Keep hips high. Control the rotation with your core.

8.  **Free Flow & Cooldown (1-2 mins):**
    *   **Free Skipping:** Skip freely around the space, incorporating different arm swings, knee heights, tempos, directions. Be expressive! Focus on maintaining tall posture and active ankles.
    *   **Light Walk:** Gradually slow down to a walk, letting heart rate come down.

**Key Refinements & Explanations:**

1.  **Structured Progression:** Starts simple (Skipping), builds complexity and planes of motion (Lunges, Shuffles, Carioca, Backwards), adds mobility/stability (Swings, RDL Drill), includes ground work (Stretches), finishes with power (Push-ups, Rotation) and freedom.
2.  **Ankle Stiffness:** Explicitly linked dorsiflexion to the landing phase ("just before and during ground contact"). The "hot plate" cue reinforces quickness and stiffness. Leg swings done "on the toe" build ankle stability dynamically.
3.  **Posture Cues:** "Stand Tall", "Chest Open", "Head Neutral/Up", "Core Engaged" emphasized throughout.
4.  **Running Technique Integration:** The RDL w/ Knee Drive Swing drill directly trains the powerful hip hinge -> explosive knee drive sequence crucial for sprinting, with the hold emphasizing posture and balance. The contrast between skipping/jogging walks and the explosive elements highlights the posture difference.
5.  **Exercise Clarifications:**
    *   **Elbow Push-ups:** Framed as "Pike Push-up Focus" to clarify the intent and mechanics (hinging at hips, elbows back).
    *   **Rotational Side Plank:** Added "extend top arm up first" for clarity on the starting position of the rotation.
    *   **Single-Leg RDL w/ Knee Drive:** Added the critical "hold for 3 seconds" explicitly and emphasized the explosive transition and controlled landing. This is a key drill for your goals.
    *   **Free Skipping:** Placed at the end as a fun, expressive cooldown element where all the practiced elements can be integrated naturally.
6.  **Timing & Automation:** The structure provides consistent practice. Focusing on the cues (posture, ankles) *consciously* during the warm-up is how the body learns to do it *automatically* over time during sport.
7.  **Safety:** Landings emphasized as "soft" and "controlled". Leg swings controlled. Hinges (RDL, Lunges) emphasize core bracing and neutral spine.

**How to Use:**

*   Perform the sequence in order before your main workout.
*   Focus intensely on the form cues, especially posture and ankle stiffness during skipping/jogging/landing drills.
*   Adjust durations/reps based on your time and feel. Start shorter and build up.
*   Listen to your body. If something hurts, modify or skip it.
