For your **size (120 lbs), age (31), and BJJ focus**, here’s how to tailor your hip thrust max goals for **optimal performance** without overemphasizing raw weight:  
  
---  
  
### **1. The BJJ-Specific Strength Sweet Spot**  
- **Ideal Working Weight**: **1.5x bodyweight (180 lbs)** for 5 reps.  
- Why? This builds explosive bridging power for sweeps/escapes without unnecessary bulk.  
- **Max Goal**: **1.75–2x bodyweight (210–240 lbs)** for 1 rep.  
- Only test this occasionally—**BJJ benefits plateau past 2x BW**, and injury risk rises.  
  
#### *If You Gain Weight (e.g., 155 lbs):*  
- **New Ideal Working Weight**: 230–265 lbs (still 1.5–1.7x BW).  
- **Max Goal**: 300–315 lbs (~2x BW).  
  
---  
  
### **2. Why 240 lbs is a Solid Max (But Not Mandatory)**  
- **At 120 lbs**, 240 lbs is **2x bodyweight**—a respected strength benchmark for athletes.  
- **BJJ Reality**: Few techniques require *absolute* max strength; **speed and endurance matter more**.  
- Example: A 180-lb explosive thrust is more useful than a grinding 240-lb max.  
  
---  
  
### **3. Prioritize These Metrics Over Max Weight**  
| **Goal** | **How It Helps BJJ** | **How to Train It** |  
|-------------------|---------------------------------------|------------------------------------|  
| **Explosiveness** | Faster sweeps, stand-ups, scrambles | 50–60% max weight, **fast reps** |  
| **Endurance** | Guard retention, late-round hips | Bodyweight x 30+ reps or bands |  
| **Single-Leg Strength** | Asymmetrical fights (half-guard, lockdown) | Single-leg hip thrusts |  
  
---  
  
### **4. Sample Program (Mix Strength + Practical Power)**  
**Day 1 (Heavy)**  
- Barbell Hip Thrust: 4x5 @ 160–180 lbs (1.5x BW)  
- Banded Pulse Thrusts: 3x15 (explosive at top)  
  
**Day 2 (Endurance)**  
- Single-Leg Hip Thrust: 3x12/leg @ 40–50 lbs  
- Bodyweight Hip Thrust Holds: 3x30 sec (squeeze glutes)  
  
**Day 3 (Speed)**  
- Light Hip Thrust: 6x3 @ 90 lbs (**max speed**)  
- Kettlebell Swing (for hip snap): 4x15  
  
---  
  
### **5. When to Stop Chasing Weight**  
- **Diminishing Returns**: Past 2x BW, strength gains won’t translate linearly to BJJ.  
- **Risk/Reward**: Heavy loads can strain lumbar spine if form slips.  
  
**Better Focus**:  
- Add **bands/chains** to mimic resisting opponents.  
- Train **unstable surfaces** (e.g., Swiss ball) for guard recovery.  
  
---
