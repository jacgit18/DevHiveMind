### **Optimized Water & Meal Timing for 80 oz Daily Intake**

If you're drinking **80 oz of water per day** (four 20 oz bottles) and aiming to finish eating by **4:00 PM**, it's important to time your intake so it doesn’t interfere with digestion or make you feel too full before meals.

---

### **Best Order of Operations for Water & Meals**

**🏋️ Workout Days:** Drink **two bottles** before 4:00 PM.

#### **Morning (Before Breakfast)**

- **7:00 AM – First Bottle (20 oz):** Hydrate immediately after waking up.
- **7:30-8:00 AM – Breakfast:** Eat **30 minutes after water** to avoid feeling too full.
- **8:30-9:00 AM – Small Sips:** Light sipping is fine, but avoid another full bottle immediately after eating.

#### **Mid-Morning (Pre-Lunch Hydration)**

- **10:30 AM – Second Bottle (20 oz):** Drink about **1 hour before lunch** to stay hydrated without suppressing appetite.

#### **Lunch Timing**

- **11:30 AM – Lunch:** Eat once your stomach has had time to clear from water.

#### **Early Afternoon (Final Meals & Water Balance)**

- **1:00 PM – Third Bottle (20 oz):** Hydrate post-lunch while allowing digestion to settle before your last meal.
- **2:30-3:00 PM – Final Meal:** Finish eating while keeping room for your last bottle of water.
- **3:30-4:00 PM – Fourth Bottle (20 oz):** Complete your hydration goal without feeling too bloated before stopping food intake.

---

### **Key Tips to Avoid Feeling Too Full**

✅ Drink most of your water **between meals**, not with them.  
✅ Leave at least **30-60 minutes** between large water intakes and meals.  
✅ Sip **instead of chugging** during meals.  
✅ Adjust based on hunger—drink more if you feel light, space it out if feeling too full.
