Here’s a **Stability Ball Workout Plan** tailored specifically for **BJJ (Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu)** and **Swimming**, focusing on **core control**, **mobility**, **balance**, and **injury prevention**. Exercises are rated for **priority (1–5)** based on:

- Relevance to grappling or swimming mechanics
    
- Core and joint engagement
    
- Translational movement patterns
    

---

# **Stability Ball Workout – BJJ + Swimming Focus**

## **1. Core Stability & Control** _(Priority: 1 – Foundational for both sports)_

- **Stir the Pot (on forearms)** – **1**
    
- **Ball Pike (feet on ball)** – **1**
    
- **Dead Bug with Ball Press (ball between arms & knees)** – **1**
    
- **Ball Rollouts (knees or toes on ground)** – **2**
    
- **Plank + Knee Tucks (feet on ball)** – **2**
    

---

## **2. Dynamic Hip & Shoulder Mobility** _(Priority: 2 – For guard work, transitions, and swimming reach)_

- **Wall-Supported Stability Ball Arm Circles** – **1** _(great for shoulder health)_
    
- **Bridges with Shoulders on Ball + Marching** – **2**
    
- **Hip Circles on Ball (seated, hands off)** – **2**
    
- **Swiss Ball Scapular Push-ups** – **3**
    

---

## **3. Grappling Simulation & Positional Control** _(Priority: 2 for BJJ)_

- **Mount Control Drills (knees on ball, hold position)** – **1**
    
- **Side Control Pressure Drill (chest on ball, hip drop)** – **2**
    
- **Post and Base Transitions (hands/feet on floor, ball under hips)** – **3**
    

---

## **4. Rotational Core & Balance** _(Priority: 3 – For takedowns, sweeps, and turns in water)_

- **Russian Twists (feet elevated or on ground)** – **2**
    
- **Diagonal Ball Slams (with or without resistance)** – **3**
    
- **Single-Arm Chest Pass (kneeling on ball)** – **3**
    
- **Kneeling Ball Balance + Partner Pushes** – **4**
    

---

## **5. Recovery, Breathing & Control** _(Priority: 4 – Nervous system reset and lung control)_

- **Backbend Relaxation Over Ball** – **2**
    
- **Crocodile Breathing Prone on Ball** – **2**
    
- **Side-Lying Thoracic Rotations on Ball** – **3**
    

---

## **6. Swimming-Specific Shoulder & Core Integration** _(Priority: 2 – Helps streamline and stroke mechanics)_

- **Prone Swimmers on Ball (Y-T-W holds)** – **1**
    
- **Superman Reach (alternating arms/legs, hips on ball)** – **2**
    
- **Flutter Kicks with Hips on Ball + Arm Swims** – **2**
    

---

## **7. BJJ-Specific Anti-Rotation & Base Work** _(Priority: 2–3 – Core reactions to pressure)_

- **Bird Dog with Knee on Ball** – **2**
    
- **Plank Elbow Taps with Feet on Ball** – **3**
    
- **Standing Shin Trap with Ball Against Wall (simulate balance in guard)** – **3**
