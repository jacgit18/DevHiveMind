### **Cable Machine Leg Exercises for Kicks**

#### **1. Cable Hip Flexion (Front Kick / Knee Drive)**

- _Mimics:_ Teep kick, knee strikes, guard retention in BJJ
    
- **How:** Attach ankle strap, drive knee forward and up
    
- **Variation:** Slow eccentric for control
    

#### **2. Cable Hip Extension (Back Kick / Base Stability)**

- _Mimics:_ Rear leg drive, base-building in clinch or stand-ups
    
- **How:** Kick leg straight back, squeeze glutes
    
- **Focus:** Don’t overarch the back
    

#### **3. Cable Side Kicks (Lateral Hip Abduction)**

- _Mimics:_ Side kick, roundhouse chamber, guard recovery
    
- **How:** Face the anchor, kick leg out to the side
    
- **Variation:** Perform slow for control, fast for explosiveness
    

#### **4. Cable Roundhouse Chamber (Rotational Hip Raise)**

- _Mimics:_ Thai roundhouse wind-up and control
    
- **How:** Lift knee, rotate hip externally like prepping a roundhouse
    
- **Control phase:** Focus on decelerating the return
    

#### **5. Cable Adduction (Inner Thigh Pull-In)**

- _Mimics:_ Base recovery in BJJ, leg pummeling, sweeping
    
- **How:** Pull leg across body inward against resistance
    
- **Variation:** Superset with abduction for hip balance
    

#### **6. Cable Step-Through Lunges (Forward/Rear)**

- _Mimics:_ Weight transfers during kicks, explosive transitions in scrambles
    
- **How:** Anchor behind you, step through under tension
    
- **Bonus:** Engages core and balance
    

#### **7. Cable Bulgarian Split Squats**

- _Mimics:_ Single-leg power for rear-leg kicks and takedown defense
    
- **How:** Back foot elevated, band or cable in front or to side
    
- **Focus:** Drive through front heel
    

#### **8. Cable Knee Circle (Hip Mobility Drill)**

- _Mimics:_ Knee shield movement, kick chambering and recovery
    
- **How:** Move knee in a wide circular motion under tension
    
- **Great for:** Warm-ups or cooldowns
