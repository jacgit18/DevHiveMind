Here’s a **calisthenics regimen tailored for a martial artist** training in **BJJ, Judo, and Muay Thai**, progressing from **beginner to advanced**. It focuses on **functional strength, mobility, core stability, and explosive power**—all vital for grappling and striking.

---

### **GENERAL GUIDELINES:**

- **Frequency:** 3x/week on non-consecutive days (or light versions on same days as martial arts if needed)
    
- **Warm-Up (every session):**
    
    - Jump rope – 2 min
        
    - Arm circles, hip circles – 1 min each
        
    - Dynamic lunges with rotation – 10 reps
        
    - Scapular push-ups – 10 reps
        

---

## **BEGINNER PHASE (4–6 weeks)**

**Goal:** Build base strength, joint stability, and control

**Workout A – Full Body Focus**

- Push-ups – 3x8-10
    
- Bodyweight Squats – 3x15
    
- Dead Bug (core stability) – 3x10
    
- Hollow hold – 3x20 sec
    
- Plank shoulder taps – 3x10/side
    

**Workout B – Pull + Mobility Focus**

- Australian Rows (low bar or rings) – 3x8
    
- Glute Bridges – 3x12
    
- Superman Hold – 3x20 sec
    
- Cat-Cow to Downward Dog – 3x5
    
- Wall angels – 3x10
    

**Optional Active Recovery:**

- Controlled hip openers
    
- Crawling patterns (bear crawl, crab walk)
    

---

## **INTERMEDIATE PHASE (4–6 weeks)**

**Goal:** Increase core strength, introduce explosiveness, unilateral strength

**Workout A – Upper Body & Core**

- Archer push-ups or diamond push-ups – 3x6-8
    
- Pike push-ups – 3x6
    
- Hanging leg raises (or lying leg raises) – 3x10
    
- Plank reach outs – 3x10
    
- Band pull-aparts or doorway rows – 3x15
    

**Workout B – Lower Body & Explosiveness**

- Bulgarian split squats – 3x8/leg
    
- Jump squats – 3x10
    
- Wall sits – 3x30 sec
    
- Single-leg glute bridge – 3x8/leg
    
- Lateral lunges – 3x8/side
    

**Add mini finishers** (e.g., 3 rounds of: 10 push-ups, 10 squats, 20 mountain climbers)

---

## **ADVANCED PHASE (Ongoing)**

**Goal:** Explosive athleticism, control under fatigue, advanced core + pulling strength

**Workout A – Explosive Upper Body**

- Clapping push-ups – 3x6
    
- Handstand push-ups (wall-assisted) – 3x3-5
    
- Archer rows (on rings or bar) – 3x6/side
    
- L-sit hold (parallettes or floor) – 3x10-15 sec
    
- Hanging windshield wipers – 3x8
    

**Workout B – Lower Body Power & Balance**

- Pistol squats – 3x6/leg
    
- Broad jumps – 3x5
    
- Jump lunges – 3x10
    
- Wall sit with punches – 3x30 sec
    
- Calf raises – 3x20
    

**Optional Core Finisher:**

- 3 Rounds:
    
    - Hollow hold – 20 sec
        
    - Side plank with reach-under – 10/side
        
    - Flutter kicks – 20 reps
        

---

### **BONUS: Grappler + Striker-Specific Additions**

- **Grip work**: Towel hangs or gi pull-ups
    
- **Neck strength**: Neck bridges (carefully), band resisted nods
    
- **Rotational core**: Russian twists, side-to-side med ball slams (if using equipment)
    
- **Anti-rotation**: Pallof press (band or partner resisted)
