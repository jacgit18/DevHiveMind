## **Onion Selection Framework**

**Step 1 — Decide the Role of the Onion in the Dish**

- **Base Flavor (long cooking)** → You want it to dissolve into the background.
    
- **Supporting Role (medium cooking)** → You want it to be noticeable but not overpowering.
    
- **Raw/Garnish** → You want crisp texture and specific bite or sweetness.
    

---

### **1. Shallots**

- **Profile:** Mild, slightly sweet, a little garlicky, small bulbs.
    
- **Best When:** You want subtle flavor and a touch of elegance without overpowering.
    
- **Use In:**
    
    - **Dressings & vinaigrettes** (finely minced raw)
        
    - **Pan sauces** (sautéed, then deglazed with wine or stock)
        
    - **Delicate dishes** (fish, omelets, French food)
        
- **Framework Rule:** _Pick shallots when subtlety > punch._
    

---

### **2. Yellow Onions** (standard cooking onion)

- **Profile:** Balanced sweetness and sulfur, gets sweeter with cooking.
    
- **Best When:** All-purpose — the default choice for most savory dishes.
    
- **Use In:**
    
    - **Soups, stews, braises** (caramelizes well)
        
    - **Sauté bases** (with celery, carrots, garlic, etc.)
        
    - **Roasts** (tucked around meat)
        
- **Framework Rule:** _When in doubt, go yellow._
    

---

### **3. Sweet Onions** (Vidalia, Walla Walla, Maui)

- **Profile:** High sugar, low sulfur → very sweet, mild bite.
    
- **Best When:** You want onion flavor without sharpness, even raw.
    
- **Use In:**
    
    - **Raw** (sandwiches, burgers, salads)
        
    - **Quick sautés** (stir-fries, fajitas)
        
    - **Grilling** (thick slices caramelize beautifully)
        
- **Framework Rule:** _Pick sweet onions when “mild and juicy” matters._
    

---

### **4. Red Onions**

- **Profile:** Sharp raw, slightly sweet when cooked, color adds appeal.
    
- **Best When:** You care about both flavor _and_ visual impact.
    
- **Use In:**
    
    - **Raw** (salsas, salads, pickled)
        
    - **Quick-cooked** (pizzas, flatbreads)
        
    - **Mediterranean & Latin dishes** (especially fresh toppings)
        
- **Framework Rule:** _Use red when you want color contrast or crisp raw punch._
    

---

### **5. White Onions**

- **Profile:** Sharper than yellow, cleaner flavor, higher water content.
    
- **Best When:** Crisp, bright onion flavor is part of the cuisine’s signature.
    
- **Use In:**
    
    - **Mexican** (pico de gallo, tacos, salsas)
        
    - **Southeast Asian** (fresh salads, stir-fries)
        
    - **Light soups** (chicken tortilla, pho)
        
- **Framework Rule:** _Use white when the cuisine wants onion freshness to stand out._
