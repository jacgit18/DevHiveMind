### **The Ultimate Adaptable Chili Recipe**

This recipe yields a hearty, medium-spiced, bean-inclusive chili. The instructions guide you through building layers of flavor.

**Yields:** 6-8 servings
**Prep time:** 15 minutes
**Cook time:** 1 hour 15 minutes (mostly hands-off)

---

#### **Ingredients**

**The Aromatics & Base:**
*   2 tbsp vegetable or olive oil
*   1 large yellow onion, diced
*   Carrot
*   Celery
*   1 large bell pepper (any color), diced
*  1 Bay leaf
*   **4 cloves garlic,** minced

**The Spice Blend:**
*   chili powder - MAKE IT YOURSELF - toast it in pan also 
	*  1 tbsp ground cumin
	*   1 tsp smoked paprika
	*   1 tsp Coriander 
	*   1 tsp dried oregano
	*   **½ - 1 tsp cayenne pepper** (adjust for heat level)
	*   ½ tsp black pepper
*   1 tsp salt, plus more to taste


**The Protein & Tomatoes:**
*   **2 lbs ground beef** (80/20 lean/fat is ideal)
*   1 (12 oz) bottle or can of beer, water, or beef broth *(deglazing liquid)*
*   1 (28 oz) can crushed tomatoes
*   2 tbsp tomato paste

**The Beans & Final Touch:**(exclude to stay out of bathroom)
*   2 (15 oz) cans beans (kidney, pinto, or black), drained and rinsed
*   **1-2 tbsp brown sugar** or maple syrup *(to balance acidity)*

**For Serving:**
*   Sour cream, shredded cheese, diced red onion, cilantro, corn chips, etc.

---

#### **Instructions**

1.  **Sauté Aromatics:** In a large Dutch oven or heavy-bottomed pot, heat the oil over medium heat. Add the onion and bell pepper and cook until softened, about 5-7 minutes. Add the garlic and cook for 1 more minute until fragrant.
2.  **Bloom Spices:** Push the vegetables to the side. Add the spice blend (chili powder, cumin, paprika, oregano, cayenne, salt, pepper) to the center of the pot. Toast for 30-60 seconds, stirring constantly, until very fragrant.
3.  **Brown the Meat:** Add the ground beef to the pot, breaking it up with a spoon. Cook until no longer pink, about 8-10 minutes. **Drain excess fat** if desired (for a less greasy chili).
4.  **Deglaze:** Pour in the beer (or substitute). Scrape the bottom of the pot with your spoon to release all the browned bits (this is flavor!).
5.  **Simmer:** Add the crushed tomatoes, tomato sauce, diced tomatoes, and tomato paste. Stir well to combine. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low. Cover and simmer for **at least 45 minutes**, stirring occasionally (1.5+ hours is even better for deeper flavor).
6.  **Final Adjustments:** Stir in the drained beans and brown sugar. Simmer, uncovered, for another 15-20 minutes. Taste and adjust seasoning—add more salt, cumin, or a pinch of cayenne if needed.
7.  **Serve:** Ladle into bowls and top with your favorite garnishes.

### **The Ultimate Chili Substitution Guide**

Use this guide to customize your chili based on dietary needs, what's in your pantry, or just to try something new.

#### **1. Protein Substitutes**
*   **Ground Beef:** The standard. For leaner chili, use **90/10** or ground sirloin. For richer chili, use **ground chuck**.
*   **Ground Turkey or Chicken:** A leaner option. **Add 1 extra tbsp of oil** when sautéing aromatics to compensate for less fat.
*   **Vegetarian/Vegan:** Use **2-3 cans of beans** (mix types) or **1 lb of plant-based ground "meat"** (add it in step 5 to avoid overcooking). **1 cup of dried lentils** (brown or green) also works great—add them in step 5 with an extra ½ cup of liquid.
*   **Shortcut:** Use **1-2 lbs of pre-cooked shredded beef or pork** (like from a roast or slow cooker). Add it in step 5 to heat through.

#### **2. Bean Substitutes**
*   **Kidney Beans:** Classic choice, holds shape well.
*   **Pinto Beans:** Creamier texture, classic in "Texas-style" chili (though purists argue no beans at all!).
*   **Black Beans:** Slightly sweeter, earthier flavor.
*   **No Beans?** Simply omit them for a meat-centric chili. You may want to add an extra ½ lb of meat.
*   **Using Dried Beans:** Substitute 1 cup of **dried beans** for one 15 oz can. **You must soak and fully cook them separately** before adding in step 6.

#### **3. Liquid & Tomato Substitutes**
*   **Beer:** Adds depth and malty notes. A lager or ale works well. **Non-alcoholic sub:** equal amount of beef broth.
*   **Beef Broth:** Standard and always delicious.
*   **Coffee or Strong Espresso:** Adds a rich, robust, smoky depth without a coffee taste.
*   **Crushed Tomatoes:** Can sub with an additional can of **diced tomatoes** blended briefly, or more **tomato sauce**.
*   **Tomato-Free:** For a unique twist, use **pureed roasted red peppers** instead of tomato sauce.

#### **4. Spice & Heat Level Substitutes**
*   **Chili Powder:** A blend is standard. For more control, use **2 tbsp ground ancho chili powder** + **1 tsp paprika** + **½ tsp garlic powder** + **½ tsp oregano**.
*   **For Smokiness:** Use **chipotle chili powder** or add **1-2 canned chipotle peppers in adobo sauce** (minced). Smoked paprika is already in the recipe for this.
*   **Milder Chili:** Omit the cayenne pepper entirely. Use **ancho chili powder** (which is mild and sweet).
*   **Spicier Chili:** Add **1-2 minced jalapeños or serrano peppers** with the onions. Use **hot chili powder** or add **½ tsp of crushed red pepper flakes** with the spices.

#### **5. Flavor Boosters (Secret Ingredients!)**
Add any of these with the tomatoes in step 5:
*   **Umami/Bass Notes:** 1 tbsp **soy sauce** or **Worcestershire sauce** (adds saltiness too).
*   **Sweetness/Depth:** 1 square of **dark chocolate** or 1 tbsp **unsweetened cocoa powder**.
*   **Acidity/Brightness:** A splash of **apple cider vinegar** or the juice of **½ a lime** stirred in at the very end.
*   **Earthiness:** 1 tsp **instant coffee granules** or **espresso powder**.
