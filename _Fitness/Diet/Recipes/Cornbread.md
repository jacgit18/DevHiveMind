Of course! Here is a classic, highly-rated cornbread recipe that yields a deliciously moist and slightly sweet cornbread with a perfect crumbly texture and a crispy, golden crust.

  

Classic Skillet Cornbread

  

This recipe is designed for a standard 10-inch cast iron skillet, but a 9x9 inch baking pan will also work.

  

Ingredients:

  

· 1 ½ cups cornmeal (yellow or white)

· 1 cup all-purpose flour

· ⅔ cup granulated sugar (reduce to 1/3 cup for a less sweet cornbread)

· 1 tablespoon baking powder

· 1 teaspoon salt

· 1 ¼ cups whole milk (or buttermilk for a tangier flavor)

· 2 large eggs, lightly beaten

· ½ cup (1 stick) unsalted butter, melted, plus extra for greasing

· 1 tablespoon vegetable oil or bacon fat (for the skillet)

  

Instructions:

  

1. Preheat and Prepare: Place your 10-inch cast iron skillet in the oven and preheat the oven to 400°F (200°C). This step is key for a crispy crust.

2. Mix Dry Ingredients: In a large bowl, whisk together the cornmeal, flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt.

3. Mix Wet Ingredients: In another bowl, whisk the beaten eggs, milk, and melted butter until well combined.

4. Combine: Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients. Stir with a spatula until just combined. Do not overmix; a few lumps are perfectly fine. Overmixing leads to tough cornbread. Let the batter rest for 5 minutes while the oven finishes preheating.

5. Prepare the Hot Skillet: Carefully remove the hot skillet from the oven using oven mitts. Add the tablespoon of oil or bacon fat and swirl it around to coat the bottom and sides of the skillet.

6. Bake: Immediately pour the batter into the hot, greased skillet. It should sizzle slightly. Smooth the top with your spatula.

7. Bake: Place the skillet back in the oven and bake for 20-25 minutes, or until the top is golden brown and a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.

8. Cool and Serve: Let the cornbread cool in the skillet for at least 10-15 minutes before slicing and serving. It's best served warm with a pat of butter and a drizzle of honey.

  

---

  

Recipe Variations & Tips:

  

· Buttermilk Version: For a more traditional, tangy flavor, substitute the whole milk for 1 ¼ cups of buttermilk. If you use buttermilk, add ½ teaspoon of baking soda to the dry ingredients. The acid in the buttermilk reacts with the baking soda, making the bread extra light and fluffy.

· Jalapeño Cheddar: Fold in 1 cup of shredded sharp cheddar cheese and 1-2 finely diced jalapeños (seeds removed for less heat) into the batter just before pouring it into the skillet.

· Add Corn: For extra texture and sweetness, fold in 1 cup of fresh, canned (drained), or frozen (thawed) corn kernels into the batter.

· No Cast Iron Skillet? No problem! Grease a 9x9 inch baking pan or a standard muffin tin (fill cups 2/3 full, bake for 15-18 mins). You don't need to preheat the pan.

· The Resting Step: Letting the batter rest for 5-10 minutes allows the cornmeal to hydrate, resulting in a more tender final product.

· Storage: Store leftover cornbread in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 2 days, or in the refrigerator for up to a week. It can also be frozen for up to 3 months.

  

Enjoy your homemade cornbread
