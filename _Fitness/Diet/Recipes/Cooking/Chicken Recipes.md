#### **1. Grilled Lemon Herb Chicken**

- **Calories**: ~350-400 calories per serving
- **Ingredients**:
    - 4 chicken breasts
    - 2 tbsp olive oil
    - Juice and zest of 1 lemon
    - 2 cloves garlic, minced
    - 1 tbsp fresh parsley, chopped
    - Salt and pepper to taste
- **Instructions**:
    - Marinate chicken breasts in olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, parsley, salt, and pepper for at least 30 minutes.
    - Grill over medium heat for 6-7 minutes per side, or until fully cooked.


#### **2. Chicken Fajitas**

- **Calories**: ~400-500 calories per serving
- **Ingredients**:
    - 2 chicken breasts, sliced thin
    - 1 onion, sliced
    - 1 bell pepper, sliced
    - 1 tbsp olive oil
    - 1 tbsp fajita seasoning (or homemade)
    - Flour or corn tortillas
    - Toppings: Sour cream, salsa, guacamole
- **Instructions**:
    - Heat olive oil in a skillet over medium-high heat. Add chicken, seasoning, onion, and bell pepper.
    - Cook until the chicken is browned and vegetables are tender.
    - Serve with tortillas and desired toppings.


#### **3. Baked Chicken Parmesan**

- **Calories**: ~500-600 calories per serving
- **Ingredients**:
    - 4 chicken breasts
    - 1 cup breadcrumbs (or gluten-free)
    - 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese
    - 1 egg
    - 1 cup marinara sauce
    - 1 cup mozzarella cheese, shredded
    - Olive oil for greasing
- **Instructions**:
    - Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
    - Dip chicken breasts in beaten egg, then coat in a mixture of breadcrumbs and Parmesan.
    - Place on a greased baking sheet and bake for 25-30 minutes, until cooked through.
    - Top with marinara sauce and mozzarella cheese. Bake for an additional 5-10 minutes, until cheese melts.


#### **4. Chicken Caesar Salad**

- **Calories**: ~400-500 calories per serving
- **Ingredients**:
    - 2 chicken breasts, grilled and sliced
    - 4 cups romaine lettuce, chopped
    - 1/4 cup Caesar dressing
    - 1/4 cup croutons
    - 1/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese
- **Instructions**:
    - Grill chicken breasts and slice them thinly.
    - Toss lettuce with Caesar dressing, croutons, and Parmesan cheese.
    - Top with sliced chicken.


#### **5. Chicken Stir-Fry**

- **Calories**: ~350-450 calories per serving
- **Ingredients**:
    - 2 chicken breasts, sliced
    - 2 cups mixed vegetables (broccoli, bell pepper, carrots, etc.)
    - 2 tbsp soy sauce or coconut aminos
    - 1 tbsp sesame oil
    - 1 tbsp honey
    - 1 tsp ginger, grated
- **Instructions**:
    - Heat sesame oil in a pan and cook chicken until browned.
    - Add vegetables, soy sauce, honey, and ginger. Stir-fry for 5-7 minutes, until vegetables are tender.
