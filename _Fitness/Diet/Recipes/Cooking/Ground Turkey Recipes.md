#### **1. Ground Turkey Tacos**

- **Calories**: ~300-400 calories per serving (2 tacos)
- **Ingredients**:
    - 1 lb ground turkey
    - 1 packet taco seasoning
    - 1/4 cup water
    - Soft or hard taco shells
    - Toppings: Lettuce, cheese, salsa, sour cream
- **Instructions**:
    - Brown ground turkey in a skillet. Add taco seasoning and water, simmer for 5-7 minutes.
    - Serve in taco shells with your favorite toppings.


#### **2. Ground Turkey Meatballs**

- **Calories**: ~300-400 calories per serving (4 meatballs)
- **Ingredients**:
    - 1 lb ground turkey
    - 1/4 cup breadcrumbs (or almond flour for gluten-free)
    - 1 egg
    - 1/4 cup Parmesan cheese, grated
    - 1 tsp dried oregano
    - 1/2 tsp garlic powder
    - Salt and pepper to taste
- **Instructions**:
    - Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
    - Mix all ingredients together and form into meatballs.
    - Bake for 20-25 minutes or until cooked through.


#### **3. Ground Turkey Chili**

- **Calories**: ~400-500 calories per serving
- **Ingredients**:
    - 1 lb ground turkey
    - 1 can diced tomatoes
    - 1 can kidney beans, drained
    - 1 can black beans, drained
    - 1 onion, chopped
    - 1 tbsp chili powder
    - 1 tsp cumin
    - Salt and pepper to taste
- **Instructions**:
    - Brown ground turkey in a large pot.
    - Add chopped onion and cook until soft.
    - Stir in tomatoes, beans, and spices. Simmer for 30-40 minutes.


#### **4. Ground Turkey Stuffed Peppers**

- **Calories**: ~350-450 calories per serving (1 stuffed pepper)
- **Ingredients**:
    - 4 bell peppers, tops removed and seeds scooped out
    - 1 lb ground turkey
    - 1 cup cooked rice or quinoa
    - 1 cup marinara sauce
    - 1/4 cup grated cheese (optional)
    - Salt and pepper to taste
- **Instructions**:
    - Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
    - Brown ground turkey in a skillet. Add rice, marinara sauce, and seasonings.
    - Stuff peppers with the turkey mixture and place in a baking dish.
    - Bake for 25-30 minutes, then top with cheese (if desired) and bake for an additional 5-10 minutes.


#### **5. Ground Turkey Lettuce Wraps**

- **Calories**: ~250-350 calories per serving (3 wraps)
- **Ingredients**:
    - 1 lb ground turkey
    - 1/4 cup soy sauce or coconut aminos
    - 1 tbsp hoisin sauce
    - 1 tbsp rice vinegar
    - 1/4 cup chopped green onions
    - Lettuce leaves (e.g., butter lettuce or iceberg)
- **Instructions**:
    - Cook ground turkey in a skillet until browned.
    - Stir in soy sauce, hoisin sauce, and rice vinegar. Cook for an additional 2-3 minutes.
    - Spoon the mixture into lettuce leaves and top with green onions.


#### **6. Ground Turkey Bolognese**

- **Calories**: ~450-600 calories per serving
- **Ingredients**:
    - 1 lb ground turkey
    - 1 onion, chopped
    - 2 cloves garlic, minced
    - 1 can crushed tomatoes
    - 1 tsp dried basil
    - 1 tsp dried oregano
    - 1 tbsp olive oil
    - 1 lb pasta (gluten-free or regular)
    - Parmesan cheese for serving
- **Instructions**:
    - Heat olive oil in a pan, then add onion and garlic. Cook until softened.
    - Add ground turkey and cook until browned.
    - Stir in crushed tomatoes, basil, oregano, salt, and pepper. Simmer for 20-30 minutes.
    - Serve over cooked pasta and top with Parmesan.


#### **7. Ground Turkey and Vegetable Skillet**

- **Calories**: ~350-450 calories per serving
- **Ingredients**:
    - 1 lb ground turkey
    - 1 zucchini, chopped
    - 1 bell pepper, chopped
    - 1/2 onion, chopped
    - 2 tbsp olive oil
    - 1 tbsp Italian seasoning
    - Salt and pepper to taste
- **Instructions**:
    - Heat olive oil in a skillet over medium heat.
    - Add ground turkey and cook until browned.
    - Add vegetables and Italian seasoning. Cook for 5-7 minutes until tender.
