### **Red Bean Filling (for Cuapao or other steamed buns)**

#### **Ingredients for the Filling:**
- **1 cup dried red beans (azuki beans or pat in Korean)**
- **¼ cup dark brown sugar** (or white or light brown sugar, depending on preference)
- **¼ cup rice syrup** (or substitute with honey or maple syrup for a different sweetness)
- **¼ teaspoon kosher salt**
- **2 teaspoons vanilla extract**

#### **Instructions for the Filling:**
1. **Cook the Red Beans**:
    - Rinse the dried red beans thoroughly and place them in a large pot. Add enough water to cover the beans by about 2 inches. Bring to a boil, then reduce the heat and simmer for about 60-90 minutes, or until the beans are tender. Add more water as needed to prevent burning.
    - Once cooked, drain any excess water and let the beans cool.

2. **Prepare the Sweetened Filling**:
    - In a saucepan, combine the cooked beans, brown sugar, rice syrup, and salt. Stir over medium heat until the sugar is fully dissolved and the mixture thickens slightly. You can mash some of the beans for a smoother filling, or leave them partially mashed for a chunkier texture, depending on your preference.
    - Once the mixture reaches your desired consistency, remove from heat and stir in the vanilla extract.
    - Let the filling cool to room temperature before using it.

### **Dough Options:**

#### **Option 1: Make Your Own Dough (for steamed buns)**
- **Ingredients for the Dough**:
    - 2 cups all-purpose flour
    - 1 tablespoon sugar
    - 1 teaspoon instant yeast
    - ½ teaspoon baking powder
    - ½ teaspoon kosher salt
    - ¾ cup warm water
    - 2 tablespoons vegetable oil

- **Instructions**:
    1. Combine the flour, sugar, yeast, baking powder, and salt in a large mixing bowl.
    2. Gradually add the warm water and oil, stirring to form a dough.
    3. Knead the dough for 10-12 minutes until smooth and elastic. Add more flour if the dough is too sticky.
    4. Cover the dough with a damp cloth and let it rise for 1-1.5 hours or until doubled in size.
    5. Punch the dough down, divide it into small portions, and roll each portion into a ball. Roll each ball into a flat circle, then place a spoonful of red bean filling in the center. Pinch the dough around the filling to form a bun.
    6. Steam the buns for 10-15 minutes, or until cooked through.

#### **Option 2: Store-Bought Dough (Cuapao or Other Dough)**
- If you prefer convenience, you can purchase **Cuapao dough** (Chinese steamed bun dough) or any other pre-made dough used for steamed buns.
- Simply thaw and use according to the package instructions, then proceed with filling the dough with the prepared red bean filling. Steam as directed on the package.

---

### **Assembly:**

1. Roll out the dough into small discs (if using homemade dough).
2. Place a spoonful of the red bean filling in the center of each disc.
3. Pinch the dough together to seal the filling inside.
4. Steam the buns for about 10-15 minutes, or until the dough is soft and cooked through.

### **Calories per Serving:**

For the **red bean filling**, it's roughly **100-120 calories per serving** (assuming 8-10 servings). The dough will add more calories depending on the type used, but for homemade dough, it’s typically **150-200 calories per bun**.
