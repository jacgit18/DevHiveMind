### **1. Ground Beef Tacos**

- **Calories**: ~300-400 per serving (2 tacos)
- **Ingredients**:
    - 1 lb ground beef
    - 1 packet taco seasoning (or homemade)
    - 1/4 cup water
    - Taco shells (soft or hard)
    - Toppings: lettuce, cheese, salsa, sour cream
- **Instructions**:
    - Brown ground beef in a skillet. Add taco seasoning and water. Simmer for 5-7 minutes.
    - Serve in taco shells with your favorite toppings.

### **2. Meatball Sub Sandwiches**

- **Calories**: ~400-500 per sandwich
- **Ingredients**:
    - 1 lb ground beef
    - 1/2 cup breadcrumbs
    - 1/4 cup Parmesan cheese
    - 1 egg
    - 1 tsp garlic powder
    - Salt and pepper to taste
    - 1 cup marinara sauce
    - 4 sub rolls
    - 1 cup mozzarella cheese
- **Instructions**:
    - Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C).
    - Mix ground beef, breadcrumbs, Parmesan, egg, garlic, salt, and pepper. Form into meatballs.
    - Place on a baking sheet and bake for 20-25 minutes.
    - Heat marinara sauce in a pan. Add meatballs and cook for 5 minutes.
    - Toast sub rolls, top with meatballs, marinara, and mozzarella cheese. Serve hot.

### **3. Ground Beef Stir-Fry**

- **Calories**: ~350-450 per serving
- **Ingredients**:
    - 1 lb ground beef
    - 2 cups mixed vegetables (carrots, bell peppers, broccoli)
    - 2 tbsp soy sauce (or coconut aminos)
    - 1 tbsp sesame oil
    - 1 tbsp rice vinegar
    - 1 tsp ginger, grated
- **Instructions**:
    - Heat sesame oil in a pan. Brown ground beef.
    - Add vegetables, soy sauce, rice vinegar, and ginger. Stir-fry for 5-7 minutes.
    - Serve over rice or noodles.

### **4. Ground Beef Chili**

- **Calories**: ~400-500 per serving
- **Ingredients**:
    - 1 lb ground beef
    - 1 can kidney beans, drained
    - 1 can diced tomatoes
    - 1 onion, chopped
    - 1 tbsp chili powder
    - 1 tsp cumin
    - Salt and pepper to taste
- **Instructions**:
    - Brown the ground beef with onions in a large pot.
    - Add kidney beans, tomatoes, chili powder, cumin, salt, and pepper. Simmer for 30 minutes to an hour.
    - Serve with cornbread or over rice.

### **5. Stuffed Bell Peppers**

- **Calories**: ~350-450 per stuffed pepper
- **Ingredients**:
    - 1 lb ground beef
    - 4 large bell peppers, tops cut off and seeds removed
    - 1 cup cooked rice (or quinoa)
    - 1 cup marinara sauce
    - 1/4 cup grated cheese (optional)
    - Salt and pepper to taste
- **Instructions**:
    - Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C).
    - Brown the ground beef in a skillet. Mix with cooked rice, marinara sauce, salt, and pepper.
    - Stuff the bell peppers with the meat mixture and place in a baking dish.
    - Bake for 25-30 minutes, topping with cheese during the last 5 minutes.

### **6. Ground Beef and Potato Skillet**

- **Calories**: ~400-500 per serving
- **Ingredients**:
    - 1 lb ground beef
    - 2 large potatoes, diced
    - 1 onion, chopped
    - 1 tsp garlic powder
    - 1/2 tsp paprika
    - Salt and pepper to taste
- **Instructions**:
    - Heat oil in a skillet and cook diced potatoes until golden and tender.
    - Remove potatoes and set aside. In the same skillet, brown ground beef, adding onion, garlic powder, paprika, salt, and pepper.
    - Combine potatoes and meat mixture and cook for another 5-7 minutes.

### **7. Meatloaf**

- **Calories**: ~300-400 per slice
- **Ingredients**:
    - 1 lb ground beef
    - 1/2 cup breadcrumbs
    - 1 egg
    - 1/2 cup milk
    - 1/4 cup ketchup (plus extra for topping)
    - 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
    - 1/2 tsp garlic powder
    - Salt and pepper to taste
- **Instructions**:
    - Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C).
    - Mix all ingredients together and form into a loaf.
    - Place on a greased baking sheet and bake for 45-60 minutes, topping with extra ketchup in the last 10 minutes of baking.

### **8. Ground Beef Bolognese**

- **Calories**: ~450-600 per serving
- **Ingredients**:
    - 1 lb ground beef
    - 1 onion, chopped
    - 2 cloves garlic, minced
    - 1 can crushed tomatoes
    - 1 tsp dried basil
    - 1 tsp dried oregano
    - Salt and pepper to taste
    - 1 lb pasta (spaghetti or preferred)
- **Instructions**:
    - Brown ground beef in a pan. Add onions and garlic, cook until soft.
    - Add crushed tomatoes, basil, oregano, salt, and pepper. Simmer for 20-30 minutes.
    - Cook pasta and serve with sauce on top. Sprinkle with Parmesan cheese.

### **9. Ground Beef Sliders**

- **Calories**: ~250-300 per slider
- **Ingredients**:
    - 1 lb ground beef
    - 1 egg
    - 1/4 cup breadcrumbs
    - 1 tsp garlic powder
    - 1/4 tsp black pepper
    - Slider buns
    - Toppings: cheese, pickles, lettuce, tomato
- **Instructions**:
    - Preheat a grill or stovetop pan.
    - Mix ground beef, egg, breadcrumbs, garlic powder, and pepper. Form into small patties.
    - Grill or cook patties for 4-5 minutes on each side.
    - Assemble sliders with your choice of cheese, pickles, lettuce, and tomato.

### **10. Beef and Vegetable Soup**

- **Calories**: ~300-400 per serving
- **Ingredients**:
    - 1 lb ground beef
    - 1 onion, chopped
    - 2 carrots, chopped
    - 2 potatoes, diced
    - 1 can diced tomatoes
    - 4 cups beef broth
    - 1 tsp dried thyme
    - Salt and pepper to taste
- **Instructions**:
    - Brown ground beef with onions in a pot.
    - Add carrots, potatoes, diced tomatoes, beef broth, thyme, salt, and pepper.
    - Simmer for 30-45 minutes until vegetables are tender.
