**Pastelón** is a delicious Puerto Rican dish often referred to as a "plantain lasagna." It layers sweet, ripe plantains with seasoned ground meat, typically ground beef, and cheese. Here’s a classic **Pastelón recipe**:

### **Pastelón (Puerto Rican Plantain Casserole)**

**Calories**: ~400-500 per serving (1 slice)

**Ingredients**:

- 3-4 ripe plantains (yellow with black spots)
- 1 lb ground beef
- 1/2 onion, chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1/2 bell pepper, chopped
- 1/2 cup tomato sauce
- 1/4 cup olives, sliced (optional)
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp oregano
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 1/2 tsp adobo seasoning (optional)
- 1/2 cup shredded cheddar cheese (or a mix of mozzarella and cheddar)
- 2 tbsp olive oil (for frying plantains)
- 1/4 cup capers (optional)
- 2 large eggs (for binding)

---

**Instructions**:

1. **Prepare the Plantains**:
    
    - Peel the plantains by cutting off both ends and scoring the peel along the length. Carefully remove the peel.
    - Slice the plantains into long, thin strips (about 1/4 inch thick).
    - Heat olive oil in a skillet over medium heat.
    - Fry the plantain slices until golden brown on both sides, about 2-3 minutes per side.
    - Set the fried plantains aside on paper towels to drain excess oil.
2. **Prepare the Ground Beef Filling**:
    
    - In the same skillet, sauté the chopped onion, garlic, and bell pepper until softened, about 3-4 minutes.
    - Add the ground beef to the pan and cook until browned, breaking it up with a spoon.
    - Season the meat with cumin, oregano, salt, pepper, and adobo seasoning if using.
    - Stir in the tomato sauce and olives, simmering for another 5-7 minutes. If using capers, stir them in as well. Taste for seasoning and adjust if necessary.
3. **Assemble the Pastelón**:
    
    - Preheat your oven to 375°F (190°C).
    - In a greased baking dish (9x9 or a similar size), layer the plantain slices along the bottom of the dish, overlapping them slightly to form a base layer.
    - Spread half of the ground beef mixture evenly on top of the plantains.
    - Add another layer of fried plantains on top of the beef, and then add the remaining beef mixture.
    - Finish with a final layer of plantains on top.
4. **Add the Egg and Cheese**:
    
    - In a small bowl, beat the eggs and pour them over the top layer of plantains to bind everything together.
    - Sprinkle shredded cheese evenly over the top.
5. **Bake**:
    
    - Bake the pastelón in the preheated oven for 25-30 minutes or until the top is golden brown and the cheese is melted and bubbly.
6. **Serve**:
    
    - Let the pastelón cool for a few minutes before slicing. Serve warm.
