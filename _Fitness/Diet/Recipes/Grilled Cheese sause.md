The cilantro sauce, which is described as the condiment that made the Fort Greene grilled cheese special, is prepared with a variety of fresh greens, lime, and other creamy ingredients.

Here are the core components and key preparation techniques:

**Core Components (Ingredients):**

- **Cilantro:** Three bunches, which need thorough cleaning and drying.
- **Scallions:** Two bunches, with root ends removed.
- **Chives:** Two bunches.
- **Jalapeños:** Two jalapeños, with the amount of ribs and seeds adjusted based on desired spiciness. or Poblano pepper.
- **Limes:** Three limes, aiming for about three-quarters to one cup of lime juice; it's important to select soft, juicy limes for better yield.
- **Avocado Mayo:** Half a cup.
- **Agave:** Amount to balance the lime.
- **Avocado Oil:** Half a cup to one cup.
- **Salt:** To taste.
- **Avocado (Optional):** The recipe stopped before adding avocado, but the creator likes to add it for more green, flavor, and creaminess.

**Key Preparation Techniques:**

1. **Clean and Prepare Greens:**
    
    - **Cilantro:** Cut off root ends, keep a lot of the stem for flavor, and then soak and rinse multiple times in a salad spinner base until the water is clear. After rinsing, dry it thoroughly in the salad spinner.
    - **Scallions and Chives:** Wash scallions, remove root ends, and give them a rough chop. Chop chives into small pieces to facilitate blending.
    - **Jalapeños:** Adjust spiciness by including more ribs and seeds for a spicier sauce, or just use the flesh for a milder one.
    - **Limes:** Squeeze for juice, aiming for a significant amount (3/4 to 1 cup).
2. **Blending Process:**
    
    - **Keep Blade Cold:** The blender blade should be kept nice and cold (e.g., in the freezer) to prevent the sauce from overheating during blending.
    - **Start with Lime Juice:** Add about **half of the lime juice first** to the blender, allowing for acidity adjustment later.
    - **Puree Greens:** Add all the prepared green vegetables (cilantro, scallions, chives, jalapeños) to the blender with the initial lime juice. **Puree and break down the greens**; they will reduce significantly in volume.
    - **Add Remaining Ingredients:** Once the greens are pureed, add the remaining lime juice (if desired), avocado oil, agave, avocado mayo, and salt. Optionally, add avocado for extra creaminess and flavor.
3. **Adjusting and Balancing Flavor:**
    
    - The sauce should be **heavy on cilantro, have a nice lime flavor (not overpowering), be spicy, and creamy**.
    - **Adjust acidity** by adding more lime juice or mellowing it out with more agave if it's too limey.
    - Adjust salt and sweetness as needed.
    - After blending, let the sauce "marry" or sit to allow flavors to combine.
