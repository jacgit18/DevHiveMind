Here's a revised version of the high-calorie French toast recipes with average calorie counts for each ingredient, including calories per slice of whole-grain bread:

---

### **1. Peanut Butter and Honey French Toast**

- **2 slices of whole-grain or sprouted bread** (~160 calories per slice)
- **2 large eggs** (~140 calories)
- **1/4 cup milk** (whole, almond, or oat) (~40-50 calories)
- **2 tbsp natural peanut butter** (~180 calories)
- **1 tbsp honey** (~60 calories)
- **1 tbsp chopped roasted peanuts (optional)** (~50 calories)
- **1 tsp cinnamon** (~5 calories)

**Total per serving**: ~635-655 calories
