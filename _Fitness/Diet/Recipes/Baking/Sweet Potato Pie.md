### Ingredients:

- **Sweet Potato**: 2 medium (about 2 cups mashed)
- **Turmeric**: 1/2 teaspoon
- **Ginger**: 1 teaspoon (fresh or ground)
- **Lemon**: 1 tablespoon (juice)
- **Nutmeg**: 1/2 teaspoon
- **Cinnamon**: 1 teaspoon
- **Butter (or coconut oil)**: 2 tablespoons (use plant-based butter for a dairy-free version)
- **Almond or Coconut Milk**: 1/2 cup (unsweetened)
- **Eggs**: 3 large or **Flax Seeds**: 3 tablespoons (ground flax mixed with 9 tablespoons water for a flax egg substitute)
- **Salt**: 1/4 teaspoon
- **Sugar**: 1/2 cup (use coconut sugar or maple syrup for a healthier option)
- **Flour**: 1/2 cup (whole wheat or almond flour as a healthier alternative)
- **Vanilla Extract**: 1 teaspoon

### Instructions:

1. Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C).
2. Boil or bake the sweet potatoes until soft, then mash them.
3. In a mixing bowl, combine the mashed sweet potatoes with turmeric, ginger, lemon juice, nutmeg, cinnamon, and salt. Mix well.
4. In another bowl, whisk together the butter (or coconut oil), milk, eggs (or flax eggs), sugar, flour, and vanilla extract.
5. Combine both mixtures and pour into a pie crust (pre-made or homemade with a healthier flour, such as almond flour).
6. Bake for 45-50 minutes until the filling is set and a toothpick comes out clean.
7. Let the pie cool before slicing.

### Approximate Calorie Count (per slice, assuming 8 slices):

- **Sweet Potato**: ~100 calories
- **Butter (2 tbsp)**: ~200 calories
- **Almond Milk (1/2 cup)**: ~15 calories
- **Eggs (3 large)**: ~210 calories (or flax eggs ~60 calories)
- **Sugar (1/2 cup)**: ~400 calories (can reduce with alternatives)
- **Flour (1/2 cup)**: ~200 calories (if using almond flour, around 300 calories)
- **Spices, vanilla, and salt**: Negligible calories

**Total for the pie**: ~1125-1225 calories

**Calories per slice (8 slices)**: ~140-160 calories per slice (with alternatives like flax eggs and coconut sugar).

Feel free to adjust the sweetness or spice levels based on preference, and you can reduce calories further by swapping the sugar or using a lighter crust option!
