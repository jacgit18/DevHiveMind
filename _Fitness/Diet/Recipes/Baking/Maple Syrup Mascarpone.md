**Maple Syrup Mascarpone**  
A deliciously rich and creamy topping or filling that combines mascarpone cheese and maple syrup. It’s perfect for desserts, breakfast dishes, or even as a dip. Here’s a quick recipe to make it:

### Ingredients:
- 1 cup mascarpone cheese  
- 2–3 tablespoons pure maple syrup (adjust to taste)  
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract (optional)  

### Instructions:
1. In a bowl, combine the mascarpone cheese and maple syrup.  
2. Gently fold the ingredients together until smooth and creamy. Avoid overmixing to maintain the mascarpone’s texture.  
3. Taste and adjust the sweetness by adding more maple syrup, if needed.  
4. Add the vanilla extract if you want a hint of extra flavor.  
5. Serve immediately or refrigerate until ready to use.  

### Uses:
- Spread on pancakes, waffles, or crepes.  
- Dollop on fresh fruit or berries.  
- Use as a frosting or filling for cakes and pastries.  
- Serve with scones or toast.
