**Ingredients:**

- **Flour**: Use whole wheat flour, almond flour, or oat flour for a healthier alternative.
- **Sugar**: Substitute with honey, maple syrup, or a sugar alternative like stevia or monk fruit sweetener.
- **Baking powder**: 1 teaspoon
- **Baking soda**: A pinch
- **Pink salt**: A pinch
- **Cacao**: Unsweetened cacao powder (for added antioxidants)
- **Butter/Oil**: Use coconut oil, avocado oil, or a plant-based butter for a healthier fat source.
- **Milk**: Use almond milk, oat milk, or another plant-based milk for fewer calories.
- **Blueberries/Other fruit**: Fresh or frozen blueberries (or any berries or diced apple).
- **Oatmeal**: Rolled oats (or use oat flour for more texture).
- **Flax seeds**: Ground flax seeds (for omega-3s and fiber).

**Instructions:**

1. In a microwave-safe mug, mix the dry ingredients: flour, baking powder, baking soda, pink salt, cacao, and oatmeal.
2. Add wet ingredients: butter/oil, milk, and any sweetener you're using.
3. Stir in blueberries or your choice of fruit and flax seeds.
4. Microwave for 1-2 minutes or until set in the center.
5. Let cool for a few minutes before eating.

**Approximate calorie count (per serving, using almond flour, almond milk, and honey)**:

- 1 microwave muffin: **250-300 calories** (depending on specific brands used and portion sizes).

You can adjust ingredients based on your preferences, but this version provides a healthier, lower-sugar muffin with healthy fats and fiber!
