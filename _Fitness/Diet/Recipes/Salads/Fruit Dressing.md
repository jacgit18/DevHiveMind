### **Overview: How Fruit Dressings Work**

The base of most fruit dressings is **fruit puree**. This acts as both a flavoring and a natural thickener, helping the dressing emulsify and cling to your greens beautifully. The general formula is:

**Pureed Fruit + Acid + Oil + Sweetener (if needed) + Seasonings**

---

### **1. Classic Fresh Strawberry Vinaigrette**

This dressing is fresh, slightly sweet, and brilliantly pink. It's perfect for spring and summer salads.

**Ingredients:**
*   1 cup fresh strawberries, hulled and chopped
*   ¼ cup extra virgin olive oil
*   2 tbsp apple cider vinegar or white balsamic vinegar
*   1 tbsp honey or maple syrup (adjust to taste)
*   1 tbsp fresh lemon juice
*   1 small shallot, minced (about 1 tbsp)
*   Salt and freshly cracked black pepper to taste
*   *Optional: ½ tsp poppy seeds*

**Instructions:**
1.  **Puree:** In a blender or food processor, combine the chopped strawberries, vinegar, honey, and lemon juice. Blend until completely smooth.
2.  **Emulsify:** With the blender running on low, slowly drizzle in the olive oil until the dressing is smooth and well combined.
3.  **Season:** Add the minced shallot, salt, and pepper. Pulse a few times to incorporate. If using, stir in the poppy seeds at the end.
4.  **Taste and Adjust:** Taste the dressing. You may want to add more honey for sweetness, more vinegar for tang, or more salt to balance the flavors.
5.  **Store:** Transfer to an airtight jar and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes to allow the flavors to meld. Use within 3-4 days.

**Perfect For:** Spinach salad with goat cheese, toasted almonds, and red onion.

### **2. Creamy Raspberry Dressing**

This versatile dressing can be made creamy with yogurt or kept light as a vinaigrette. Raspberries provide a gorgeous color and a tangy flavor.

**Ingredients (Creamy Version):**
*   1 cup fresh or thawed frozen raspberries
*   ¼ cup plain Greek yogurt
*   2 tbsp olive oil
*   1 tbsp balsamic vinegar or red wine vinegar
*   1-2 tbsp honey or maple syrup
*   Salt and pepper to taste

**Instructions (Creamy Version):**
1.  **Strain (Optional):** For a seed-free, smooth dressing, push the raspberries through a fine-mesh sieve to remove the seeds. This step is optional but recommended for a more refined texture.
2.  **Combine:** In a blender, combine the seedless raspberry puree (or whole raspberries), Greek yogurt, vinegar, and honey. Blend until smooth.
3.  **Emulsify:** With the blender running, slowly drizzle in the olive oil.
4.  **Season:** Add salt and pepper to taste. Blend once more to combine.
5.  **Chill and Serve:** Store in a jar in the fridge. The yogurt will make it thicker; you can thin it with a teaspoon of water or lemon juice if desired.

**Vinaigrette Variation:** Omit the Greek yogurt. Use 1/2 cup raspberry puree, 1/4 cup olive oil, 2 tbsp vinegar, and 1 tbsp honey. Blend until smooth.

**Perfect For:** A salad with mixed greens, grilled chicken, pecans, and goat cheese.

### **3. Citrus-Pomegranate Vinaigrette**

This dressing is a jewel-toned, tart, and sophisticated option. Using pomegranate juice is a great shortcut for big flavor.

**Ingredients:**
*   ½ cup 100% pomegranate juice
*   2 tbsp fresh orange juice
*   1 tbsp fresh lemon juice
*   ⅓ cup extra virgin olive oil
*   1 tbsp honey or maple syrup
*   1 tsp Dijon mustard (helps with emulsification)
*   Salt and pepper to taste
*   *Optional: 1 small garlic clove, minced*

**Instructions:**
1.  **Reduce Juice (Optional but Recommended):** In a small saucepan, bring the pomegranate juice to a simmer over medium heat. Let it simmer for 5-10 minutes until it reduces by about half to a syrupy consistency. This intensifies the flavor and sweetness. Let it cool completely.
2.  **Combine:** In a jar with a tight-fitting lid, combine the cooled reduced pomegranate juice (or straight juice), orange juice, lemon juice, honey, and Dijon mustard. Shake vigorously until combined.
3.  **Emulsify:** Add the olive oil, salt, and pepper. Shake again until the dressing is fully emulsified.
4.  **Store:** Refrigerate. The dressing will keep for up to a week.

**Perfect For:** A festive holiday salad with arugula, roasted butternut squash, feta, and pomegranate arils.

### **4. Tropical Mango-Lime Vinaigrette**

Sweet, tangy, and vibrant, this dressing brings a taste of the tropics to your bowl.

**Ingredients:**
*   1 cup fresh or thawed frozen mango chunks
*   3 tbsp neutral oil (avocado, grapeseed) or light olive oil
*   2 tbsp fresh lime juice
*   1 tbsp rice vinegar
*   1 tsp fresh ginger, grated
*   1 tsp honey or agave (optional, depending on mango sweetness)
*   Pinch of salt
*   *Optional: 1 tbsp fresh cilantro*

**Instructions:**
1.  **Puree:** Combine mango, lime juice, rice vinegar, ginger, and honey (if using) in a blender. Blend until completely smooth.
2.  **Emulsify:** With the blender running, slowly drizzle in the oil until the dressing is creamy.
3.  **Season:** Add the cilantro (if using) and a pinch of salt. Pulse to combine.
4.  **Store:** Keep in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 4 days.

**Perfect For:** A salad with romaine, grilled shrimp or fish, avocado, red bell pepper, and toasted coconut flakes.

### **Pro Tips for Fruit-Based Dressings:**

*   **Sweetness Varies:** The natural sweetness of your fruit will vary. **Always taste your dressing** before sealing it and adjust the sweetener (honey/maple) and acid (vinegar/lemon) to your liking.
*   **Texture:** For a super-smooth dressing, straining berry seeds (like from raspberries or blackberries) is worth the effort.
*   **Thickness:** Dressings will thicken when chilled. If it becomes too thick, let it sit at room temperature for 15 minutes or shake in a teaspoon of warm water to loosen it up.
*   **Herb Pairings:**
    *   **Berries:** Mint, basil, thyme
    *   **Citrus:** Cilantro, dill, tarragon
    *   **Tropical Fruits:** Cilantro, mint
