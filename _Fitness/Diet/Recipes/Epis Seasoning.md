### **Ingredients for Haitian Seasoning (Epis):**

- **2 tablespoons fresh thyme** (or 1 tablespoon dried thyme)
- **3-4 scallions** (also known as green onions)
- **4-5 garlic cloves** (peeled)
- **1 small bell pepper** (preferably red or green)
- **1-2 hot peppers** (like Scotch bonnet or habanero, adjust to taste for heat)
- **2 tablespoons fresh parsley**
- **2 tablespoons fresh cilantro**
- **1/2 teaspoon black pepper** (adjust to taste)
- **2-3 tablespoons olive oil** (or your preferred cooking oil, like vegetable or coconut oil)

### **Instructions:**

1. **Prepare the Ingredients**:
    
    - Wash all herbs and vegetables (scallions, parsley, cilantro, thyme).
    - Chop the scallions and bell pepper into smaller pieces to make blending easier.
    - Peel the garlic cloves.
    - Remove the stems and seeds from the hot pepper (be sure to wear gloves or wash your hands thoroughly afterward if handling hot peppers).
2. **Blend the Seasoning**:
    
    - In a food processor or blender, combine the thyme, scallions, garlic, bell pepper, hot pepper, parsley, cilantro, and black pepper.
    - Add the olive oil and blend until smooth. You may need to scrape down the sides a few times.
    - If the mixture is too thick, add a little more oil or a small amount of water to reach a consistency you like. The goal is to have a thick, paste-like consistency.
3. **Store the Seasoning**:
    
    - Transfer the Haitian seasoning to an airtight container or jar. It can be refrigerated for up to 1-2 weeks or frozen for longer storage.

### **Usage**:

- Use Haitian seasoning as a marinade for meats, fish, or tofu.
- Add it to soups, stews, rice, or beans for a burst of flavor.
- It’s a key component in many Haitian dishes, including _Griot_ (fried pork), _Poulet aux Pois_ (chicken with peas), or even as a seasoning for vegetables.

### **Tips**:

- You can adjust the amount of hot pepper based on your spice tolerance.
- Some variations of _Epis_ include adding vinegar or lime juice for extra acidity, but it’s optional.
- This seasoning can also be mixed with a little water to create a flavorful base for cooking.

Epis (Haitian seasoning) is a deeply flavorful, all-purpose seasoning base that’s used throughout Haitian cooking to infuse dishes with bold, herbal, and slightly spicy notes. Here are **great ways to use it**:

---

### 🔥 **Best Uses for Haitian Epis**

#### 🍗 **1. As a Meat Marinade**

- **Chicken, pork, goat, or beef**: Rub generously and let it marinate for a few hours or overnight.
    
- Especially great for **griot (fried pork)** and **tassot (fried goat or beef)**.
    

#### 🍲 **2. In Stews and Braises**

- Stir a spoonful into **legume (vegetable stew)**, **beef stew**, or **soup joumou** to deepen the flavor.
    
- Add to **sauce pwa** (bean sauce) for a richer base.
    

#### 🍚 **3. In Rice and Beans**

- Use it as the base flavoring when cooking **diri kole** (rice with beans) or **diri jon jon** (black mushroom rice).
    
- Sauté it in oil first before adding rice and liquids.
    

#### 🍳 **4. With Eggs or Tofu**

- Mix a small spoon into **scrambled eggs** or a **tofu scramble** for a flavorful kick.
    

#### 🥬 **5. In Vegetable Dishes**

- Add to sautéed **spinach, okra, or eggplant** to build layers of flavor.
    

#### 🍝 **6. As a Sofrito Substitute**

- Works just like **sofrito** or **green seasoning** in other Caribbean/Latin American cuisines. Use in **spaghetti**, soups, and stir-fries.
    

#### 🍖 **7. Grilling or Roasting**

- Coat meats or vegetables before **grilling or roasting**. It forms a great crust and aroma when heat hits it.
    

#### 🥄 **8. Condiment or Spread (if blended smoother)**

- Mix with a bit of vinegar or citrus for a zesty **table sauce** or **sandwich spread**.
    
- Combine with mayo or sour cream for a flavorful dip.
    

---

### 💡 Pro Tips:

- **Batch it**: Make a big batch and freeze in ice cube trays for easy use.
    
- **Adjust heat**: Use more or fewer hot peppers depending on the dish.
    
- **Oil acts as a preservative**, so a bit more oil can help it last longer in the fridge.
