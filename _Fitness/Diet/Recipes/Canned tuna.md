### 🧂 **1. Boost Flavor & Moisture (Base Mix-Ins)**

   - **Creamy Binders:**  

     → **Mayo** (classic), **Greek yogurt/kiefer** (tangy/protein boost), **avocado** (creamy/healthy fats), **hummus** (earthy), **cottage cheese** (high-protein).  

     *Tip: Mix 2-3 tbsp per 5oz can.*  

   - **Acids (cuts fishiness, adds brightness):**  

     → Lemon/lime juice, apple cider vinegar, pickle juice, or capers + brine.  

   - **Umami Bombs:**  

     → **Dijon mustard**, **Worcestershire sauce**, **soy sauce/tamari**, **fish sauce** (tiny splash!), **miso paste**, **anchovy paste**.

  

### 🧅 **2. Add Freshness & Crunch**

   - **Aromatics:**  

     → **Red onion** (finely diced), **shallots**, **scallions**, **chives**, or **fresh herbs** (dill, parsley, cilantro).  

   - **Crisp Veggies:**  

     → **Celery** (classic crunch), **cucumber** (drained well), **bell peppers**, **jicama**, or **shredded carrots**.  

   - **Pickled/Briny Items:**  

     → **Relish** (dill or sweet), **pickles** (diced), **capers**, **olives** (kalamata/green), **pepperoncini**.

  

### 🌶️ **3. Spice & Heat (Wake Up Blandness)**

   - **Spices:**  

     → **Old Bay**, **cajun seasoning**, **smoked paprika**, **curry powder**, **everything bagel seasoning**.  

   - **Heat Sources:**  

     → **Hot sauce** (Sriracha, Cholula), **red pepper flakes**, **fresh jalapeños**, **harissa paste**, **wasabi**.

  

### 🥜 **4. Richness & Texture Contrast**

   - **Nuts/Seeds:**  

     → Toasted **almonds**, **walnuts**, **pecans**, **sunflower seeds**, or **sesame seeds**.  

   - **Sweetness (balances salt/fishiness):**  

     → **Diced apples**, **grapes**, **dried cranberries**, or **pineapple tidbits** (drained well).  

   - **Savory Add-Ins:**  

     → **Chopped hard-boiled eggs**, **crispy bacon**, **furikake** (seaweed seasoning).

  

### 🥪 **5. Next-Level Combos (Mix & Match!)**

   - **Mediterranean:**  

     → Lemon juice + olive oil + kalamata olives + red onion + fresh dill + feta.  

   - **Spicy Thai:**  

     → Lime juice + Sriracha + soy sauce + shredded carrot + cilantro + peanuts.  

   - **Curry Waldorf:**  

     → Greek yogurt + curry powder + diced apple + celery + raisins.  

   - **Avocado-Lime:**  

     → Mashed avocado + lime juice + red onion + cilantro + cumin.

  

### 🚫 **Avoid Over-Masking!**

   - **Drain tuna well** (press with a fork to remove excess liquid).  

   - **Taste as you mix**—start with 1-2 bold flavors (e.g., lemon + dill), then build.  

   - **Chill for 30+ minutes** before serving (lets flavors meld).

  

### 💡 **Pro Tip:**  

If your tuna tastes *overly* fishy, **rinse it under cold water** after draining, then pat dry. This removes surface compounds causing the odor.

  

**Example Quick Fix:**  

> *5oz tuna + 2 tbsp Greek yogurt + 1 tsp Dijon + 1 tbsp lemon juice + ¼ cup diced celery + 2 tbsp red onion + salt/pepper + dill.*  

  

Pair with crackers, in a sandwich, on greens, or stuffed in a tomato! 🥪🍋
