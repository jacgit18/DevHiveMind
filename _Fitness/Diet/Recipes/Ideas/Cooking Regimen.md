### **Meal Prep Efficiency**

To optimize the cooking process, streamline your meal prep by batch cooking and using a few key tools:

#### **Batch Cooking Tips:**

1. **Cook Protein in Bulk**: Grill or bake chicken, turkey, or other lean meats in large batches and store them in the fridge or freezer. You can reheat these throughout the week. Aim to cook 2-3 days’ worth of chicken at a time.
    
2. **Rice & Quinoa**: Cook a large batch of rice and quinoa at the start of the week. Store them in airtight containers and reheat as needed. This saves you from cooking grains every day.
    
3. **Vegetable Prep**: Chop vegetables for stir-fries, salads, and snacks ahead of time. Store them in containers in the fridge for quick access.
    
4. **Sweet Potatoes & Plantains**: Roast or sauté these in bulk. They can last up to 3 days in the fridge and are easy to reheat.
    
5. **Eggs**: Hard-boil eggs for quick breakfasts or snacks. Alternatively, make scrambled or frittata-style eggs in large batches and store in the fridge.
    

---

### **Time-Saving Tools**

- **Instant Pot** or **Pressure Cooker**: Use this to cook grains, beans, and meats faster without compromising on flavor or nutrition. It's perfect for batch cooking.
    
- **Food Processor**: Great for chopping vegetables quickly, making dressings, or preparing smoothies.
    
- **High-Powered Blender**: For making protein shakes or smoothies, it ensures a smooth, creamy consistency that will help you add extra calories quickly.
    
- **Stovetop or Countertop Grill**: If you have a grill pan or George Foreman-style grill, you can cook meat quickly without needing a full oven or stove.
    

---

### **Streamlining the Cooking Process**

- **Plan for Leftovers**: Make enough for lunch or dinner the next day. Cooking a bit extra ensures no extra cooking time is wasted, especially for meals like stir-fries, grains, or meat.
    
- **Simplify Cooking**: Focus on recipes that are one-pan or one-pot to reduce cleanup time. Dishes like stir-fries, sheet-pan meals, and casseroles work best for this.
    
- **Use Pre-Prepared Ingredients**: If possible, buy pre-chopped vegetables or pre-cooked grains to save time. The goal is to make the cooking process as simple as possible while still achieving your calorie and protein goals.
    

---

### **Sustainable Cooking Routine**

- **Meal Prep Once or Twice a Week**: Allocate a block of time (around 2-3 hours) every Sunday and Wednesday for meal prep. Focus on cooking proteins, grains, and vegetables in bulk.
    
- **Flexible Schedule**: Since you’re eating within a 5 am to 4 pm window, aim for 3 larger meals and 2 snacks. Spread meals out within that time frame to optimize calorie intake.
    
- **Keep Snacks Ready**: Having high-calorie snacks like protein bars, Greek yogurt, and nut butter on hand will help you stay on track when you're busy.
    

---

By following this plan, you'll have a simple, efficient routine for optimizing your weight gain while eating foods that fit your preferences and dietary restrictions. The key to success is consistency and preparing enough food in advance so you’re not spending too much time cooking each day.
