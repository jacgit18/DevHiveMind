## **Versatile Vegetable Pasta Sauce Template**

**Core Vegetable Base**

- **Main vegetable** (e.g., spinach, kale, broccoli, zucchini, peas) – blanched or lightly cooked.
    

**Liquid & Thickener**

- **Pasta cooking water** (contains starch) – reserve ~½–1 cup.
    
- Optional: **Cornstarch slurry** (1–2 tsp cornstarch mixed with cold water) for extra thickening.
    

**Seasoning & Acid**

- Salt – to taste.
    
- Black pepper – to taste.
    
- Lemon juice – 1–2 tsp (or substitute with vinegar or white wine for a different profile).
    

**Fat Component**

- Olive oil, butter, or other fat (e.g., ghee, bacon fat for smoky depth).
    

**Aromatics**

- Garlic – 3 cloves, minced (can be roasted for milder flavor).
    

**Cheese Options (100 g total)**

- Parmesan, pecorino, or whatever’s available.
    
- Optional blends: asiago, Gouda, Swiss, Emmental.
    

**Base Method**

1. **Prepare vegetable** – blanch, steam, or sauté until just tender.
    
2. **Blend or mash** vegetable with aromatics, cheese, fat, seasoning, and a splash of pasta water until smooth (or leave chunky for rustic style).
    
3. **Adjust texture** with more pasta water or cornstarch slurry.
    
4. **Heat through** in a pan, add cooked pasta, toss until coated, serve.
    

**Variations**

- **Add-ins:** roasted nuts, chili flakes, fresh herbs, sautéed mushrooms.
    
- **Protein boost:** shredded chicken, beans, pancetta, or crispy tofu.
    
- **Flavor swaps:** swap lemon for balsamic vinegar, or add miso paste for umami.
