## **1. Béchamel (White Sauce)**  
**Ingredients:**  
- 2 tbsp butter  
- 2 tbsp flour  
- 2 cups milk (warmed)  
- Salt, white pepper, nutmeg (optional)  

**Steps:**  
1. Melt butter in a saucepan over medium heat.  
2. Whisk in flour to form a roux (cook 1-2 min, no browning).  
3. Gradually add warm milk, whisking constantly until smooth.  
4. Simmer 5-10 min until thickened. Season.  

**Best Used For:**  
- Mac & cheese, lasagna, croque monsieur, creamy gratins.

## **2. Soubise (Onion Béchamel)**  
**Ingredients:**  
- 1 cup Béchamel  
- 2 large onions (thinly sliced, sweated until soft)  
- Butter, salt, white pepper  

**Steps:**  
1. Cook onions in butter until soft (no color).  
2. Blend with Béchamel until smooth.  

**Best Used For:**  
- Accompaniment to roasted meats, eggs, or as a creamy base for fish.

## **3. Velouté (Blond Sauce)**  
**Ingredients:**  
- 2 tbsp butter  
- 2 tbsp flour  
- 2 cups light stock (chicken, fish, or veal)  

**Steps:**  
1. Make a blond roux (butter + flour, cook 2 min).  
2. Gradually whisk in hot stock.  
3. Simmer 15-20 min until velvety.  

**Best Used For:**  
- Base for creamy soups, chicken pot pie, or gravy.  

---

## **4. Herb Velouté**  
**Ingredients:**  
- 1 cup Velouté  
- 2 tbsp fresh herbs (parsley, tarragon, chives)  

**Steps:**  
1. Prepare Velouté.  
2. Stir in finely chopped herbs before serving.  

**Best Used For:**  
- Fish dishes, poached chicken, vegetable accompaniments.

## **5. Espagnole (Brown Sauce)**  
**Ingredients:**  
- 2 tbsp butter  
- 2 tbsp flour  
- 2 cups brown stock (beef or veal)  
- 1 tbsp tomato paste  
- Mirepoix (onions, carrots, celery)  

**Steps:**  
1. Make a dark roux (cook until chocolate-colored).  
2. Add tomato paste and mirepoix, cook 2 min.  
3. Deglaze with stock, simmer 1+ hour, strain.  

**Best Used For:**  
- Demi-glace, beef dishes, braised meats.

## **6. Sauce à L’Orange (Orange Sauce)**  
**Ingredients:**  
- 1 cup Espagnole or demi-glace  
- 1 cup orange juice  
- Zest of 1 orange  
- 1 tbsp sugar (optional)  

**Steps:**  
1. Reduce orange juice by half.  
2. Add Espagnole, simmer 10 min.  
3. Finish with zest.  

**Best Used For:**  
- Duck à l’orange, roasted poultry.

## **7. Hollandaise (Emulsified Butter Sauce)**  
**Ingredients:**  
- 3 egg yolks  
- 1 tbsp lemon juice  
- 1/2 cup melted butter  
- Salt, cayenne  

**Steps:**  
1. Whisk yolks + lemon over double boiler until thick.  
2. Slowly drizzle in melted butter while whisking.  
3. Season.  

**Best Used For:**  
- Eggs Benedict, asparagus, fish.  

---

## **8. Béarnaise (Herb Hollandaise)**  
**Ingredients:**  
- Hollandaise base  
- 1 tbsp tarragon  
- 1 tbsp shallots (minced)  
- 1 tsp white wine vinegar  

**Steps:**  
1. Reduce vinegar + shallots, strain.  
2. Add to Hollandaise with tarragon.  

**Best Used For:**  
- Steak, grilled meats, seafood.  

---

## **9. Tomato Sauce (Classic Red Sauce)**  
**Ingredients:**  
- 2 cups tomatoes (canned or fresh)  
- 1 onion, 2 garlic cloves  
- Olive oil, basil, salt  

**Steps:**  
1. Sauté onions + garlic.  
2. Add tomatoes, simmer 30+ min.  
3. Blend if smoothness desired.  

**Best Used For:**  
- Pasta, pizza, meatballs.  

---

## **10. Romesco (Spanish Nut & Pepper Sauce)**  
**Ingredients:**  
- 2 roasted red peppers  
- 1/4 cup almonds/hazelnuts  
- 1 garlic clove  
- 2 tbsp olive oil  
- 1 slice bread (toasted)  

**Steps:**  
1. Blend all ingredients until chunky-smooth.  

**Best Used For:**  
- Grilled fish, vegetables, bread dip.

### **Quick Guide: When to Use Which Sauce**  
| **Sauce**       | **Best Paired With**                | **Texture/Flavor**        |  
|----------------|----------------------------------|-------------------------|  
| **Béchamel**  | Pasta bakes, creamy dishes      | Rich, velvety          |  
| **Soubise**   | Roasted meats, eggs             | Sweet, onion-forward   |  
| **Velouté**   | Chicken, fish, light proteins   | Smooth, delicate       |  
| **Espagnole** | Beef, braises                   | Deep, umami            |  
| **Hollandaise** | Eggs, veggies                  | Buttery, tangy         |  
| **Béarnaise** | Steak, grilled meats            | Herbaceous, rich       |  
| **Tomato**    | Pasta, pizza                    | Bright, acidic         |  
| **Romesco**   | Seafood, grilled veg            | Nutty, smoky           |  

---

### **Final Tips:**  
- **For beginners**: Start with **Béchamel** or **Tomato**—they’re forgiving.  
- **For elegance**: **Hollandaise/Béarnaise** (great for brunch or steak).  
- **For depth**: **Espagnole** (ideal for French-inspired meat dishes).
