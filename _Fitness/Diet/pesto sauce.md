Making **pesto sauce** is quick, easy, and requires just a few fresh ingredients. Here’s a classic **basil pesto recipe** (with variations for dietary preferences):

  

---

  

### **Classic Basil Pesto Sauce**  

*(Makes ~1 cup)*  

  

#### **Ingredients**  

- **2 cups** fresh basil leaves (packed, stems removed)  

- **⅓ cup** extra-virgin olive oil  

- **¼ cup** pine nuts (or walnuts/almonds)  

- **3 cloves** garlic (peeled)  

- **½ cup** freshly grated Parmesan cheese (or Pecorino Romano)  

- **¼ tsp** salt (adjust to taste)  

- **1–2 tbsp** lemon juice (optional, for brightness)  

  

#### **Instructions**  

1. **Toast the nuts** (optional but recommended): Lightly toast pine nuts in a dry pan over low heat for 2–3 minutes until fragrant (stir constantly to avoid burning).  

2. **Blend the base**: In a food processor or blender, pulse **garlic and nuts** until finely chopped.  

3. **Add basil & cheese**: Add basil, Parmesan, salt, and lemon juice. Pulse until combined.  

4. **Stream in oil**: While blending on low, slowly drizzle in olive oil until smooth (stop and scrape sides as needed).  

5. **Adjust consistency**: Add more oil for a thinner pesto or less for a thicker paste. Taste and adjust salt/lemon.  

  

---

  

### **Variations**  

- **Vegan Pesto**: Skip cheese or use **nutritional yeast** + a pinch of miso.  

- **Nut-Free**: Use **sunflower seeds** or **pumpkin seeds** instead of pine nuts.  

- **Spinach/Arugula Pesto**: Substitute half the basil for greens (less bitter than all arugula).  

- **Spicy Pesto**: Add **red pepper flakes** or a roasted jalapeño.  

  

---

  

### **How to Store Pesto**  

- **Fridge**: Keep in an airtight container with a thin layer of olive oil on top (lasts 5–7 days).  

- **Freezer**: Portion into ice cube trays, freeze, then transfer to a bag (lasts 6 months).  

  

---

  

### **How to Use Pesto**  

- Toss with **pasta** (mix with a splash of pasta water).  

- Spread on **sandwiches, pizza, or bruschetta**.  

- Stir into **soups, scrambled eggs, or roasted veggies**.  

- Marinade for **chicken, fish, or shrimp**.  

  

Enjoy! 🌿 Let me know if you’d like a different twist (e.g., sun-dried tomato pesto).
