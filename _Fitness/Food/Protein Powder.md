If your protein drink has a "pottery" taste or texture, it may be due to the type of protein powder or how it's being prepared. Here are some tips to eliminate that taste or consistency:

1. Switch Protein Powder

Look for a smoother, higher-quality protein powder. Some plant-based proteins, like pea or rice protein, can have a chalky texture. Opt for blends or whey protein isolates that are known for being smoother.


2. Mix It Properly

Use a blender instead of shaking by hand to eliminate lumps and create a smoother drink.

Add a little ice to improve the texture and flavor.


3. Enhance the Flavor

Add ingredients like banana, peanut butter, honey, or cocoa powder to mask any off-putting taste.

Use flavored milk (like almond, oat, or regular) instead of water to make the drink creamier.


4. Dilute It

Use more liquid to make the consistency less "pottery." For instance, instead of one scoop in 8 oz of water, try one scoop in 12-16 oz.


5. Experiment with Ratios

Reduce the amount of protein powder slightly if it’s making the drink too thick or chalky.


6. Soften the Powder First

Mix the powder with a small amount of liquid to create a paste before adding the rest of the liquid. This helps break down clumps and reduces chalkiness.


7. Try Adding Fat or Creamy Ingredients

Blend in Greek yogurt, avocado, or a splash of coconut cream to smooth out the texture.


8. Use a Sifter

Sift the protein powder before mixing to ensure there are no clumps.


9. Change Brands or Types

If nothing works, consider switching to a hydrolyzed protein powder, which is often finer and dissolves better.
